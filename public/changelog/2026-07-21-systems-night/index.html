The great systems night

A playable street became a persistent little world with rules, rhythms, and residents.

  • ONE SESSION
  • MANY LOOPS

Ur learned how to remember.

This pass connected actions, crafting, creatures, progression, time, sound, persistence, and official street populations into one continuous game loop.

375 tests across 99 suites at the final live verification

  1. Feel

    Actions gained a pulse

    Physical verbs became timed and cancellable. Crafting gained per-item progress, partial completion, tool animation, and honest reward timing.

  2. Keep

    The pack became a place

    Items could be used, sold, dropped, merged, collected, and restored after reload. Versioned saves came to cover the wider world state.

  3. Care

    Residents found their routines

    Piggies, Chickens, Butterflies, and six trant species gained rank-aware care, feeding, harvests, daily limits, dialogue, and persistent state.

  4. World

    Every street found its cast

    Official archived location data supplied 1,084 street rows of animals, trees, rocks, gardens, vendors, Giant shrines, and confirmed empty private locations, placed with one collision-aware planner.

Systems that joined the loop

  • Official XP levels, skill study, Dametime, food memory, buffs, tool wear, and repair.
  • Street Spirit vendors, four catalogs, drag-to-sell, drop piles, Giant donations, and eight quoin types.
  • Musicblocks, Triple Jump, camera polish, speech bubbles, Wood Trees, living trants, and Animal Kinship.
  • Exact achievement counters and three recovered gameplay-video badges.

What live QA proved

  • Completed actions rewarded; cancelled actions did not.
  • Animal care, quest progress, inventory, favors, and tool condition survived reloads.
  • Fresh games started muted and browser logs finished clean.
  • Source and groddle.localhost deployment matched.
