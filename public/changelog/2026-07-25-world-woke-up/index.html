World fixes

SMALL THINGS · LARGE RELIEF

The world stopped fighting the player.

Visual repairs use clean source art and authored geometry—not recording blur, seams, or compression.

All trees, one plant each

Thirty exact maturity cells now cover Fruit, Egg, Bean, Bubble, Gas, and Spice trees. Wood Tree keeps all six source stages.

Deposits returned

Metal, Dullite, Beryl, and Sparkly Rocks each preload five valid depletion frames, removing the blank Sparkly node.

Grounded feet

Street collision, character scaling, and vertical camera framing keep the avatar on the walkable surface instead of sinking below the UI.

HUD breathing room

Safe camera edges, larger selected controls, paged inventory rows, complete activity bubbles, and readable achievement labels rebalance the interface.

Individual road signs

Hover and click are inverse-transformed into each rotated road-name board. The post and neighboring destinations stay inert.

A calmer boot

A static loading cover now hides zeroed counters and half-built scenery until the world is genuinely ready—or explains a startup failure.

Systems

Source-authored quests

Original objectives, timers, dialogue, rewards, repeat rules, and safe return behavior now drive every reconstructed adventure.

Persistent Ur

Quest stories, tools, trees, rocks, animals, badges, skills, food memories, quoins, and dropped stacks survive travel and reload.

Smooth movement

Camera lookahead, platform-aware placement, climbing, flight inertia, slide momentum, and Triple Jump make traversal feel intentional.

  • Amp, Bumbishfish, and Lily Linguistic now walk the street as clean local Glitches, gather for Feats, and visibly react to Emotional Bear emotes.

The clean-art promise

The gameplay recordings are behavioral references. Visuals come from CC0 location data, original atlases, recovered SWFs, or crisp authored replacements.

  • No video blur copied into the game.
  • No compression seams treated as geometry.
  • No broken alpha frames promoted as assets.
