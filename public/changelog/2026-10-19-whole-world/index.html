Groddle Field Notes · Notes · OCT 19, 2026 · THE WHOLE WORLD

Ur got its depth, its names, its kitchens, and its afterlife

Every street now scrolls in true parallax under its own sky, the Magic Rock hands out real quests, and croaking sends you to Naraka.

  • PARALLAX
  • 285 RECIPES
  • NARAKA
  • MAGIC ROCK QUESTS
  • PUBLIC STREET NAMES

From a scrap of Ur toward all of it.

The first street import had quietly dropped each layer's size, colour treatment, and sky. Restoring them from the archived location XML turned flat backdrops into deep, blurred forests and Naraka's red sky with black silhouettes, using a port of the client's own colour matrix. Mirrored decorations face the right way again, and 573 streets now carry the names players actually saw. The making panel grew from 41 to 285 source recipes across 28 tools, every street spirit sells its real stock, croaking at zero energy sends you to Hell One to squish eleven bunches of grapes, and the Magic Rock now offers the original quests with their original dialogue.

7,794 street layers whose original colour treatment returned
⌁

True parallax and skies

Background and foreground canvases slide with the client's parallax rule, 1,226 streets get their own gradient sky, and blurred layers bake once at half resolution.

✶

Croak and come back

Zero energy sends you to Hell One. Squish eleven bunches of Hellish Grapes to return at ten percent energy; the dead walk greyscale, like the original.

✉

The Magic Rock's quests

One quest offer at a time, with the original conversations. Accepted quests advance from crafting, vendors, mail, harvests, verbs, visits, and your pack.

◍

A full kitchen

Saucepans, the Famous Pugilist Grill, cocktail shakers, the Awesome Pot, seasoners, and the cauldron, with source energy and mood for 193 foods and 26 drinks.

⌂

Every street spirit

Fifteen stores at source prices, and 893 streets now host the vendor the production map assigned them.

✿

A world to harvest

Barnacles, ice nubbins, jellisacs, dirt piles, peat bogs, paper trees, fireflies, salmon, sloths, and dust traps fill the streets the archive says they lived on.

❦

Plant a tree

Tend a Patch, plant a seasoned bean, pet the seedling, and watch a living tree take its place on the street.

ᴥ

Foxes and Heli Kitties

Fox preserves have their Rangers, licenses, bait, and brushable foxes; Heli Kitties hover, startle, and purr on 33 streets.

◎

Meditate and teleport

The Focusing Orb meditates, focuses, and levitates; teleport points and one-way Teleportation Scripts join the map.

⚑

Seven races and Game of Crowns

Ticket Dispensers start Space Race, Crystal Climb, The Amazing Race, Time Warp, Canyon Run, Lava Leap, and Star Sprint, rebuilt from their recordings.

⌂

Make a house a home

Decorate with 43 pieces of recovered furniture, choose wallpaper and floors, and cultivate gardens, trees, and rocks on your home street.

✦

The whole skill tree

All 105 skills are browsable; ranks now change mining, refining, gardening, and cooking, and starter skills bring their prerequisites.

✉

Neighbours and notes

Street mailboxes open your mail anywhere, notes and notice boards carry new Glitch-voiced writing, and Visiting Stones lead to six neighbours' homes.

☾

The Rooks return

A Forehorseman heralds the attack; stun the Rook with your Focusing Orb and shrine, then revive rooked critters and trees with balm.

↯

Friends stay grounded

Local companions no longer climb into the canopy, and the stand-in Glitches are now off unless you ask for them.
