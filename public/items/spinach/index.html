Spinach

 

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Hearty green spinach. It's strong to the finich.

  • Activating this will give you the High Jumper buff (You can jump really, really high).
  • You can grow this by planting Spinach Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.

Buffs

  • High Jumper: You can jump really, really high

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Produce Vendor for 3 currants.
  • Sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor for 3 currants.
  • Sold by the Uncle Friendly's Emporium for 3 currants.
  • Grown in a garden plot from Spinach Seed.

What it is for

  • An ingredient in 7 recipes: Juicy Carpaccio (Knife & Board), Scrumptious Frittata (Frying Pan), Big Salad (Knife & Board), Green Eggs (Frying Pan), Spinach Salad (Knife & Board), Death to Veg (Frying Pan), Green (Famous Pugilist Grill).
  • Feed it to a Piggy, which plops out Piggy Plop.
  • Eat it for 3 energy.
  • Activate it: Higher jumps and faster movement for 7 seconds.
