Pungent Sunrise

Plural: Pungent Sunrises

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A refreshingly pungent sunrise.

  • Drinking this will give you the Small Enlightenment buff (doubles skill learning speed).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Cocktail Crafting II.

Buffs

  • Small Enlightenment: Doubles skill learning speed
