Tincturing Kit

Plural: Tincturing Kits

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Anyone who wants to beef up the benefits of herbs or create ingredients for powerful potions would do well to avail themselves of this basic Tincturing Kit and the Tincturing skill. As the name suggests, it is perfect for making tinctures. And no use at all for anything else.

  • You need the skill Tincturing I to use this.
  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
