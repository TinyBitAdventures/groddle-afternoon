Gassifier

Plural: Gassifiers

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

An ergonomically correct gassifier. It can turn general vapour into much more exciting gases.

  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Gasmogrification first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Hardware Vendor, a Produce Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Gardening Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV and Engineering I.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Produce Vendor for 167 currants.

  • Sold by the Tool Vendor for 158 currants.
  • Sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor for 158 currants.
  • Gassifier: With a Tinkertool, from a Copper Ingot and a Plain Metal Ingot. Needs Tinkering IV.
  • Every new player starts with one.

What it is for

  • The tool for 5 recipes: Heavy Gas, Helium, Laughing Gas, White Gas, Crying Gas.
