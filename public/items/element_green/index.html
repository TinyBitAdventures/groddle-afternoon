How to get it

  • Crush Dullite: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Dullite. Makes 8. Also makes 5 Red Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Crush Beryl: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Beryl. Makes 2. Also makes 20 Red Elements and 4 Blue Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Crush Sparkly: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Sparkly. Also makes 5 Red Elements, 4 Blue Elements and 6 Shiny Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Ground from Chunk of Beryl in a Grinder.
  • Ground from Chunk of Dullite in a Grinder.
  • Ground from Chunk of Sparkly in a Grinder.
