Irrigator 9000

Plural: Irrigator 9000s

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Galvanised for your pleasure, the Irrigator 9000 offers unparalleled waterability for the constant gardener. With reinforced spout for a five-times longer life - you get what you pay for.

  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Light Green Thumb first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Light Green Thumb III and Tinkering V.
