Fiber

Plural: Fibers

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A patch of downy hair brushed from the tail of a fox, perfect for spinning into thread or stuffing into furniture for padding. Also might be perfect for gluing to your face as a moustache, but due to its origin (the underside of a fox tail), no one does.

  • You can find this by brushing Foxes.
  • You can use a Spindle to turn these into Thread.
