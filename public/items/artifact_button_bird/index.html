Button in the Shape of a Bird

Plural: Buttons in the Shape of a Bird

Never finished

In Tiny Speck’s game

This is a lost artifact, known only through ancient stories of long-deceased ancestors, and has not been discovered in Ur.

For a while, the intricately carved buttons that fastened the garments of Grendalinians were the envy of everyone. Though slightly scuffed and with a chip to the beak, this button, which once adorned the bonnet of Phoebe - a Grendalinian cocktail guru with an ever-present glint in her eye - so perfectly resembles a miniature Sandpiper that the fact it doesn’t run off onto nearby beach is a constant surprise to anyone who sees it.
