Grinder

Plural: Grinders

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A sturdy, no-nonsense grinder. Harness its awesome power to grind rocks (and those rock-like barnacles) into alchemical elements.

  • You need the skill Refining I to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Mining Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V.

Quest timers

  • The Daily Grind: Refine 200 Ore in 2 minutes. The countdown for the quest “The Daily Grind”, shown with this item’s picture: if it runs out first, the quest fails.

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Hardware Vendor for 224 currants.
  • Sold by the Alchemical Goods Vendor for 214 currants.
  • Sold by the Tool Vendor for 210 currants.
  • Sold by the Mining Vendor for 300 currants.
  • Grinder: With a Tinkertool, from a Molybdenum Ingot and a Plain Metal Ingot. Needs Tinkering V.
  • Every new player starts with one.

What it is for

  • The tool for 4 recipes: Crush Dullite, Crush Beryl, Crush Sparkly, Grind Barnacles.
  • Grind chunks of Beryl, Dullite and Sparkly into elements.
