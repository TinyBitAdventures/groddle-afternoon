Powder of Mild Fecundity

 

In the remake

500 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

The dust in this jar of reasonably fecund powder gets up the noses of piggies, butterflies and chickens (up to a point, there is only so much dust) and causes them to shed small amounts of their edibles everywhere.

  • This can be made with a Beaker.
  • You need to learn Intermediate Admixing to use a Beaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Intermediate Admixing and complete the associated quest.
