Carrot Margarita

Plural: Carrot Margaritas

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A thick, warm carrot margarita.

  • Drinking this will give you the Carrot Zing buff (a triple zing of mood and energy).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker or purchased from Helga's Liquor Store.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Cocktail Crafting I and complete the associated quest.

Buffs

  • Carrot Zing: A triple zing of mood and energy
