Hatchet

Plural: Hatchets

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A slightly dull hatchet, useful for both dead tree-clearing and showy beard-shaving.

  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Hardware Vendor, a Gardening Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Gardening Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV.
