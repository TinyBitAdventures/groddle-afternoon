Peat Bog

Plural: Peat Bogs

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Filled with a substance thicker than soil and wetter than loam, each bog gradually replenishes itself, but can only be harvested by each peat-parasite once a day (or twice, with a certain upgrade card).

  • Harvesting this is easier with Bog Specialization.
  • Digging this yields Blocks of Peat.
  • This can be dug with Shovelor Ace of Spades.
