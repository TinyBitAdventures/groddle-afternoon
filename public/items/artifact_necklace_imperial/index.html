Ouzian Necklace

Plural: Ouzian Necklaces

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A necklace of 23 swirling blue beads, traditionally given to family (and those loved like family) to celebrate the feast of the Sprinkling. Based on the necklace of ancient Mistress of the Water-filled Can, Nephelokokkygia (though not named after her, because during Sprinkling most people tended to lose the ability to pronounce her name).
