Cucumber

Plural: Cucumbers

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

An innocuous cucumber.

  • You can grow this by planting Cucumber Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Produce Vendor for 4 currants.
  • Sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor for 4 currants.
  • Sold by the Uncle Friendly's Emporium for 4 currants.
  • Grown in a garden plot from Cucumber Seed.

What it is for

  • An ingredient in 3 recipes: Common Crudites (Knife & Board), Pickle (Awesome Pot), Green (Famous Pugilist Grill).
  • Feed it to a Piggy, which plops out Piggy Plop.
  • Eat it for 4 energy.
