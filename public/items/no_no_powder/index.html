No-No Powder

Plural: Flaps of No-No Powder

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Some tempting no-no powder. You know you shouldn't, but maybe just this once …

  • This substance is known to be addictive.
  • Sprinkling this will give you the No-No Powder Rush buff (this feels really, really fantastic).
  • This can be made with a Beaker.
  • You need to learn Intermediate Admixing to use a Beaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Beaker a bit more.

Buffs

  • No-No Powder Rush: This feels really, really fantastic
  • No-No Powder Crash: Crashing! Get more powder or die!
