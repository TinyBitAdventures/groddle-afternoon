This is a lost artifact, known only through ancient stories of long-deceased ancestors, and has not been discovered in Ur.

Seeking ways to please their particular, peculiar, pristinely manicured protector, the Tii-ites were always seeking the greatest possible way of pleasing the giant with the perfectly smooth bald head. Dedicated supplicant Threepwood declared his decision to create a giant-sized head-polishing cloth. However, since it took him 47 years to collect enough batterfly silk for this small (perfect) corner, the great supplication never came to pass.
