Fruit Changing Machine

Plural: Fruit Changing Machines

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A triple-carbine fruit-changing machine. It will transmogrify cherries into other fruit. This can come in surprisingly handy.

  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Fruit Changing first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Gardening Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV and Engineering I.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Kitchen Tools Vendor for 220 currants.

  • Sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor for 210 currants.
  • Fruit Changing Machine: With a Tinkertool, from a Tin Ingot and a Copper Ingot. Needs Tinkering IV.
  • Every new player starts with one.

What it is for

  • The tool for 11 recipes: Plum, Bunch of Grapes, Orange, Apple, Lemon, Pineapple, Banana, Whortleberry, Cloudberry, Strawberry, Mangosteen.
