Green Sno Cone

Plural: Green Sno Cones

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A bright green blend of ice, hooch and elements make a drink the color of days when spring is born, or nights when the northern lights perform. Packed with a modest hit of iMG, winter cannot fade it. Mainly because it's 40% elemental additives and preservatives.

  • You must be at least level 4 to eat this Sno Cone.
  • This can be made with a Blender or purchased from a Sno Cone Vendor.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
