Spice Mill

Plural: Spice Mills

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A basic spice mill for converting basic allspice into other more exciting spices.

  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Spice Milling first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Gardening Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV and Engineering I.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Kitchen Tools Vendor for 165 currants.
  • Sold by the Tool Vendor for 158 currants.
  • Sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor for 158 currants.
  • Spice Mill: With a Tinkertool, from 2 Tin Ingots. Needs Tinkering IV.
  • Every new player starts with one.

What it is for

  • The tool for 16 recipes: Pinch of Salt, Curry, Hot Pepper, Garlic, Ginger, Black Pepper, Mustard, Cumin, Cinnamon, Licorice, Nutmeg, Saffron, Turmeric, Cardamom, Camphor, Old(er) Spice.
