Face Smelter

Plural: Face Smelters

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A powerful brew with a scent acrid enough to perm your nostril hairs.

  • Drinking this will give you the Smelt It, Dealt It buff (smelt metal rocks without using energy).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker or purchased from a Mining Vendor or a Meal Vendor.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Cocktail Crafting II.

Buffs

  • Smelt It, Dealt It: Smelt metal rocks without using energy
