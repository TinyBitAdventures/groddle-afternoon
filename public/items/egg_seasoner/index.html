Egg Seasoner

Plural: Egg Seasoners

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

An Egg Seasoner is an important tool for seasoning and incubating eggs that hatch into all kinds of exciting animals.

  • You need the skill Animal Husbandry to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from an Animal Goods Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Engineering I.
