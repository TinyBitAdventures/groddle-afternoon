Elixir of Avarice

Plural: Elixirs of Avarice

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A natural blend that increases one's appetite for Quoins. Dosage: effective up to one potion per day.

  • The recipe for this will become available after you earn the achievement 'Numismatizer, Leprechaun Class'.
  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Cauldron a bit more.
