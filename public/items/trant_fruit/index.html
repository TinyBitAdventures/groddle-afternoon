Fruit Tree

Plural: Fruit Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The glorious Fruit Tree. Thick with tasty cherries ripe to be converted into other delicious fruits, these venerable plants may not be chatty, but they're indispensable for fine edibles and quaffables of all sorts.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting a Fruit Tree Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Cherries which can be converted into other kinds of fruit using the Fruit Changing Machine.
