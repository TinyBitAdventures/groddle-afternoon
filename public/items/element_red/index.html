How to get it

  • Crush Dullite: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Dullite. Makes 5. Also makes 8 Green Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Crush Beryl: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Beryl. Makes 20. Also makes 2 Green Elements and 4 Blue Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Crush Sparkly: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Sparkly. Makes 5. Also makes a Green Element, 4 Blue Elements and 6 Shiny Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Ground from Chunk of Beryl in a Grinder.
  • Ground from Chunk of Dullite in a Grinder.
  • Ground from Chunk of Sparkly in a Grinder.
