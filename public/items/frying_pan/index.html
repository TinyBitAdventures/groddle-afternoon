Frying Pan

Plural: Frying Pans

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A useful pan for frying things in.

  • You need the skill Cheffery I to use this.
  • Additional recipes will become available if you use the Frying Pan more.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Kitchen Tools Vendor for 138 currants.

  • Frying Pan: With a Tinkertool, from a Plain Metal Ingot and a Tin Ingot. Needs Tinkering IV.
  • Every new player starts with one.
