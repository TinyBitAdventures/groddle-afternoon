Ice Nubbin

Plural: Ice Nubbins

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A dribble of purest water, frozen in time, space, and temperature (mainly temperature, actually: it's very cold) into a nubbin of purest ice. Attacking the nubbin with a sharp enough scraper (NB: not as painful as it sounds) will result in handfuls of fresh cuboid ice lumps.

  • Ice Nubbins can be found in the region of Nottis.
  • Scraping this yields Ice.
