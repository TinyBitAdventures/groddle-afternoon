Plural: Parsnips

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A pungent parsnip.

  • You can grow this by planting Parsnip Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, Uncle Friendly's Emporium or a Gardening Tools Vendor.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Produce Vendor for 7 currants.
  • Sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor for 6 currants.
  • Sold by the Uncle Friendly's Emporium for 7 currants.
  • Grown in a garden plot from Parsnip Seed.

What it is for

  • An ingredient in 4 recipes: Choice Crudites (Knife & Board), Mega Healthy Veggie Juice (Blender), Face Smelter (Cocktail Shaker), Vegmageddon (Famous Pugilist Grill).
  • Feed it to a Piggy, which plops out Piggy Plop.
  • Eat it for 6 energy.
