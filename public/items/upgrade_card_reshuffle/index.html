If you don't like the hand fate has dealt you, fight for a new one. And so it is here, though for "fight" read "swap this card for", for "hand" read "Upgrade Cards". And apparently for "that first sentence" read "something entirely different with lots of different words in". Basically, you can swap this card for a free reshuffle of Upgrade Cards. Probably should have said that in the first place.
