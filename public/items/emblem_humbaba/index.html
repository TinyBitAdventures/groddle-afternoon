Emblem of Humbaba

Plural: Emblems of Humbaba

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Behold a dazzling Emblem of Humbaba. Keep it safe, and someday it may entitle you to wield special powers and whatnot.

  • This can be obtained by earning favor with Humbaba. The quickest way to earn favor is by donating to Shrines.
  • This item belongs to a collection which grants you the achievement Emblems Trophy
