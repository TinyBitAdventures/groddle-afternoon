Wood Tree

Plural: Wood Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

While all trees are woody, the Wood Tree is woodier than most. Tending to your wood is a rewarding solitary pursuit, but gathering friends to engorge your wood before all wielding your choppers simultaneously is both mutually satisfying, and sure to result in the injection of firm, tasty planks into your bag.

  • The more mature the Wood Tree, the more Planks can be harvested from it!
  • Wood Tree grows quicker if petted by many people!
  • You can grow this by planting a Wood Tree Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Planks.
