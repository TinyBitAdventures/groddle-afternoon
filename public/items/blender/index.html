Blender

Plural: Blenders

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A Blender is very good for blending juices, smoothies and other concoctions. This seems a generally sturdy model.

  • You can't use a Blender yet: you need to learn Blending I first.
  • Additional recipes will become available if you use the Blender more.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV and Engineering I.

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Sold by the Kitchen Tools Vendor for 165 currants.
  • Sold by the Uncle Friendly's Emporium for 165 currants.
  • Blender: With a Tinkertool, from a Copper Ingot and 3 Clumps of Jellisac. Needs Tinkering IV.
  • Every new player starts with one.

What it is for

  • The tool for 16 recipes: Fruity Juice, Lemon Juice, Orange Juice, Mega Healthy Veggie Juice, Cloud 11 Smoothie, Exotic Juice, Too-berry Shake, Savory Smoothie, Earthshaker, Bubble Tea, Blue Sno Cone, Green Sno Cone, Orange Sno Cone, Red Sno Cone, Purple Sno Cone, Rainbo Sno Cone.
