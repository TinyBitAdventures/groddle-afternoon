Bean Seasoner

Plural: Bean Seasoners

In the remake

175 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

A Bean Seasoner seasons beans. You probably guessed that already.

  • You need the skill Botany to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Gardening Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Gardening Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Engineering I.
