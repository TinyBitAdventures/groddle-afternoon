Tinkertool

Plural: Tinkertools

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Are you the DIY type who likes to repair your own tools? The nitpicky type who likes to craft their own gear from scratch? Then a Tinkertool is a very handy thing for you to have.

  • You need the skill Tinkering I to use this to repair tools.
  • You need the skill Tinkering IV to craft tools.
  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Mining Vendor.
