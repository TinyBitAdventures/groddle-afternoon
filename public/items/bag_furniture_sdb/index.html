Storage Display Box

Plural: Storage Display Boxes

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

For the pragmatic showoff in everyone, a box with hidden depths. Not only can you display your prized possessions, but you can tuck a virtually infinite amount of the exact same prized possession behind it, for safekeeping.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Engineering I and Furnituremaking II.
