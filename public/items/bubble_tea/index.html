Bubble Tea

Plural: Bubble Teas

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A super-frothy bubble tea.

  • Drinking this will give you the Bubble-Enhanced Meditation buff (double reward when meditating).
  • This can be made with a Blender.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Blending II.

Buffs

  • Bubble-Enhanced Meditation: Double reward when meditating
