Dung-Kicker Drops

 

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

One squirt of Dung-Kicker Drops spreads fertilizer over all fertilizable plots in a garden (no extra guano necessary), leaving you free to enjoy more pleasant-scented parts of the gardening experience.

  • You will not be able to use this until you have the Master Gardener skill.
  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Potionmaking III.
