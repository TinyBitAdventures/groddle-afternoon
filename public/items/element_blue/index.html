How to get it

  • Crush Beryl: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Beryl. Makes 4. Also makes 20 Red Elements and 2 Green Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Crush Sparkly: With a Grinder, from a Chunk of Sparkly. Makes 4. Also makes 5 Red Elements, a Green Element and 6 Shiny Elements. Needs Refining I.
  • Ground from Chunk of Beryl in a Grinder.
