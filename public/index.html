A fan remake of Glitch (2009 to 2012)

Groddle Afternoon

Tiny Speck's Glitch was a gentle, funny world made of imagination: you squeezed chickens, petted piggies, talked to trees and found quoins floating over the streets of Ur. It closed in December 2012. Tiny Speck gave its art, music and code to the public domain, and Groddle Afternoon puts them back together in your browser.

Play in your browser See what's been restored

New to Ur? Start fresh on Gentle Island, the original tutorial, map reveal and all. Free. Play solo with no account, saved in this browser, or sign in to play multiplayer with other Glitches. Plays on a computer with a keyboard.

Things to do in Ur

  • Grow, harvest and cook

    Plant gardens, water and pet trees, milk butterflies and cook what you grow. Poison a tree and it dies; clear it, and plant a new one.

  • Mine, make and build

    Swing a pick, run Woodworkers and Blockmakers, and help build 689 new streets through the original street projects.

  • Quests and secret rooms

    Story quests from the Magic Rock, all 22 private quest rooms, pilgrimages and the secret rooms of Jethimadh Tower.

  • Fight off the Rook

    When the Rook attacks, prime the shrines, stun it with a Focusing Orb and nurse the street back to life, alone or with everyone on the street.

  • Races and Paradise

    Race rival Glitches or other players in Grab 'Em Good, wrangle piggies in the Great Hog Haul, and fly through Cloud Rings and Radial Heights.

  • A home of your own

    Paper your rooms wall by wall with Tiny Speck's own 109 wallpapers, floors and ceilings, decorate, and raise a tower.

  • Explore Ur

    Jump into the Great Hole to Ix, find the Enchanted Forest hollows, and help the Juju Bandits that come out of the Savanna.

  • Skills and imagination

    Learn 99 skills and spend imagination on 247 of the original upgrade cards, from bigger batches to longer levitation.

Drawn from the original files

Every picture in Groddle Afternoon is Tiny Speck's own art. Glitch was drawn in vectors, so the trees, quoins, Glitches and street scenery are rendered again from their original Flash files: sharp on today's screens, and still exactly the shapes and colours they always were.

Follow the development

Every rule comes from Tiny Speck's own server code where it survives. Where it doesn't, the quest rooms and streets are rebuilt from players' recordings, the community wiki and archived pages, and each of those rebuilds says so in the restoration report.

Solo, or together

Glitch was a world you shared, and now you can choose. Solo, Ur is yours alone: races, Game of Crowns, parties and street projects come with rival and helper Glitches, and your game saves in this browser. Make a free character with just an email and a password, and in multiplayer you share the street with other players: chat, form a party, race, trade and fight off the Rook together, and keep a buddy list. Global chat, groups and the auction house aren't back yet, and the restoration report lists each one plainly.
