Groddle Field Notes · Restoration report

How much of Glitch is back?

Glitch closed in December 2012. Tiny Speck released its art, music and server code into the public domain, and almost everything else was lost: the maps of private quest rooms, most of the sound library, the multiplayer world itself. Groddle Afternoon is a fresh web build that puts the pieces back together, rule by rule from the original code, and room by room from players' recordings where nothing else survives.

      Quest rooms, rebuilt

      Many of Glitch's best quests happened in private rooms built just for you. Only a handful survive in the archive, so most are reconstructed from the quest's code (its timers, flags, dialogue and rewards) and recordings players and Tiny Speck's own level designer left behind.
