How do I get Wine of the Dead?

Hell's Bartender in the Hell Bar swaps one Diabolical Drink Ticket for one Wine of the Dead, and you get a ticket when someone mourns or celebrates your grave while you are dead.

Step by step

  1. Croak. When your energy runs out you die, leave a Graveside Marker where you fell, and wake in Hell One in Naraka.
  2. Wait to be recognised. While you are still dead, a companion may walk past your grave and Mourn or Celebrate it, 20 to 50 seconds after you croak. Until you finish Beelzebooze this happens every time you croak; after that, one croak in 3. Online, another player who finds your grave can do it too.
  3. Get a ticket. The first time, you are offered It's Better in a Hell Hole: jump down the Hell Hole. Finishing it offers Beelzebooze, which gives you your first Diabolical Drink Ticket. After that, each recognition gives you a ticket.
  4. Trade it. Go to Panka Surazu at the far end of Naraka, step into the Hell Bar, and choose Buy wine at Hell's Bartender. One ticket buys one bottle.

Drinking it

  • In Naraka, drinking it takes away Pooped.
  • Drinking it among the living kills you.
  • It does not bring you back to life: squish 11 bunches of Hellish Grapes for that.
  • Beelzebooze ends when you drink your first bottle, for 250 iMG and 150 currants.
  • On your home street, pouring it on something you cultivated wears it out.
