How do I add a floor to my house, and can I move the Doors?

Set a Door down on the top floor of your house: that starts the Build a New Floor project, and 100 Planks finish it.

Adding a floor

  1. Get a Door. No vendor sells one: make it with a Construction Tool from 8 Wood Posts, 16 Boards and 20 Snails. The recipe needs Engineering and Furnituremaking II.
  2. In your house, set the Door down on your top floor. That starts the Build a New Floor project.
  3. Bring 100 Planks to the project.
  4. When it is done, the Door is the way up, and a Door down stands in the same place on the new floor. A house can have up to 3 floors.

Can I move a Door?

  • Yes, sideways: drag a placed Door left or right along its own floor, in Decoration Mode or by dragging it in your house. It stays inside the walls and never goes up, down or to another floor.
  • Each Door of a pair keeps its own spot, and going through one lands you at the other, wherever it stands.
  • You can only pick a Door back up before its project starts. After that, and on every finished floor, it slides but never comes up.

Making the house wider

Expanding works on every floor at once, so each new wall segment takes one Wall Segment per floor. On two floors it also takes an Urth Block; on three floors, 3 of them. Every fourth segment on more than one floor also takes Girders.
