Where are the community gardens, and how do I use one?

Community gardens are public crop and herb beds on a few streets, and anyone can plant, water and pick there.

Where they are

The Streets with a Community Garden list shows every street with one, by region, such as Community Gardens and Kipacre Greens.

On the three Herb Gardens streets every bed is an herb bed.

How to grow something

  1. Bring seeds. Crop seeds are sold by the Gardening Tools Vendor and the Gardening Goods Vendor. Herb seeds come from shucking herbs, or now and then from a Dust Trap.
  2. Plant in a clean plot. Crop seeds only go in a crop bed, and herb seeds only in an herb bed.
  3. Water it with a Watering Can. Seeds only grow while the plot is wet. One watering lasts 72 minutes, and a dry plot stops growing until you water it again.
  4. Pick the crop when it is ripe. Without Croppery I (or Herbalism I in an herb bed) you get half the crop.
  5. Clear the picked plot with a Hoe, and it is clean for the next seeds.

Playing online

  • Everyone shares the same plots in the public gardens, except the tutorial bed on Doonboggle Trail.
  • When a crop ripens, the player who planted it has 60 seconds to pick it before anyone else can.
