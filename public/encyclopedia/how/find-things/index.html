How do I find a particular resource or animal?

Search for it by name: the Streets with… list for it comes first, then every street whose page names it.

Step by step

  1. Type its name in the Encyclopedia search, for example salmon or metal rock. Plurals work, and one typo is fine.
  2. Open its Streets with… list, such as the salmon list or the Metal Rock list: every street that has it, by region, with how many when a street has more than one.
  3. Pick a street near you. Each street page shows what lives and grows there, and the other lists it is on.

Other ways in

  • Browse every Streets with… list: gardens, shrines, vendors, subway stations, rocks, trees, animals and more.
  • An animal or item page says where to find it and what gives it, such as Salmon and Chunk of Metal Rock.
  • The subway map shows the street each station comes up on.
