How do I get visitors to my home street?

Choose Get visitors at any Visiting Stone, and players who press Visit on a stone can be sent to your home street.

Before you can opt in

  • Reach level 4.
  • Have your home street.
  • Cultivate 3 items on your home street.
  • Unbox your Butler. Its Special One-Time Delivery turns up on your home street once you have a home and reach level 3.

What counts as cultivated

Each thing you set down in Cultivate mode on your home street counts as one: a Patch, a rock, a garden, a Dirt Pile, a Firefly Hive and the rest. Things in your back yard do not count.

Open Cultivate mode with the Cultivate button on your home street, or press Shift+C. Each thing costs iMG, and the price grows with each one you add.

How players get visited

  1. Walk up to a Visiting Stone and choose Get visitors. Stop random visitors opts you out again.
  2. Anyone who chooses Visit at a stone goes to the home street of a random player who opted in.
  3. Friends can also come any time with Visit Home Street, from your name or their buddy list, and a house key lets them inside.

Playing solo

With no other players, Get visitors needs level 4, a home and your Butler. A neighbour then drops by your home street each new game day and leaves a gift with your Butler.
