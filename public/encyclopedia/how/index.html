How do I…

Short answers to questions players have asked, from the game as it is today. Each guide links the pages, lists and streets it relies on.

  • Where are the community gardens, and how do I use one? Community gardens are public crop and herb beds on a few streets, and anyone can plant, water and pick there.
  • How do I get visitors to my home street? Choose Get visitors at any Visiting Stone, and players who press Visit on a stone can be sent to your home street.
  • How do I get Wine of the Dead? Hell's Bartender in the Hell Bar swaps one Diabolical Drink Ticket for one Wine of the Dead, and you get a ticket when someone mourns or celebrates your grave while you are dead.
  • How do I add a floor to my house, and can I move the Doors? Set a Door down on the top floor of your house: that starts the Build a New Floor project, and 100 Planks finish it.
  • Why does learning a skill take so long? Once you know more skills than your brain capacity, which starts at 20, each skill past it makes the next one take 5% longer, and that adds up.
  • How do I find a particular resource or animal? Search for it by name: the Streets with… list for it comes first, then every street whose page names it.
