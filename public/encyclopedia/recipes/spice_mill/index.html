Spice Mill

Transmogrifier · 16 recipes

In the remake

The tool

Anyone can use it. Without Spice Milling each make takes twice as long, costs a quarter more energy and fails 20% of the time.

Where to buy it

  • Kitchen Tools Vendor, 165 currants
