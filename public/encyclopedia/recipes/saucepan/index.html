Cooking tool · 16 recipes

In the remake

The tool

You need Saucery I to use it at all; some recipes need more skills, as shown on each.

  • Saucepan, the tool

Where to buy it

  • Kitchen Tools Vendor, 220 currants

What the Saucepan makes

16 recipes, 16 different things to make, with Tiny Speck’s names and numbers.

The energy and time below are per item, before skills: from level II of the tool’s skill a make takes half the time and less energy.
