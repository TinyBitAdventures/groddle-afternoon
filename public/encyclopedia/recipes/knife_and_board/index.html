Knife & Board

Cooking tool · 28 recipes

In the remake

The tool

You need EZ Cooking I to use it at all; some recipes need more skills, as shown on each.

  • Knife & Board, the tool

Where to buy it

  • Uncle Friendly, 83 currants
  • Kitchen Tools Vendor, 83 currants

What the Knife & Board makes

28 recipes, 28 different things to make, with Tiny Speck’s names and numbers.

The energy and time below are per item, before skills: from level II of the tool’s skill a make takes half the time and less energy.
