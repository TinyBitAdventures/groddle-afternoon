Tinkertool

Crafting tool · 35 recipes

In the remake

The tool

You need Tinkering I to use it at all; some recipes need more skills, as shown on each.

  • Tinkertool, the tool

Where to buy it

  • Tool Vendor, 525 currants
  • Hardware Vendor, 560 currants
  • Mining Vendor, 750 currants

What the Tinkertool makes

35 recipes, 35 different things to make, with Tiny Speck’s names and numbers.

The energy and time below are per item, before skills.
