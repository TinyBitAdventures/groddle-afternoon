Frying Pan

Cooking tool · 31 recipes

In the remake

The tool

You need Cheffery I to use it at all; some recipes need more skills, as shown on each.

  • Frying Pan, the tool

Where to buy it

  • Uncle Friendly, 138 currants
  • Kitchen Tools Vendor, 138 currants

What the Frying Pan makes

31 recipes, 31 different things to make, with Tiny Speck’s names and numbers.

The energy and time below are per item, before skills: from level II of the tool’s skill a make takes half the time and less energy.
