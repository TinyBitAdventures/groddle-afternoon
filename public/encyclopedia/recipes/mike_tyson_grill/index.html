Cooking tool · 18 recipes

The tool

You need Grilling I to use it at all; some recipes need more skills, as shown on each.

  • Famous Pugilist Grill, the tool

Where to buy it

  • Kitchen Tools Vendor, 193 currants

18 recipes, 18 different things to make, with Tiny Speck’s names and numbers.

The energy and time below are per item, before skills: from level II of the tool’s skill a make takes half the time and less energy.
