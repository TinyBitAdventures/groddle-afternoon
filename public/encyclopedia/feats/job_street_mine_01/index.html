Bubbles (Pollokoo)

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. Plain Bubble: 2

    • ×2 Plain Bubble, 100 in the original

  2. Hard Bubble: 17

    • ×17 Hard Bubble, 850 in the original

  3. Finetune 69 times

    • Bubble Tuner, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Bubble Tuning, 1 tool wear a go, 3,450 in the original, after the earlier steps

  4. Blue Bubble: 104

    • ×104 Blue Bubble, 5,175 in the original

  5. Tiny Bubble: 25

    • ×25 Tiny Bubble, 1,215 in the original

  6. Contemplate 86 times

    • Focusing Orb, tool

    3 energy a go, needs Meditative Arts I, 1 tool wear a go, 4,275 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

18,750 iMG, 31,250 mood, 3,125 favor with Grendaline

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

375 iMG, 625 mood, 63 favor with Grendaline

Part of the project that builds 5 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
