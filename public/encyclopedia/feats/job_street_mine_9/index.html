Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

24,875 iMG, 40,280 mood, 4,000 favor with Pot

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

498 iMG, 806 mood, 80 favor with Pot

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 8 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Callopee

Onoma Peas 1 of 2, PokiPok Press 1 of 2, Limmu Later 2 of 2, Kinche Klap 2 of 2

Pollokoo

Quluuwaa Luwa 2 of 2, Hamli Egza 1 of 2, Kotteletti Kota 1 of 2, Intate Treats 2 of 2
