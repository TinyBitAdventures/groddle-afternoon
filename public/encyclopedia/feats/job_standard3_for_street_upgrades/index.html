Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

4,000 iMG, 1,000 mood, 1,000 energy

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

80 iMG, 20 mood, 20 energy

For the top contributors

100 iMG
