Spice 2

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. Garlic: 30

    • ×30 Garlic, 1,500 in the original

  2. Cumin: 19

    • ×19 Cumin, 901 in the original

  3. Black Pepper: 21

    • ×21 Black Pepper, 1,001 in the original

  4. Mince 36 times

    • Knife & Board, tool

    4 energy a go, needs EZ Cooking II, 1 tool wear a go, 1,789 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Licorice: 15

    • ×15 Licorice, 747 in the original

  6. Saffron: 12

    • ×12 Saffron, 597 in the original

  7. Turmeric: 14

    4 energy a go, needs Spice Milling, 1 tool wear a go, 1,911 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

19,900 iMG, 32,225 mood, 3,200 favor with Pot

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

398 iMG, 645 mood, 64 favor with Pot

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 11 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
