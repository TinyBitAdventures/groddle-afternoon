The Call to Feast

Feat · part of an epic

Not yet

In Tiny Speck’s game

A Feat was a goal the whole world worked toward together, for one day. Each time you added to it, the game told you so:

Invitation, ho! Your inviting of this fine friend has been counted as a contribution towards recreating the Feat of "The Call to Feast". There are so many friends left to be invited in order to complete the feat.

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The goals did not survive: the remake sets its own.
