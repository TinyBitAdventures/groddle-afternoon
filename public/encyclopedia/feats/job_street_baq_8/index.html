Spice 1

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. Pinch of Salt: 40

    • ×40 Pinch of Salt, 2,000 in the original

  2. Hot Pepper: 18

    • ×18 Hot Pepper, 877 in the original

  3. Cardamom: 13

    • ×13 Cardamom, 625 in the original

  4. Grind 36 times

    • Spice Mill, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Spice Milling, 1 tool wear a go, 1,789 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Curry: 17

    • ×17 Curry, 803 in the original

  6. Old(er) Spice: 16

    • ×16 Old(er) Spice, 777 in the original

  7. Mustard: 27

    • ×27 Mustard, 1,333 in the original

  8. Cut 38 times

    • Scraper, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Botany, 1 tool wear a go, 1,881 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

20,000 iMG, 31,500 mood, 3,100 favor with Pot

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

400 iMG, 630 mood, 62 favor with Pot

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 11 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Baqala

Parsin's Trail W, Empty Via 5, Mart's Way 3, Range Rd 7

Zhambu

Rube's Way 4, Rube's Way 3, Swain Way 4

Choru

Redhorn Rd E, Hayseed Rd 4

Xalanga

Ramble Way 3, Ardent Ave 3
