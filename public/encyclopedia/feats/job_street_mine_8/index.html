Spice 1 (Pollokoo)

Street project phase

In the remake

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  3. Cardamom: 28

    • ×28 Cardamom, 1,375 in the original

    4 energy a go, needs Spice Milling, 1 tool wear a go, 5,367 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Curry: 35

    • ×35 Curry, 1,709 in the original

  6. Old(er) Spice: 34

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

25,000 iMG, 39,375 mood, 3,875 favor with Pot

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

500 iMG, 788 mood, 78 favor with Pot

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 7 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Callopee

Bayadd Yadding 1 of 2, Fosolia Flaps 2 of 2, Kohanger Hang 2 of 2, Vekke Vets 1 of 2

Pollokoo

Sambossa Bossa 2 of 2, Derjo Jo 2 of 2, Masod Match 1 of 2
