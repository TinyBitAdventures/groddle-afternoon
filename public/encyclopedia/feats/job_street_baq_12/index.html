Currants

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

    200 currants, 10,000 in the original

  2. Currants: 400

    400 currants, 20,000 in the original

  3. Currants: 800

    800 currants, 40,000 in the original

  5. Currants: 445

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

50,000 iMG, 100,000 mood, 5,000 favor with Pot

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

1,000 iMG, 2,000 mood, 100 favor with Pot

For the top contributors

250 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 15 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
