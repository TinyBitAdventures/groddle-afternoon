Sparkla's Shine on the Giants

Feat · part of an epic

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A Feat was a goal the whole world worked toward together, for one day. Each time you added to it, the game told you so:

Those sparkly rocks you just donated? They have advanced all Glitchdom toward accomplishment of the Feat "Sparkla's Shine on the Giants". If 25 more chunks of sparkly ore are donated to shrines, the Feat will have met its goal.

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The goals did not survive: the remake sets its own.

On your own

  • Goal: 25 chunks of Sparkly. Donate chunks of Sparkly at any shrine.
  • Every solo Feat is open at once in a solo game. Reach the goal and you get a Small Petrified Rock.
  • A finished Feat opens again one game week later (8 game days, 32 real hours), for another rock.
  • Small Petrified Rock, for each finished run

With other players

  • One Feat runs for everyone playing for one real day, and the 11 Feats take turns, so this one comes back every 11 days.
  • Goals: a minimum of 75 chunks of Sparkly, a bonus goal of 150 and a supermegabonus goal of 225, counted from everyone’s contributions. A Feat that misses its minimum goal fails.
  • When it meets its minimum, everyone who helped gets the rewards below, up to 3 times as much as more of the goals are met, and a Small Petrified Rock. The 13 biggest contributors also get a Large Petrified Rock, places 14 to 26 a Medium one, and places 27 to 30 earn The Cigar.

272 iMG, 170 currants, 102 energy, 34 favor with every Giant

  • Small Petrified Rock, everyone who helped
  • Medium Petrified Rock, places 14 to 26
  • Large Petrified Rock, places 1 to 13

Epic

One of 6 Feats in the same epic. Contribute to every one of them to complete the epic.

  • Hispikulus's Amazing Ramble
  • Deaths & Reminiscences
  • The Call to Feast
  • Questing and Contesting
  • The Incantations of Absurdity
