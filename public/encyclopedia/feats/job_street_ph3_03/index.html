Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

15,000 iMG, 15,000 mood, 15,000 energy, 300 favor with every Giant

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

300 iMG, 300 mood, 300 energy, 6 favor with every Giant

For the top contributors

50 iMG, 20 mood, 10 energy

Achievements

For working on many different project phases.

  • Interested Party
  • Team Player
  • Project Enthusiast
  • Up 'n' Comer
  • Deeply Concerned Citizen

In the remake

In the remake, solo and online. Online, and on your own far from the start, every amount is a fiftieth of the original’s (at least 1), because the original totals were sized for a whole server of players. On your own, a street nearer the start asks less, down to 1/500 next to Hechey Track, and its work needs lower skills or none. One of 4 phases with this name, each asking for different things.
