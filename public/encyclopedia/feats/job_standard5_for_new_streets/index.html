In the remake

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

8,000 iMG, 20,000 currants, 10,000 mood, 10,000 energy

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

160 iMG, 400 currants, 200 mood, 200 energy

For the top contributors

300 iMG, 50 mood

And a roll for one of these:

  • A Piece of Street Creator Dirt Trophy
  • A Piece of Street Creator Wood Trophy
  • A Piece of Street Creator Rock Trophy
  • A Piece of Street Creator Earth Trophy

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 5 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
