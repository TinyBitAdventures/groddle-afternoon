Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

10,000 iMG, 10,000 currants, 10,000 mood, 5,000 energy, 100 favor with every Giant

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

200 iMG, 200 currants, 200 mood, 100 energy, 2 favor with every Giant

For the top contributors

200 iMG, 50 mood, 25 energy

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 14 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Groddle Forest

Addingfoot Trip, Hellenturret Pass, Sapslow Tweeds, Ferncaster End, Follybottom Stritt

Groddle Heights

Larceny Ladder, Highland Fling, Bishop's Arch, Ledge Narrows, Coldham Shift

Groddle Meadow

Ramsley Sway, Tillymurry Hurry

Shimla Mirch

Tlemcen 1 of 4, Nilpsama 2 of 2
