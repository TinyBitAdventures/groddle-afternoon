12,000 iMG, 10,000 currants, 10,000 mood, 5,000 energy, 100 favor with every Giant

240 iMG, 200 currants, 200 mood, 100 energy, 2 favor with every Giant

For the top contributors

100 iMG, 50 mood, 25 energy

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 21 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Groddle Forest

Grand Mile, Middle Arbor, Sylvan Grove, The Landing, Fairgower Lane, Evanfell Craine, Grimssea Bottom

Groddle Meadow

Leftmost Graze, Nightshade Woods, Footlands Heath, Bailestorth Shin, Buckward Vale, Wickdoon Mood, Broxhurst Green

Groddle Heights

Clamber Crag, Alpine Vista, Tallish Crest, Uplands Verge, Yeoman's Bluff

Shimla Mirch

Wettish Walk, Leeteg 2 of 4

In the remake

In the remake, solo and online. Online, and on your own far from the start, every amount is a fiftieth of the original’s (at least 1), because the original totals were sized for a whole server of players. On your own, a street nearer the start asks less, down to 1/500 next to Hechey Track, and its work needs lower skills or none.
