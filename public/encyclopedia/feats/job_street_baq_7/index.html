Harvest

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. Meat: 19

    • ×19 Meat, 901 in the original

  2. Grain: 25

    • ×25 Grain, 1,221 in the original

  3. Cherry: 39

    • ×39 Cherry, 1,935 in the original

  4. Squish 36 times

    • Cocktail Shaker, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Cocktail Crafting II, 1 tool wear a go, 1,789 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Egg: 27

    • ×27 Egg, 1,301 in the original

  6. Bean: 29

    • ×29 Bean, 1,444 in the original

  7. Butterfly Milk: 25

    4 energy a go, needs Light Green Thumb III, 1 tool wear a go, 1,445 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

18,888 iMG, 28,500 mood, 2,811 favor with Spriggan

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

378 iMG, 570 mood, 56 favor with Spriggan

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 15 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
