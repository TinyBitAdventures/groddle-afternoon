8,000 iMG, 8,000 mood, 8,000 energy, 175 favor with every Giant

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

160 iMG, 160 mood, 160 energy, 4 favor with every Giant

80 iMG, 30 mood

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 9 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Groddle Heights

Smallend High, Beesfast Heave, Pinhigh Prose

Groddle Meadow

Rookthills Lay, Bonebottle Sands

In the remake

In the remake, solo and online. Online, and on your own far from the start, every amount is a fiftieth of the original’s (at least 1), because the original totals were sized for a whole server of players. On your own, a street nearer the start asks less, down to 1/500 next to Hechey Track, and its work needs lower skills or none.
