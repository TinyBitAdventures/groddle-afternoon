Gas

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. General Vapour: 10

    • ×10 General Vapour, 500 in the original

  2. Heavy Gas: 13

    • ×13 Heavy Gas, 622 in the original

  3. Crying Gas: 15

    • ×15 Crying Gas, 701 in the original

  4. Gassify 37 times

    • Gassifier, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Gasmogrification, 1 tool wear a go, 1,825 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Helium: 10

    • ×10 Helium, 455 in the original

  6. Laughing Gas: 16

    • ×16 Laughing Gas, 800 in the original

  7. White Gas: 10

    • ×10 White Gas, 455 in the original

  8. Pop 30 times

    • Fancy Pick, tool

    3 energy a go, needs Mining IV, 1 tool wear a go, 1,475 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

25,000 iMG, 40,000 mood, 4,000 favor with Grendaline

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

500 iMG, 800 mood, 80 favor with Grendaline

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 14 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Choru

Bumpkin Tr E, Chester Way 1C, Naif Lane SW, Chester Way 1D

Xalanga

Distant Drag C, Ramble Way 1, Fervor Tack E, Distant Drag F

Baqala

Range Rd 5, Parsin's Trail SE, Contour Rd E

Zhambu

Swain Way 3, Yokel Yarn 3, Rube's Way 6
