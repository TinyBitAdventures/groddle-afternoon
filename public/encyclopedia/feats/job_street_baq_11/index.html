Compounds 2

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. Green Element: 1,300

    • ×1,300 Green Element, 65,000 in the original

  2. Ixite: 35

    • ×35 Ixite, 1,711 in the original

  3. Tiite: 19

    • ×19 Tiite, 921 in the original

  4. Grind 33 times

    • Grinder, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Refining II, 1 tool wear a go, 1,623 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Blue Element: 1,100

    • ×1,100 Blue Element, 55,000 in the original

  6. Abbasidose: 17

    • ×17 Abbasidose, 819 in the original

  7. Mabon: 11

    • ×11 Mabon, 515 in the original

  8. Cook 27 times

    • Awesome Pot, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Element Handling, 1 tool wear a go, 1,331 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

24,000 iMG, 38,750 mood, 3,875 favor with Tii

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

480 iMG, 775 mood, 78 favor with Tii

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 13 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
