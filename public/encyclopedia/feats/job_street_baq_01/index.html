Bubbles

Street project phase

In the remake

What it needs

The remake’s amounts. Work is done with any tool from its list, one go at a time; some steps open only once the steps before them are done.

  1. Plain Bubble: 10

    • ×10 Plain Bubble, 500 in the original

  2. Tiny Bubble: 15

    • ×15 Tiny Bubble, 750 in the original

  3. Hard Bubble: 9

    • ×9 Hard Bubble, 425 in the original

  4. Finetune 35 times

    • Bubble Tuner, tool

    4 energy a go, needs Bubble Tuning, 1 tool wear a go, 1,725 in the original, after the earlier steps

  5. Blue Bubble: 13

    • ×13 Blue Bubble, 611 in the original

  6. Plain Bubble: 12

    • ×12 Plain Bubble, 571 in the original

  7. Tiny Bubble: 9

    • ×9 Tiny Bubble, 405 in the original

  8. Contemplate 30 times

    • Focusing Orb, tool

    3 energy a go, needs Meditative Arts III, 1 tool wear a go, 1,475 in the original, after the earlier steps

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

15,000 iMG, 25,000 mood, 2,500 favor with Grendaline

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

300 iMG, 500 mood, 50 favor with Grendaline

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 14 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.
