461 iMG, 800 mood, 70 favor with Grendaline

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 6 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Pollokoo

ZilZiil Zeal 1 of 2, Sinig Safes 2 of 2, Timtim Timm 1 of 2, Intate Treats 1 of 2

Callopee

Yebeg Yobs 2 of 2, Oyas Yeh 1 of 2

In the remake

In the remake, solo and online. Online, and on your own far from the start, every amount is a fiftieth of the original’s (at least 1), because the original totals were sized for a whole server of players. On your own, a street nearer the start asks less, down to 1/500 next to Hechey Track, and its work needs lower skills or none.
