Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

22,500 iMG, 35,000 mood, 3,375 favor with Mab

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

450 iMG, 700 mood, 68 favor with Mab

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 6 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Callopee

Gomen Grap 2 of 2, Hari Haring 2 of 2, Kinche Klap 1 of 2

Pollokoo

Quluuwaa Luwa 1 of 2, Tibsii Wibbs 2 of 2, Dhalakk Dalliance 2 of 2
