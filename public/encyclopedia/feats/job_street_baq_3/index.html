In the remake

Rewards

Everyone who works on a phase shares its rewards by how much they put in. In the original the whole phase paid this much:

18,500 iMG, 32,000 mood, 2,800 favor with Grendaline

With the remake’s amounts, doing the whole phase yourself pays a fiftieth of that:

370 iMG, 640 mood, 56 favor with Grendaline

For the top contributors

125 iMG

Where it is offered

Part of the project that builds 12 streets. A Street Spirit on a street next door offers it, and the number after a street is this phase’s place among the project’s phases.

Baqala

Plains Rte B, Mart's Way 4, Empty Via 2

Choru

Xalanga

Zhambu

Yokel Yarn 6, Chuff Chase 2, Loutish Ln C
