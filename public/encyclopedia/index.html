Groddle Encyclopedia

Stuck on a quest? Look up the quest, the creature, the person or the place: how to get it, every step, and where to go. Everything here comes from Tiny Speck’s own game code, with what the remake has today, down to every tune and sound, every recipe, buff and Imagination card, the Feats, street projects and the Giants.

  • Items 982. Every item, how to get it and what it is for. 852 are in the remake.
  • Quests 229. How to get each quest, every step, what it needs and what it gives.
  • Animals and creatures 63. Where they live, what you can do with them and what they give.
  • People 53. Vendors and what they sell, the Giants’ shrines and everyone else you meet.
  • Skills 109. What each skill needs, how long it takes and what it opens.
  • Places 59. Every region on the map, its streets and what lives on them.
  • Achievements 653. Every badge, what earns it and what it rewards.
  • Music and sounds 639. Every tune and sound of the original, what the remake plays for it, and what is still missing. 462 of 606 play Tiny Speck’s own recording.
  • Streets 1,417. Every street in the remake: its vendor, shrine, animals, trees and rocks, where its roads lead and how you get there.
  • Stores 15. Every kind of store: what it sells, at what price, and the streets that have one.
  • Imagination upgrades 275. Every upgrade card: what it costs, what it needs, what it opens and what it does in the remake.
  • Tools and recipes 30 tools · 327 recipes. The tools and machines you make things with, and every recipe each one makes: what goes in, what comes out, the skill it needs, energy and time.
  • Buffs 104. Every buff and debuff: what it does, how long it lasts and what gives it to you.
  • Feats and street projects 122. The Feats the whole world worked on together, and the street projects that build new streets: their goals, what they need and what they give.
  • Giants 11. The eleven Giants of Ur: what each one is the Giant of, their shrines, the skills they favor and their Emblems and Icons.
