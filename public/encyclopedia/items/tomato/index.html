Tomato

Plural: Tomatoes

In the remake

5 currants, sells for 3, stacks to 80, 5 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A ripe, heirloom-quality tomato.

  • You can grow this by planting Tomato Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
