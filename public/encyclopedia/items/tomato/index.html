Tomato

Plural: Tomatoes

In the remake

5 currants, sells for 3, stacks to 80, 5 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A ripe, heirloom-quality tomato.

  • You can grow this by planting Tomato Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.

How to get it

  • Produce Vendor, 6 currants, sell for 3
  • Gardening Tools Vendor, 5 currants, sell for 3
  • Uncle Friendly's Emporium, 6 currants, sell for 3
  • Grown in a garden plot from Tomato Seed.
  • Lying in the Mazza'la Gala Party Space while a party is on.
  • Lying in the Nylon Phool Party Space while a party is on.

What it is for

  • Feed it to a Piggy, which plops out Piggy Plop.
  • Eat it for 5 energy.
