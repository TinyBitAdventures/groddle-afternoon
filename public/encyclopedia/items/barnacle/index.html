Mortar Barnacle

Plural: Mortar Barnacles

In the remake

8 currants, sells for 5, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

If clinging to things is a skill, this little fella was a champion. Once prised from a cluster (or "Blister") of pals, Barnacles can be introduced to the Grinder to find a new purpose.

  • This can be scraped from a Mortar Barnacle by using either a Scraper or a Super Scraper.
  • You can use a Grinder or a Grand Ol' Grinder to turn these into Barnacle Talc.
