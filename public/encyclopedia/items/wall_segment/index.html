Wall Segment

Plural: Wall Segments

In the remake

2,500 currants, sells for 1,650, stacks to 10

In Tiny Speck’s game

On its own, it would just fall over. But among a house and other Wall Segments, it becomes the building block of home expansions. Useful for widening and adding floors to houses.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Engineering I and Furnituremaking II.

What it is for

  • Use one in your house to make it a wall segment wider (up to 20). On a house with more floors each new segment takes one per floor, plus Urth Blocks and Girders.
  • Building your home street Tower takes 16, and each extra Tower floor takes 8.
