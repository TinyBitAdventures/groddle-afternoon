What it is for

  • Browse goods to buy and sell the street's wares, and Talk to it for stories and quests.
  • Sometimes a lonely spirit calls for help: visit it to cheer it up.
  • The Firebog Street Spirit: browse goods to buy and sell the street's wares, and Talk to it for its Street Spirit Mysteries, and for quests.
  • The Groddle Street Spirit: browse goods to buy and sell the street's wares, and Talk to it for its Street Spirit Stories and Sonnets of the Street Spirit, and for quests.
  • The Ix Street Spirit: browse goods to buy and sell the street's wares, and Talk to it for quests. It has no conversations of its own.
  • The Uralia Street Spirit: the Uralia spirit rises when you come near. Browse goods to buy and sell the street's wares, and Talk to it for When Down Was Up, and for quests.
