Floor Lamp

Plural: Floor Lamps

In the remake

800 currants, sells for 528

In Tiny Speck’s game

A basic low-level provider of atmospheric downlighting. Upgrade for downlighting with wider appeal and higher aesthetic appeal.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Furnituremaking II.

What it is for

  • Set it on the floor of your house, or of a Tower floor, in Decorate mode.
  • It casts a pool of warm light in the room.
  • Upgrade it to one of 18 other styles: 3 are free and the rest cost currants.
  • Online, you can put it up for sale where it stands, and visitors to your house can buy it.
