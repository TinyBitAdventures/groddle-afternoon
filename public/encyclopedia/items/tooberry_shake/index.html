Too-Berry Shake

Plural: Too-Berry Shakes

In the remake

76 currants, sells for 50, stacks to 24, 5 energy, 5 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A berry tasty too-berry shake.

  • Drinking this will give you the Emberried Hiccups buff (a little pep and vim, every 10 seconds).
  • This can be made with a Blender or purchased from Helga's Liquor Store.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Blending II.

Buffs

  • Emberried Hiccups: A little pep and vim, every 10 seconds
