Hooch

Plural: Hooches

In the remake

15 currants, sells for 10, stacks to 24, 5 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

One jug of premium high-octane hooch.

  • You can make this using Still.
  • You need to learn Distilling to use a Still.
  • Drinking this will give you the Smashed buff (lose energy, gain mood every minute).
