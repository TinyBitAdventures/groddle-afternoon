Potato

Plural: Potatoes

In the remake

7 currants, sells for 5, stacks to 80, 7 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A humble potato.

  • You can grow this by planting Potato Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • Using a Still and the Distilling skill, this can be turned into Hooch.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
