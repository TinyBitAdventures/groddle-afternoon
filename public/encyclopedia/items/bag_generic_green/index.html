In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Hardware Vendor, 1,036 currants, sell for 611
  • Tool Vendor, 971 currants, sell for 694

What it is for

  • It holds 10 stacks of almost anything in one pack slot; not another bag, furniture or Elements.
  • With the Internal Sortitude card you can sort it for one kind of goods (keys, food, herbs, mining and more), and what you pick up goes there first.
  • Rename it (up to 32 letters); drop it and it keeps what is inside until you pick it up again.
