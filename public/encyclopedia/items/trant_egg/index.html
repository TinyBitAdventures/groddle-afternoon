Egg Plant

Plural: Egg Plants

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The noble Egg Plant. As is fitting to a fruit as ovoidly perfect as a prolate moon, Egg Plants (of course) can only be found underground in cool, dark places.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting an Egg Plant Bean in a Dark Patch, or a Patch in your yard or home street.
  • Harvesting this will yield Eggs.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it for Eggs: 3 a harvest, 6 with Arborology I and 12 from Arborology III, once a game day (twice from Arborology II). It must be at least small-sized to harvest.
  • Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) once a game day: when both are done it grows healthier and regrows its Eggs.
