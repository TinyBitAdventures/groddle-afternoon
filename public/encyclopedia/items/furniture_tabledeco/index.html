Tabletop Decoration

Plural: Tabletop Decorations

In the remake

225 currants, sells for 149

In Tiny Speck’s game

A simple wooden table decoration? Perhaps. But squint a little, and you can see the potential that lies within it. Squint a *little* more, and it kind of looks like a chicken with a hat on. But squint any more than that and you'll get wrinkles. Stop squinting.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.

What it is for

  • Set it on a table, desk, counter or other surface in your house or a Tower floor in Decorate mode (or on the floor).
  • Upgrade it to one of 25 other styles: 2 are free and the rest cost currants.
  • Online, you can put it up for sale where it stands, and visitors to your house can buy it.
