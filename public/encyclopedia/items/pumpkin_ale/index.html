Pumpkin Ale

Plural: Pumpkin Ales

In the remake

83 currants, sells for 55, stacks to 10, 50 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

All the refreshment of beer combined with the autumnal savoury heft of pumpkin. Then combined with cinnamon. For no reason anyone can decipher.

  • This item is seasonal. Some of its ingredients can only be grown during the appropriate holidays.
  • Drinking this will give you the Moonwalk buff (a new way to move).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
