Slow Gin Fizz

Plural: Slow Gin Fizzes

In the remake

76 currants, sells for 50, stacks to 24, 10 energy, 10 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A low and lazy slow gin fizz.

  • Drinking this will give you the Slow Gin buff (mood increases every 10 seconds).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker or purchased from Helga's Liquor Store.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Cocktail Shaker a bit more.

Buffs

  • Slow Gin: Mood increases every 10 seconds
