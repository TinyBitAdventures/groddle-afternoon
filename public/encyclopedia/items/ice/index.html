Ice

 

In the remake

6 currants, sells for 4, stacks to 40, 6 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A nugget of purest ice, plucked with a scraper from the Nubbins of the north and ripe in readiness for sno cones, cocktail making, and icing the occasional strained glitch-groin. Basically: frozen water in pleasingly cuboid form.

  • This can be scraped from an Ice Nubbins, located in the region of Nottis.
