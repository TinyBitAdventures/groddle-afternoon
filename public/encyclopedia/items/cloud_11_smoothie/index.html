Cloud 11 Smoothie

Plural: Cloud 11 Smoothies

In the remake

69 currants, sells for 46, stacks to 24, 5 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A Cloud 11 smoothie. Pretty much the best smoothie ever invented in the history of smoothies.

  • Drinking this will give you the Sky Cruisin' buff (a nice mood boost, every 10 seconds).
  • This can be made with a Blender.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Blending I and complete the associated quest.

Buffs

  • Sky Cruisin': A nice mood boost, every 10 seconds
