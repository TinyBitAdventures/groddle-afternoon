Scraper

Plural: Scrapers

In the remake

300 currants, sells for 198

In Tiny Speck’s game

Wielding only this hardy flat-edged blade, a song in their heart and a will to succeed, anyone can boast a backpack of Barnacles or a pocket full of Ice. Why that glitch might want a clacking bag and potentially wet pants is not the problem of the scraper. Scraper just scrapes.

  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from an Alchemical Goods Vendor, a Hardware Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V.
