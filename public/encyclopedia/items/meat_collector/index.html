Meat Collector

Plural: Meat Collectors

In the remake

500 currants, sells for 330

In Tiny Speck’s game

A farm-grade meat collector. It works around the clock to collect meat from your piggies when you can't.

  • You need the skill Remote Herdkeeping to use this.
  • This tool only works in your yard or on your home street.
  • Too many Meat Collectors in a yard may confuse the Piggies and result in the Meat Collectors filling up more slowly.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from an Animal Goods Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Remote Herdkeeping and Tinkering V.
