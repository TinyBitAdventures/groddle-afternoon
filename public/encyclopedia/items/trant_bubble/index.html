Bubble Tree

Plural: Bubble Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The delicate Bubble Tree. Bursting with plain bubbles waiting to be tuned, these somewhat unstable specimens harbor fruit of perfect transparency - and thoughts of bizarre conspiracy.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting a Bubble Tree Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Plain Bubbles, which can be converted into other kinds of bubbles using the Bubble Tuner .

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it for Plain Bubbles: 3 a harvest, 6 with Arborology I and 12 from Arborology III, once a game day (twice from Arborology II). It must be at least small-sized to harvest.
  • Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) once a game day: when both are done it grows healthier and regrows its Plain Bubbles.
