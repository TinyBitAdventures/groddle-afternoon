Fuel Cell

Plural: Fuel Cells

In the remake

380 currants, sells for 251, stacks to 10

In Tiny Speck’s game

Hundreds of tiny power-pellets in each capsule provide gigajoules of energy to fuel the machines.

  • This can be made with a Fuelmaker.
  • You need to learn Fuelmaking to use a Fuelmaker.

What it is for

  • Refuel a Woodworker, Metalmaker, Fuelmaker or Blockmaker with it: each Fuel Cell adds 50 fuel to the machine's 200 tank, which its making burns.
  • Refuel a Crafty Bot with it: each Fuel Cell adds 500 fuel, up to 3,000.
  • Some street projects ask for Fuel Cells.
  • In the quest We're Off to See the Lizard you refuel MaintenanceBot M-64 with one.
