Extremely Hallowed Shrine Powder

 

In the remake

2,000 currants, sells for 1,320

In Tiny Speck’s game

A jar of extremely hallowed shrine powder. It lets the giants know that, when it comes to currying favor, you really mean business.

  • Sprinkling this will give you the Extremely Hallowed Shrine Powder buff (your next shrine donation: superduper powered).
  • This can be made with a Beaker.
  • You need to learn Intermediate Admixing to use a Beaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Beaker a bit more.

Buffs

  • Extremely Hallowed Shrine Powder: Your next shrine donation: superduper powered
