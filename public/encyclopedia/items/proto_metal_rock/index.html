In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Cultivate it in Cultivate mode on your home street or in your back yard from level 10, for 1,200 iMG or more (the price rises with each thing already growing there).
  • It grows into a Metal Rock you can mine with a pick.
  • After 40 uses it wears out; start a Restore project on it to bring it back with more lifespan each time.
