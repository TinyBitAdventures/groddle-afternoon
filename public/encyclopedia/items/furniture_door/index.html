Door

Plural: Doors

In the remake

2,500 currants, sells for 1,650

In Tiny Speck’s game

That which brings you down can also lift you up. This is true of many things, but never more so than in the case of this magical-handled uppy-downy between-floor-device. Or 'Door'.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Engineering I and Furnituremaking II.

What it is for

  • Set it down on the top floor of your house to start the Build a New Floor project; finishing it adds a floor, up to 3, and the Door becomes the way between floors.
  • Once its project has started, it can no longer be picked up.
