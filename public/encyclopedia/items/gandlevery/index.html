Gandlevery

Plural: Sprigs of Gandlevery

In the remake

40 currants, sells for 26, stacks to 50

In Tiny Speck’s game

Plant with furry fern-fronds of fleetfooted goodness.

  • Munching on this will give you the Gandlevery Boost buff (an energy boost every 15 seconds).
  • You can grow this by planting Gandlevery Seeds in a Herb Garden.

Buffs

  • Gandlevery Boost: Gandlevery keeps you wide awake, giving you an energy boost every 15 seconds.
