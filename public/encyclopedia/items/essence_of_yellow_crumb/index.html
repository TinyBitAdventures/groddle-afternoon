Essence of Yellow Crumb

Plural: Essences of Yellow Crumb

In the remake

541 currants, sells for 357, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

Purest concentration, concentrated, for those who need a more expansive brain-span.

  • Imbibing this will give you the Crumbly Thoughts buff (double learning speed for ten minutes).
  • This can be made with a Tincturing Kit.
  • You need to learn Tincturing to use a Tincturing Kit.
