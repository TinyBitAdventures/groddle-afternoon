Essence of Gandlevery

Plural: Essences of Gandlevery

In the remake

181 currants, sells for 119, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

A potent distillation of Gandlevery, as rich in protein and good for energy as it is foul on the tongue and bad for mood.

  • Imbibing this will give you the Gandlevery Charge buff (gain energy and lose a bit of mood).
  • This can be made with a Tincturing Kit.
  • You need to learn Tincturing to use a Tincturing Kit.

Buffs

  • Gandlevery Charge: The Essence of Gandlevery leaves you full of energy at the cost of some mild stomach pain
