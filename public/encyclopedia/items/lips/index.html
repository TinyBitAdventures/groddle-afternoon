Lips

 

In the remake

10 currants, sells for 7

In Tiny Speck’s game

Pre-pursed, super-soft and infused with an antibacterial salve, these lips can be attached to whatever might help you kiss people better. Like, say, an Emotional Bear.

  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor or a Toy Vendor.
