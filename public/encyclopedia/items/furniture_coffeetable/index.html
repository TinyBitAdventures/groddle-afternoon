Coffee Table

Plural: Coffee Tables

In the remake

1,100 currants, sells for 726

In Tiny Speck’s game

A starter table for those who enjoy the concept of resting their brew on an almost flat surface. For those who dream of an *actually* flat one, upgrades are available.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Furnituremaking II.

What it is for

  • Set it on the floor of your house, or of a Tower floor, in Decorate mode.
  • Tabletop pieces and things you set down in the room rest on top of it.
  • Upgrade it to one of 12 other styles: 1 is free and the rest cost currants.
  • Online, you can put it up for sale where it stands, and visitors to your house can buy it.
