Storage Display Box

Plural: Storage Display Boxes

In the remake

500 currants, sells for 330

In Tiny Speck’s game

For the pragmatic showoff in everyone, a box with hidden depths. Not only can you display your prized possessions, but you can tuck a virtually infinite amount of the exact same prized possession behind it, for safekeeping.

  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Engineering I and Furnituremaking II.

What it is for

  • Hang it on a wall of your house, or of a Tower floor, in Decorate mode.
  • Deposit one kind of item in it to show it off on your wall, as many as you like.
  • Set a price and, online, visitors to your house can buy from it; collect the currants it earns from the box.
  • Upgrade it to one of 47 other styles: 4 are free and the rest cost currants.
  • Online, once it is empty, you can put it up for sale where it stands, and visitors to your house can buy it.
