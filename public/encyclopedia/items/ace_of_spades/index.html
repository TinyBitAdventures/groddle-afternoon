Ace of Spades

 

In the remake

3,000 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

Finely honed from old metal, the Ace of Spades is made for the heavy digger. Some say it's overkill, but anyone who dreams of spending another perfect day ruling the world of diggery with an iron fist, this is the ideal purchase, lemme tell you.

  • This is better for harvesting bogs with Bog Specialization skill.
  • This is better for digging patches and dirt piles with Soil Appreciation I skill.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Soil Appreciation V and Tinkering V.
