Knife & Board

Plural: Knives & Boards

In the remake

75 currants, sells for 50

In Tiny Speck’s game

A good knife and cutting board. They can be used to make simple foods.

  • You need the skill EZ Cooking I to use this.
  • Additional recipes will become available if you use the Knife & Board more.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV.
