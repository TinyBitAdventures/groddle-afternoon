Precious Potato Salad

Plural: Precious Potato Salads

In the remake

860 currants, stacks to 10, 860 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

Potato salad, traditionally an affordable staple at Glitchen birthdays, is now fit for fancy dinners and lavish banquets with the addition of the particularly precious Trump Rub.

  • This can be made with an Awesome Pot.
  • You need to learn Master Chef I to use an Awesome Pot.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Master Chef II.
