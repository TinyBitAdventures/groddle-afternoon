Silvertongue

Plural: Sprigs of Silvertongue

In the remake

50 currants, stacks to 50

In Tiny Speck’s game

A blue sparkly bloom of joy.

  • Munching on this will give you the Silverytongued Charmer buff (5% bonus rewards on your next quest or achievement).
  • You can grow this by planting Silvertongue Seeds in a Herb Garden.

Buffs

  • Silverytongued Charmer: Everything good just feels even better. 5% bonus rewards on your next quest or achievement

How to get it

  • Grown in a garden plot from Silvertongue Seed.

What it is for

  • Its shuck verb turns it into Silvertongue Seed.

  • Munch it: +5 mood, -5 energy; 5% bonus on the next quest or badge reward.
