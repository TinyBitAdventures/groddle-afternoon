This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Toy Vendor, 220 currants, sell for 132

What it is for

  • Activate it to open a private Aquarius party space for 10 minutes; a teleporter appears beside you to take you in.
  • Feed currants to the Party ATM inside for more time: 5 minutes for 5,000 or 15 minutes for 15,000.
  • Online, everyone in your party is offered a way in.
