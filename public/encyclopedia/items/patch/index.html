Patch

Plural: Patches

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A humble, weedy patch. With a spade you can dig it, a hoe you can tend it, and, with a bean, even grow a tree in it. (NB: Trees are delicate organisms, and beans will only consent to being planted in the correct region.)

  • You can tend this with a Hoe, dig it with a Shovel or plant a Seasoned Bean in it.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.
  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Tend it with a Hoe to clear the weeds, then plant a Seasoned Bean in it. Pet the seedling three times and it grows into a tree.
  • Out in the world a bean only grows in the regions its tree belongs to, and never an Egg Plant Bean; on your home street any bean grows.
  • Dig it with a Shovel or Ace of Spades for 2 Lumps of Earth (more with Soil Appreciation, sometimes Loam). The hole fills back in after 10 minutes.
