Patch

Plural: Patches

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A humble, weedy patch. With a spade you can dig it, a hoe you can tend it, and, with a bean, even grow a tree in it. (NB: Trees are delicate organisms, and beans will only consent to being planted in the correct region.)

  • You can tend this with a Hoe, dig it with a Shovel or plant a Seasoned Bean in it.
