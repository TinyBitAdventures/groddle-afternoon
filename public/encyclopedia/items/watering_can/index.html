Watering Can

Plural: Watering Cans

In the remake

75 currants, sells for 50

In Tiny Speck’s game

A stainless-steel 5-gallon watering can. It can be used to water plants, trees, and crops.

  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Light Green Thumb I first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Gardening Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Gardening Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV.
