Onion

Plural: Onions

In the remake

4 currants, sells for 3, stacks to 80, 4 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A patented no-tears onion.

  • You can grow this by planting Onion Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
