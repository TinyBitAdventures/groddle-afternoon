Jellisac Growth

Plural: Jellisac Growths

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

It's not something you particularly want to stick your hand into, but the Jellisac Clumps that result from a successful "sac-delving" are worth it. Even if they do make reaching in your bag an unpleasant experience.

  • Scooping this yields Clumps of Jellisacs.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Jellisac Hands.
