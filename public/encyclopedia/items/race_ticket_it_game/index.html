This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Race Ticket Dispenser

What it is for

  • Activate it from your pack to start Game of Crowns: hold the crown for 60 seconds to win. Touch the crown holder to steal it.
  • The winner gets 200 iMG and 50 currants.
  • Played alone, three stand-in challengers join you; in multiplayer it challenges the real players on your street.
