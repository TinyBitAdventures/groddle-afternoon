Spice Plant

Plural: Spice Plants

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The delectable Spice Plant. Seductively dangling with Allspice, the millability of this simple brown seed means that everyone's favorite varietal of spice can be found bounded in one shell if you look hard enough.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting a Spice Plant Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Allspice, which can be ground into other kinds of spice using the Spice Mill.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it for Allspice: 3 a harvest, 6 with Arborology I and 12 from Arborology III, once a game day (twice from Arborology II). It must be at least small-sized to harvest.
  • Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) once a game day: when both are done it grows healthier and regrows its Allspice.
