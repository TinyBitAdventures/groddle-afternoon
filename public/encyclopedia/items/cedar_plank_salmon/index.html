Cedar Plank Salmon

Plural: Cedar Plank Salmons

In the remake

70 currants, sells for 46, stacks to 12, 70 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A wild salmon cooked on a wild cedar plank.

  • This can be made with a Famous Pugilist Grill.
  • You need to learn Grilling I to use a Famous Pugilist Grill.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Famous Pugilist Grill a bit more.
