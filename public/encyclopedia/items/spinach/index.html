Spinach

 

In the remake

3 currants, sells for 2, stacks to 80, 3 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

Hearty green spinach. It's strong to the finich.

  • Activating this will give you the High Jumper buff (You can jump really, really high).
  • You can grow this by planting Spinach Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.

Buffs

  • High Jumper: You can jump really, really high
