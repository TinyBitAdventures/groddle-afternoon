Essence of Hairball

Plural: Essences of Hairball

In the remake

449 currants, sells for 296, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

All the speed-giving power of the Hairball Flower, scraped from the individual hairball ball-hairs, and concentrated into a tincture.

  • Imbibing this will give you the Hairball Dash buff (drastically increase your speed for 1 minute with some energy loss).
  • This can be made with a Tincturing Kit.
  • You need to learn Tincturing to use a Tincturing Kit.

Buffs

  • Hairball Dash: Drastically increase your speed for 1 minute with some energy loss
