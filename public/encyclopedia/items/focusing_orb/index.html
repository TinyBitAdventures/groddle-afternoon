Focusing Orb

Plural: Focusing Orbs

In the remake

1,200 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

An iridescent focusing orb. It calms your swirly thoughts, helps you meditate, levitate and fight the enemies of the imagination.

  • You need the skill Meditative Arts I to use this.
  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor or a Tool Vendor.

Buffs

  • Focused Meditation Cooldown: You can't use Meditation until this is over!
  • Levitating: Yogic flying! You are able to levitate at will (hit spacebar!!)
  • Meditation Cooldown: You can't use Meditation until this is over!
  • Zen: You cannot gain energy from meditation anymore today
