Garlic

 

In the remake

4 currants, stacks to 200, 1 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A nice stinky garlic.

  • Spices are much easier to manage if you have a Spice Rack.
  • Eating this will give you the Garlic Breath buff (your breath is icky).
  • This can be made with a Spice Mill or purchased from a Groceries Vendor or a Produce Vendor.

Buffs

  • Garlic Breath: Your breath is icky
