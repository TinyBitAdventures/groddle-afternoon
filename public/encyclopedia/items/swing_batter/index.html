Swing Batter

 

In the remake

31 currants, stacks to 40, 31 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

After roughly chopping with a knife, this batter is not mixed, blended or whisked, but swung at the end of the arm. Swing batter, batter batter swing, batter (as they say).

  • This can be made with a Knife & Board.
  • You need to learn EZ Cooking I to use a Knife & Board.
