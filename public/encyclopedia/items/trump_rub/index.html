Trump Rub

 

In the remake

128 currants, sells for 84, stacks to 50, 13 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

So called because it trumps all the other rubs in expense, flavour, and the ability to be extremely annoying (if you get it in your eye).

  • This can be made with a Knife & Board.
  • You need to learn EZ Cooking I to use a Knife & Board.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn EZ Cooking II.
