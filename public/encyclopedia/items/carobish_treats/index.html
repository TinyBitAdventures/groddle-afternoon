Raw Bio-Organic Carob-ish Treat

Plural: Raw Bio-Organic Carob-ish Treats

In the remake

41 currants, sells for 27, stacks to 20, 41 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

A chewy, crunchy, apparently ethically-sourced, gritty 'candy-alternative' with a whiff of winter. Leaves a unique chocolatey aftertang you can't quite put your finger on.

  • This item is seasonal. Some of its ingredients can only be grown during the appropriate holidays.
  • Eating this will give you the Sugar Rush buff (faster movement, mood bonus).
  • This can be made with a Knife & Board.
  • You need to learn EZ Cooking I to use a Knife & Board.
