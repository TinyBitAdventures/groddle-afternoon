Roasted Pepitas

 

In the remake

5 currants, sells for 3, stacks to 50, 5 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

Crunchy, nutty, seeds pulled from a pumpkin ready roasted (it must be that hot knife you used). May cause a roasted pumpkin to grow in your stomach. No, not really. Will cause you to feel smug about your healthy snacking choices, though.

  • This item is seasonal. Some of its ingredients can only be grown during the appropriate holidays.
  • Eating this will give you the Smug Healthy Glow buff (energy bonus every 30 seconds).

Buffs

  • Smug Healthy Glow: You feel cleansed and healthy from top to intestine
