Metal Rock

 

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A stupefying combination of classic rock and the power of metal. Most often found in rocky areas, these heavyweights are indispensable in the production of ingots and the metallurgic arts.

  • Metal Rocks can only be mined with a Fancy Pick.
  • Mining this yields Chunks of Metal Rock.

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.
  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Mine it with a Pick or Fancy Pick (you need Mining I) for 3 to 9 Chunks of Metal Rock a swing.
  • It holds 50 chunks, then crumbles and reforms a little later.
