Loomer

Plural: Loomers

In the remake

400 currants, sells for 264

In Tiny Speck’s game

The Loomer (TM) is used to Loom thread into String, General Fabric and Rugs through a process of Loomeration so complex, only those with a deep enough understanding of the Fiber Arts II can perform the Loomering.

  • You need the skill Fiber Arts II to use this.
  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Animal Goods Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
