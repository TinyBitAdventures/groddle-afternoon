Flummery

Plural: Flummerys

In the remake

229 currants, sells for 151, stacks to 12, 229 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

Few things in this life are as tasty as a masterfully prepared flummery. "Just like Mom used to make!" you might say. If you had a mom, that is.

  • This can be made with an Awesome Pot or purchased from a Meal Vendor.
  • You need to learn Master Chef I to use an Awesome Pot.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Awesome Pot a bit more.
