Sparkly Rock

 

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The most desperate-to-be-loved mineral you could ever hope to meet, this minable mini-mountain brings a touch of disco to out-of-the-way places. Full of high-value chunks and crushable minerals, every major miner seeks their own vein of sparkly.

  • Mining this yields Chunks of Sparkly.
  • Mining this requires a Pick or a Fancy Pick.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.
  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Mine it with a Pick or Fancy Pick (you need Mining I) for 3 to 7 Sparkly a swing.
  • It holds 50 chunks, then crumbles and reforms a little later; 20% of the time it comes back as a huge rock of 150 chunks.
