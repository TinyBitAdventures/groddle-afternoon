Super Scraper

Plural: Super Scrapers

In the remake

2,000 currants, sells for 1,320

In Tiny Speck’s game

Replacing the rustic charm of the bestselling original Scraper with super-streamlined barnacle-busting ice-nubbin-bustin' action, the highly desirable Super Scraper offers five times the lasting power for slightly-more-than-five-times the price. What can I say? You pay for convenience (and natty design).

  • This can be made with a Tinkertool.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Engineering I.
