In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Sometimes drops when you pet a Paper Tree.

What it is for

  • Play it to hear its tune: it costs 2 energy, gives 2 mood and is not used up. The first time you hear this tune it pays 50 iMG.
  • Carry it up to a Crab and play it a tune the Crab has not heard, once a game day per Crab, for iMG and currants.
  • Collect all five DR blocks to earn the Musicblock DR Collector badge.
  • Online, play it for another player on your street; that counts toward the quest Blocks That Rock.
