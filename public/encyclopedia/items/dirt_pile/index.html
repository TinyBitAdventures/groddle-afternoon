Dirt Pile

Plural: Dirt Piles

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A pile of dirt. Digging it with a shovel produces various dirty rewards, their quality depending on how deeply you appreciate soil, and how many friends are digging with you.

  • Digging this yields Lumps of Earth and sometimes Lumps of Loam.
  • This can be dug with Shovel or Ace of Spades.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Dig it with a Shovel or Ace of Spades for 2 Lumps of Earth (up to 4 with Soil Appreciation, which can also turn up Loam instead). A pile gives about ten digs and refills one every 5 minutes.
