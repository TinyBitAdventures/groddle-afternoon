Splendid Spindle

 

In the remake

2,000 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

The ancients whispered of the spindle of Cosma that could spin the very fibres of air into cloths of heaven, enwrought with golden and silver light; blue and dim and dark cloths of night and light and the half-light. This is not that spindle. But it is really quite a good spindle, and it lasts longer than the other kind of spindle.

  • You need the skill Fiber Arts I to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Fiber Arts I.
