Fancy Pick

Plural: Fancy Picks

In the remake

1,000 currants, sells for 660

In Tiny Speck’s game

A super-deluxe fancy pick for fancy miners. Okay, maybe it doesn't look that fancy on the outside. It's the inside that counts.

  • If you give Brent too much tequila then be prepared to deal with the consequences. This metal pick would be handy in that situation.
  • You need the skill Mining I to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Mining Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Engineering I.
