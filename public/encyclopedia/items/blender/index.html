Blender

Plural: Blenders

In the remake

150 currants, sells for 99

In Tiny Speck’s game

A Blender is very good for blending juices, smoothies and other concoctions. This seems a generally sturdy model.

  • You can't use a Blender yet: you need to learn Blending I first.
  • Additional recipes will become available if you use the Blender more.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV and Engineering I.
