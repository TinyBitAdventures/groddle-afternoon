Beer

Plural: Beers

In the remake

10 currants, sells for 7, stacks to 24, 3 mood

A shiny can of mmm... beer.

  • Drinking this will make you Buzzed (mood goes up, energy goes down).
  • This can be purchased from a Groceries Vendor, Uncle Friendly's Emporium or Helga's Liquor Store.

Buffs
