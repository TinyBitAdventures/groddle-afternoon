Cauldron

Plural: Cauldrons

In the remake

3,500 currants, sells for 2,310

In Tiny Speck’s game

A charmed pot for potioneers of all levels. This thick, heavy, magic-imbued cauldron is perfect for potions, from novice concoctions to expert unctions.

  • You need the skill Potionmaking I to use this.
  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
