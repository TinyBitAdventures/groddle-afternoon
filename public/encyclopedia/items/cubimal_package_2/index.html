How to get it

  • Toy Vendor, 5,500 currants, sell for 3,300

What it is for

  • Open it to get one of 20 Series 2 Cubimals, picked at random. The Fox and Sloth are common; the Scion of Purple Cubimal is the rarest.
  • Opening it shows how much of the Series 2 collection you own.
