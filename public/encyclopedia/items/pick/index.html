Pick

Plural: Picks

In the remake

500 currants, sells for 330

In Tiny Speck’s game

A relatively sturdy pick, without which mining cannot be done. A plain pick takes care of business, but it's not designed to last.

  • You need the skill Mining I to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Mining Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering IV and Engineering I.

Quest timers

  • Another Day Older and Deeper In Debt: Use your Pick to wear 5 Rocks down to nary a nub in just one game day. The countdown for the quest “Another Day Older and Deeper In Debt”, shown with this item’s picture: if it runs out first, the quest fails.
  • Giv'r till you Shiver: Mine 15 times in just 5 minutes. The countdown for the quest “Giv'r 'til you Shiver”, shown with this item’s picture: if it runs out first, the quest fails.
