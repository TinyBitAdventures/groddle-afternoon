High-Class Hoe

Plural: High-Class Hoes

In the remake

600 currants, sells for 396

In Tiny Speck’s game

More refined than your average hoe, the High-Class Hoe is a hoe you'll be proud to be seen with. Extra investment brings a five-times longer lifespan: With this glamorous new companion you'll be hoeing the whole night through.

  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Soil Appreciation first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Soil Appreciation V and Tinkering V.
