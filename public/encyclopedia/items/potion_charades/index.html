Charades Potion

Plural: Charades Potions

In the remake

400 currants, sells for 264

In Tiny Speck’s game

A classic 'Parlour Potion', this benefits those surrounding the quaffer more than the quaffer themselves - but only if they can guess the word that unlocks the effect.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.

Buffs

  • Charades: Get other players here to guess the secret word, and they'll win a prize!

What it is for

  • One quaff per bottle. Alone, it refuses: you need other people around.
