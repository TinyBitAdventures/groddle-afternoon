Paper Tree

Plural: Paper Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Turns out that paper DOES grow on trees, as long as the tree in question is this one. The sheety fruit of this practical tree is a prerequisite for all manner of Penpersonship skills.

  • Harvesting this will yield Paper.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it by hand for 5 sheets of Paper, once a game day.
  • Water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) and pet it: with both done it grows 9 more sheets, up to 21.
