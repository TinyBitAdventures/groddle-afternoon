Essence of Rookswort

Plural: Essences of Rookswort

In the remake

509 currants, sells for 336, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

Distilled Rookswort harnesses the power of the ancients for ultra-absorbent protection during those heavy rook attacks.

  • Imbibing this will give you the Rook Armor buff (prevents mood loss and lessens energy loss during Rook attacks).
  • This can be made with a Tincturing Kit.
  • You need to learn Tincturing to use a Tincturing Kit.

Buffs

  • Rook Armor: Gird your loins to face down Rookly threats
