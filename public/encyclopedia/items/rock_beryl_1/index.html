Beryl Rock

 

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A mineral dull in colour if not composite, Beryl can generally be found underground, where its eerie green surface calls to miners, refiners and gem collectors alike.

  • Mining this yields Chunks of Beryl.
  • Mining this requires a Pick or a Fancy Pick.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.
  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Mine it with a Pick or Fancy Pick (you need Mining I) for 4 to 8 Beryl a swing.
  • It holds 50 chunks, then crumbles and reforms a little later; 10% of the time it comes back as a huge rock of 150 chunks.
