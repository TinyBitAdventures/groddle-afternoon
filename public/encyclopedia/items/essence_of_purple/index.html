Essence of Purple

Plural: Essences of Purple

In the remake

491 currants, sells for 324, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

You thought Purple was purple? This is seriously purple. Seriously: The purplest.

  • Imbibing this will give you the Purple Journey buff (it cannot be described... only experienced).
  • This can be made with a Tincturing Kit.
  • You need to learn Tincturing to use a Tincturing Kit.

Buffs

  • Purpled Out: Your brain feels like it's trying to escape
  • Purple Journey: HURRY UP PLEASE ITS TIME
