In the remake

6 currantsstacks to 806 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

Many kernels of sweet corn, conveniently located on a sturdy cob.

  • You can grow this by planting Corn Seeds in a Crop Garden.
  • Using a Still and the Distilling skill, this can be turned into Hooch.
  • This can be purchased from a Produce Vendor, a Gardening Tools Vendor or Uncle Friendly's Emporium.
