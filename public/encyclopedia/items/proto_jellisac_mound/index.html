In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Cultivate it in Cultivate mode on your home street or in your back yard from level 7, for 300 iMG or more (the price rises with each thing already growing there).
  • It grows into a Jellisac Mound you can scoop for Clumps of Jellisac.
  • After 240 uses it wears out; start a Restore project on it to bring it back with more lifespan each time.
