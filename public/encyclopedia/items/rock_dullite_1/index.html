Dullite Rock

 

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A study in shades of grey, gray, and a monochromatic 8-bit scale of debatable spelling, this common rock is mostly found underground, is minable, and may contain precious gems.

  • Mining this yields Chunks of Dullite.
  • Mining this requires a Pick or a Fancy Pick.

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Mine it with a Pick or Fancy Pick (you need Mining I) for 5 to 9 Dullite a swing.
  • It holds 50 chunks, then crumbles and reforms a little later; 5% of the time it comes back as a huge rock of 150 chunks.
