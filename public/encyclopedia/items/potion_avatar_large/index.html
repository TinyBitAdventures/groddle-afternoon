Embiggenifying Potion

Plural: Embiggenifying Potions

In the remake

1,000 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

"A noble potion embiggenifies the smallest glitch" reads the tiny label on this tinier bottle. Embiggenifies, you ask? "Yes" say the voices of the ancients, drifting to you through the sour aftersmell of the potion, "Embiggenifies. It is a perfectly cromulent word." For creating an embiggenifying cloud wherever you choose.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Potionmaking II.
