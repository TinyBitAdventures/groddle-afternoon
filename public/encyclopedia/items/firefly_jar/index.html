Firefly Jar

Plural: Firefly Jars

In the remake

100 currants

In Tiny Speck’s game

This is no ordinary jar. Well, it IS, but once filled with fireflies (seven maximum: objects in space need room to fly) just looking at it gives you a great sense of serenity.

  • You won't be able to coax fireflies into this jar till you get the Firefly Whistling achievement.
  • Get yourself a Crystalmalizing Chamber and you can use captured Fireflies (along with some Barnacle Talc) to make Plain Crystals.
  • This can be purchased from an Alchemical Goods Vendor.
