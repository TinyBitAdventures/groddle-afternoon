In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Placed in your home yard with Cultivate.

What it is for

  • Go in to reach its floors: it starts with 2 and grows to 9 through floor projects at its Workbench, and an elevator takes you between them.
  • Decorate each floor with your furniture, as you do your house.
  • Customize it: pick one of 6 styles, choose its parts and paint a name on its sign.
