Tree Poison

Plural: Tree Poisons

In the remake

750 currants, sells for 495

In Tiny Speck’s game

Thick glass conceals a dark truth: the death sentence for five trees. But really, since the inevitable broom of death clears the way for new life, isn't it actually just "a life sentence for five NEW trees"?
No, it's mainly just tree poison.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Botany.

Buffs

  • Poisoner's Guilt: You feel bad for the tree poisoning

What it is for

  • With Botany, pour it on the closest tree within reach (or use Apply poison on a tree): the tree dies in three minutes unless someone gives it the antidote.
  • A dead Wood Tree leaves a Patch and 25 Planks; a dead fruit tree becomes a dead tree you can clear.
  • Off your own home street it costs mood and gives you Poisoner's Guilt. Each bottle holds 5 pours.
