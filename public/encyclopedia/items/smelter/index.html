Smelter

 

In the remake

700 currants, sells for 462

In Tiny Speck’s game

Extracting purest metal from even the dullest chunk of Metal Rock, this classic Smelter is the second step to creating all manner of exciting secondary metals. (Learning Smelting is step one.)

  • You need the skill Smelting to use this.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from an Alchemical Goods Vendor, a Hardware Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Mining Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Engineering I.
