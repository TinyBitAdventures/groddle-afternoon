Crystalmalizing Chamber

Plural: Crystalmalizing Chambers

In the remake

2,500 currants, sells for 1,650

In Tiny Speck’s game

With this tool, every armchair alchemist can be the master of their own future. Or at least make some Plain Crystals. Who knew Barnacle Talc and essence of Firefly would combine to make such fine new age bling?

  • You’ll need Barnacle Talc and Fireflies to make Crystals.
  • You can use this, but it's much better to learn Crystallography first.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Hardware Vendor or an Alchemical Goods Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V and Engineering I.
