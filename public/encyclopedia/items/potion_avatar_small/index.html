De-Embiggenifying Potion

Plural: De-Embiggenifying Potions

In the remake

1,000 currants, sells for 660

In Tiny Speck’s game

When poured on the floor, it releases a very pleasant scent (a strange mixture of cherry tart, custard, pineapple, roast turkey, toffee, and hot buttered toast) and a cloud causing people to feel most curious, like they're shutting up like a telescope or some such.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Potionmaking II.
