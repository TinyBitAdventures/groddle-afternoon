Bean Tree

Plural: Bean Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The good old Tree of Bean. Laden with 57 varieties of identical-looking legume, these protein-machines provide the raw ingredients for hearty chow and base material for new trees.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting a Bean Tree Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Beans.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it for Beans: 3 a harvest, 6 with Arborology I and 12 from Arborology III, once a game day (twice from Arborology II). It must be at least small-sized to harvest.
  • Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) once a game day: when both are done it grows healthier and regrows its Beans.
