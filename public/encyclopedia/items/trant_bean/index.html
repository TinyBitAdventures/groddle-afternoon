Bean Tree

Plural: Bean Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The good old Tree of Bean. Laden with 57 varieties of identical-looking legume, these protein-machines provide the raw ingredients for hearty chow and base material for new trees.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting a Bean Tree Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Beans.
