In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Toy Vendor, 1,100 currants, sell for 660

What it is for

  • Activate it to open a private Val Holla party space for 60 minutes; a teleporter appears beside you to take you in.
  • Feed currants to the Party ATM inside for more time: 10 minutes for 8,500 or 30 minutes for 25,000.
  • Online, everyone in your party is offered a way in.
