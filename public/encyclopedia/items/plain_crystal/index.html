Plain Crystal

Plural: Plain Crystals

In the remake

300 currants, sells for 198, stacks to 30

In Tiny Speck’s game

It shimmers, it glitters, and it's worth a good few currants, but you can't help but think that for a crystal, it's a little bland. Not that you're complaining.

  • This can be made with a Crystalmalizing Chamber.
  • You need to learn Crystallography I to use a Crystalmalizing Chamber.

What it is for

  • Install it in a Crafty Bot: each crystal doubles how many crafts it can queue (up to 9 crystals), and burns out after 1,000 crafted items.
  • Some street projects ask for Plain Crystals.
  • Restoring a worn-out Beryl, Dullite, Metal or Sparkly Rock or a Firefly Hive in your yard takes one or two.
