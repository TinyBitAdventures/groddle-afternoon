Quill

Plural: Quills

In the remake

400 currants, sells for 264

In Tiny Speck’s game

This fancy quill (and the skill of Penpersonship) will let you write messages and notes. And maybe give yourself inky fingers.

  • You need the skill Penpersonship and some Paper to use this.
  • This can be purchased from a Tool Vendor or an Animal Goods Vendor.
