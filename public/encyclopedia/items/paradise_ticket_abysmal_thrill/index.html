In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Comes with an Imagination upgrade card.

What it is for

  • Activate it to be whisked away to Abysmal Thrill for 4 minutes: float up through the deep and collect 22 quoins.
  • Activating costs a little iMG (more at higher quoin multipliers), and you can activate up to 11 Paradise tickets a day. Paradise quoins do not count toward your daily quoin limit.
  • Or eat it for 20 iMG and 1 energy.
