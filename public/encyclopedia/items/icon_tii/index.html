Icon of Tii

Plural: Icons of Tii

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The ultimate decorative item for the true follower of Tii, this Icon of Tii, made up of eleven Emblems, contains powers far beyond simply looking pretty. Great blessings can belong to the appropriately observant.

  • Icons placed on the ground have a chance of bestowing blessings when you walk past.
  • You can make this by combining eleven Emblems of Tii.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Made from 11 Emblems of Tii.

What it is for

  • Ruminate on it for mood, Revere it for energy and Reflect on it for iMG: 25 to 100 each (more above level 20). Each works once a game day, only while it is in your pack, and uses one of the Icon's 20 actions.
  • Tithe it 121 currants (more above level 20) (from level 5, once it has 10 or fewer actions left) for 5 more actions; your next gift from that Icon is doubled.
  • Place it on a street (not in your home). As you walk past it now and then bestows mood, energy, iMG or favor with Tii, asks for a tithe when it runs out of actions, and from level 10 offers a Voices of the Giants talk. Pick it up again any time.
  • Making an Icon of every Giant earns the Giant Lover badge; your first Icon completes the quest Loyalty's Rich Reward.
