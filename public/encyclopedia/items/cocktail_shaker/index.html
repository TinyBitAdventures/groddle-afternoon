Cocktail Shaker

Plural: Cocktail Shakers

In the remake

150 currants, sells for 99

In Tiny Speck’s game

A shiny, chrome-plated cocktail shaker for making special drinks.

  • You need the skill Cocktail Crafting I to use this.
  • Additional recipes will become available if you use the Cocktail Shaker more.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from a Kitchen Tools Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Tinkering V.
