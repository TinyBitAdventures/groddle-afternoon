Chunk of Metal Rock

Plural: Chunks of Metal Rock

In the remake

3 currants, sells for 2, stacks to 50

In Tiny Speck’s game

Chunky and metally, this rock, when smelted, makes Plain Metal. Boring maybe, but it's an excellent base for more ambitious alchemical experimentation.

  • You can use a Smelter to turn these into Metal Ingots.
  • This can be mined from Metal Rock.
  • You'll need to learn Mining I to mine this.
