Butterfly Milker

Plural: Butterfly Milkers

In the remake

400 currants, sells for 264

In Tiny Speck’s game

A mechanized butterfly milker. It will gently milk your butterflies in your absence. Good for busy farmers or those with chapped hands.

  • You need the skill Remote Herdkeeping to use this.
  • This tool only works in your yard or on your home street.
  • Too many Butterfly Milkers in a yard may confuse the Butterflies and result in the Butterfly Milkers filling up more slowly.
  • This can be made with a Tinkertool or purchased from an Animal Goods Vendor or a Tool Vendor.
  • You need to learn Tinkering I to use a Tinkertool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Remote Herdkeeping and Tinkering V.
