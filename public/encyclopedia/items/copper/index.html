Copper Ingot

Plural: Copper Ingots

In the remake

70 currants, sells for 46, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

Pliable yet strong, malleably magnificent, copper is a much-prized material for building. A glitch bearing good, strong copper will always be welcome on a building project.

  • This can be made with an Alchemical Tongs.
  • You need to learn Alchemy II to use an Alchemical Tongs.
