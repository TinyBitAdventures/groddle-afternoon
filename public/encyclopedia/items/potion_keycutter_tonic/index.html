Keycutter Tonic

Plural: Keycutter Tonics

In the remake

1,250 currants, sells for 825

In Tiny Speck’s game

With a sharp metallic taste and acidic fizz, this is a concoction so strong that the fumes given off it when quaffed carve keys into the shape of other keys.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Potionmaking II.

What it is for

  • Quaff it and pick one of your door keys: it turns into a different random key (one of the six Triangle, Circle, Square and Hexagon keys numbered 1 to 6).
  • Each bottle holds 5 quaffs. You need at least one key in your pack.
