Still

Plural: Stills

In the remake

2,000 currants, sells for 1,320

In Tiny Speck’s game

A basic Still for converting Grain, Corn, or the humble Potato into versatile, potent (if not smooth) Hooch.

  • You need the skill Distilling to use this.
  • Because a Still only works when placed on the ground, it is advisable to bootleg Hooch in a private location.
  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor, Helga's Liquor Store or a Tool Vendor.

How to get it

  • Hardware Vendor, 2,240 currants, sell for 1,320
  • Alchemical Goods Vendor, 2,140 currants, sell for 1,320
  • Tool Vendor, 2,100 currants, sell for 1,500
  • Helga's Liquor Store, 2,200 currants, sell for 1,320

What it is for

  • With Distilling, Distill Grain, Corn, Potatoes or a Bushel of Grain into its hopper (500 units), then Collect the Hooch it makes.
  • It makes one Hooch every 2 minutes 20 seconds, even while you are away, and holds up to 50.
