Elemental Pouch

Plural: Elemental Pouches

In the remake

50 currants, sells for 33

In Tiny Speck’s game

An essential elemental pouch. You need it for carrying alchemical elements. What, you thought you could carry them in your HANDS? Requires Element Handling skill.

  • This can be purchased from a Hardware Vendor, an Alchemical Goods Vendor, a Tool Vendor or a Mining Vendor.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Hardware Vendor, 56 currants, sell for 33
  • Alchemical Goods Vendor, 54 currants, sell for 33
  • Tool Vendor, 53 currants, sell for 38
  • Mining Vendor, 75 currants, sell for 33

What it is for

  • It holds your Elements: one stack each of Red, Green, Blue and Shiny, up to 5,000 of each.
  • New Elements go into it first, before your pack.
  • With Elemental Shake you can shake it once a day for 4 to 6% more of each Element (8 to 12% with Super Elemental Shake).
  • Rename it (up to 32 letters); drop it and it keeps what is inside until you pick it up again.
