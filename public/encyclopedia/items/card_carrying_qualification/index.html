Card-Carrying Qualification

Plural: Card-Carrying Qualifications

In the remake

50 currants, sells for 33

In Tiny Speck’s game

This is the most basic, entry-level, bureaucratic item of all. Being qualified to carry cards is a prerequisite to becoming the sort of person who can have Papers at all, let alone Papers which are in order.

  • Don't forget to "activate" this qualification if you want to prove that you can carry cards.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Bureaucratic Hall clerk, 50 currants

What it is for

  • Activate it during Legalize It to earn the Card-Carrying Qualification badge, which you need to learn Bureaucratic Arts and to complete Your Papers.
  • Street projects named Clusterfudge also ask for Card-Carrying Qualifications.
