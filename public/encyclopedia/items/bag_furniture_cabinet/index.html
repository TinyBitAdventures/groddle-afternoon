Cabinet

Plural: Cabinets

In the remake

2,200 currants, sells for 1,452

In Tiny Speck’s game

The kind of unassuming cabinet that looks so plain and basic you suspect it may contain a portal to a secret world, rather than shelves. It contains shelves.

  • This cabinet has 32 slots.
  • This can be made with a Construction Tool.
  • You need to learn Furnituremaking I to use a Construction Tool.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Engineering I and Furnituremaking II.

What it is for

  • Set it on the floor of your house, or of a Tower floor, in Decorate mode.
  • Tabletop pieces and things you set down in the room rest on top of it.
  • Open it to store things in its 32 slots, one stack to a slot; it won't take contraband, Elements, furniture or trophies, and must be empty before you pick it up.
  • Online, players you give a key to can open it too.
  • Upgrade it to one of 13 other styles: 2 are free and the rest cost currants.
  • Online, once it is empty, you can put it up for sale where it stands, and visitors to your house can buy it.
