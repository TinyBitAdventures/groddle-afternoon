Gas Plant

Plural: Gas Plants

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

The ever-mellow Gas Plant. Packed with a stash of General Vapour that's just begging to be gassified. With a love of the murky and mystical, Gas Plants can often be found in totally far out spaces.

  • Petting and watering this is easier with Light Green Thumb.
  • Harvesting this is easier with Arborology.
  • You can grow this by planting a Gas Plant Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield General Vapour which can be gassified into other kinds of gasses using the Gassifier.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it for General Vapour: 3 a harvest, 6 with Arborology I and 12 from Arborology III, once a game day (twice from Arborology II). It must be at least small-sized to harvest.
  • Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) once a game day: when both are done it grows healthier and regrows its General Vapour.
