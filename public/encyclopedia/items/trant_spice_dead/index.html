This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Clear it with a working Hatchet (20 energy) to chop it up for 25 Planks; it leaves a Patch you can tend and plant again.
  • Or sprinkle Fertilidust on it and it comes back to life as a Spice Plant.
