Pumpkin Pie

Plural: Pumpkin Pies

In the remake

111 currants, stacks to 5, 111 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

When people use the phrase 'as nice as pie', they are, in fact, talking about this pie. What goes into it? Fresh pumpkin, crispy pastry, spicy spices, and a big dollop of love. (That's an attitude rather than a hidden ingredient, before you start feeling nauseous).

  • This item is seasonal. Some of its ingredients can only be grown during the appropriate holidays.
  • Eating this will give you the Full of Pie buff (slow movement, but mood and energy bonus).
  • This can be made with a Frying Pan.
  • You need to learn Cheffery I to use a Frying Pan.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Cheffery III.

Buffs

  • Full of Pie: Who ate all the pie? You did, and it was awesome: the pie boosts your mood and energy, though you feel a little heavy.
