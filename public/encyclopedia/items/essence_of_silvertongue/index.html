Essence of Silvertongue

Plural: Essences of Silvertongue

In the remake

283 currants, sells for 187, stacks to 20

In Tiny Speck’s game

The concentrated charm of Silvertongue, giving large returns on upcoming ventures.

  • Imbibing this will give you the Gift of Gab buff (20% bonus rewards on your next quest or achievement).
  • This can be made with a Tincturing Kit.
  • You need to learn Tincturing to use a Tincturing Kit.

Buffs

  • Gift of Gab: 20% bonus rewards on your next quest or achievement due to your incredible way with words
