Wood Tree

Plural: Wood Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

While all trees are woody, the Wood Tree is woodier than most. Tending to your wood is a rewarding solitary pursuit, but gathering friends to engorge your wood before all wielding your choppers simultaneously is both mutually satisfying, and sure to result in the injection of firm, tasty planks into your bag.

  • The more mature the Wood Tree, the more Planks can be harvested from it!
  • Wood Tree grows quicker if petted by many people!
  • You can grow this by planting a Wood Tree Bean in a Patch.
  • Harvesting this will yield Planks.

In the remake

This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found out in the world.

What it is for

  • Harvest it with a Hatchet or Class Axe for 25 to 80 Planks, more the bigger it is, once a game day. Harvesting slowly shrinks it, and harvesting the smallest one fells it.
  • Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can or Irrigator 9000) for mood and iMG.
  • In the Enchanted Forests: harvest it with a Hatchet or Class Axe for 10 to 60 Planks, more the bigger it is, once a game day. Pet it and water it (with a Watering Can) to help it grow. Harvested all the way down, it turns into a seedling and grows back as a new tree 5 minutes later. Tree Poison does not work on it.
