This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Found in Esquibeth's grottos on the quest The Last Pilgrimage of Esquibeth: walk into a note to collect it.

What it is for

  • On The Last Pilgrimage of Esquibeth, walk into it to collect it: it rings its D bell and sets itself into the carved glyph. It hides in all three of Esquibeth's waterfall locations.
  • With every note found, play Esquibeth's song back on the glyph's notes to open the way on.
