Spice Plant Bean

Plural: Spice Plant Beans

In the remake

150 currants, sells for 99, stacks to 100

In Tiny Speck’s game

A small spicy bean. It can be used to plant a spice plant.

  • Beans can only be planted in certain regions. Like attracts like.
  • You can plant this in a Patch in your yard.
  • This can be made with a Bean Seasoner.
  • You need to learn Botany to use a Bean Seasoner.

What it is for

  • Plant it in a tended Patch to grow a Spice Plant: pet the seedling three times and the tree comes up after a few minutes.
  • Out in the world it only grows in the regions where Spice Plants belong; on your home street it grows in any Patch.
