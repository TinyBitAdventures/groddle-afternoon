Cloudberry Daiquiri

Plural: Cloudberry Daiquiris

In the remake

142 currants, sells for 94, stacks to 24, 10 energy, 15 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A nourishing cloudberry daiquiri. It goes down smooth.

  • Drinking this will give you the Floating on a Cloud buff (long-lasting, slow-acting mood boost).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Cocktail Shaker a bit more.

Buffs

  • Floating on a Cloud: Long-lasting, slow-acting mood boost
