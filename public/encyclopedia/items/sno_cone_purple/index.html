Purple Sno Cone

Plural: Purple Sno Cones

In the remake

1,100 currants, sells for 726, stacks to 10

In Tiny Speck’s game

A refreshing and delicious purple Sno Cone. A sophisticated blend of Ice, Hooch and elemental additives and preservatives. Now you too can get all the social frisson of Purple but with none of the nutritional or mental expansion benefits (contains considerable iMG, though).

  • You must be at least level 11 to eat this Sno Cone.
  • This can be made with a Blender or purchased from a Sno Cone Vendor.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Blending II.
