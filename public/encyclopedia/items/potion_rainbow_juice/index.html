Rainbow Juice

Plural: Rainbow Juices

In the remake

750 currants, sells for 495

In Tiny Speck’s game

Where is the end of the rainbow? Apparently, in this bottle. Warning: may produce curious disruptive effect on air around it. Thus the term, 'Anti-Gravity Rainbow'.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
