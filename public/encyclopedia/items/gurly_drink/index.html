Gurly Drink

Plural: Gurly Drinks

In the remake

160 currants, sells for 106, stacks to 24, 5 energy, 30 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A sweet, innocent gurly drink.

  • Drinking this will give you the Easy Animalia buff (all interactions with domestic animals (except singing) do not cost energy).
  • This can be made with a Cocktail Shaker.
  • You need to learn Cocktail Crafting I to use a Cocktail Shaker.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Cocktail Shaker a bit more.

Buffs

  • Easy Animalia: All interactions with domestic animals (except singing) do not cost energy
