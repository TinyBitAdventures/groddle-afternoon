Liquid Super-Hoe

 

In the remake

1,700 currants, sells for 1,122

In Tiny Speck’s game

A bag full of hoes might be able to clear your whole garden, a bottle of Liquid Super-Hoe has more clearing power than any common old hoe-bag. One drop will clear all the plots that can be cleared from the nearest garden.

  • You will not be able to use this unless you have the Master Gardener skill.
  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Potionmaking III.
