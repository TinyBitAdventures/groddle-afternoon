Rookswort

Plural: Sprigs of Rookswort

In the remake

75 currants, sells for 50, stacks to 50

In Tiny Speck’s game

An exotic bud with a pleasantly lasting aftertaste of danger and chaos.

  • Munching on this will give you the Steady as a Rook buff (slow down your "natural mood loss" by half).
  • You can grow this by planting Rookswort Seeds in a Herb Garden.

Buffs

  • Steady as a Rook: Slows down your "natural mood loss" by half
