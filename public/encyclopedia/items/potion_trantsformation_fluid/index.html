Trantsformation Fluid

Plural: Trantsformation Fluids

In the remake

2,500 currants, sells for 1,650

In Tiny Speck’s game

A tree-reassignment liquor, capable of soaking into the roots of any tree or plant and silently, smelllessly, "almost" harmlessly rearranging its DNA.

  • This can be made with a Cauldron.
  • You need to learn Potionmaking I to use a Cauldron.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Potionmaking II.
