Savory Smoothie

Plural: Savory Smoothies

In the remake

96 currants, sells for 63, stacks to 24, 10 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A savory smoothie. It sticks to your ribs and doesn't let go.

  • Drinking this will give you the Savoring Experience buff (periodic mood-booster, based on your current mood).
  • This can be made with a Blender or purchased from Helga's Liquor Store.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you use the Blender a bit more.

Buffs

  • Savoring Experience: Periodic mood-booster, based on your current mood
