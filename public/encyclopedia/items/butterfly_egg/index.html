Butterfly Egg

Plural: Butterfly Eggs

In the remake

400 currants, sells for 264, stacks to 10

In Tiny Speck’s game

An egg seasoned with the specific mix of lepidopteral herbs and spices to hatch a Caterpillar.

  • Seasoned eggs can be incubated under a Chicken.
  • This can be made with an Egg Seasoner.
  • You need to learn Animal Husbandry to use an Egg Seasoner.
