Pumpkin

Plural: Pumpkins

In the remake

41 currants, sells for 27, stacks to 11, 41 energy

In Tiny Speck’s game

One plump and thick-skinned pumpkin. Perfect for carving, mulching, munching, and, in times of need, for using as a carriage drawn by tamed gerbils.

  • You can grow this by planting Pumpkin Seeds in a Crop or Herb Garden.
  • This item is seasonal. It can only be grown during the appropriate holidays.

  • During its holiday it becomes Jack the Pumpkin, Steve the Pumpkin, Hello Pumpkin!, Zille the Pumpkin, Lem the Pumpkin, Roasted Pepitas and Clump of Jellisac.
