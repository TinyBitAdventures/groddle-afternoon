Mortar Barnacle

Plural: Mortar Barnacles

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

An unpretentious lump commonly found growing in marshland. Slow-growing, but once a barnacle reaches full blister, they can be scraped off with the right tool, and ground down.

  • Scraping this will yield Mortar Barnacles.
