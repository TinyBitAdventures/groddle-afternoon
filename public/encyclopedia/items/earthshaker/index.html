Earthshaker

Plural: Earthshakers

In the remake

152 currants, stacks to 24, 30 energy, 10 mood

In Tiny Speck’s game

A seismically upgraded earthshaker.

  • Drinking this will give you the Impervious Miner buff (mining uses no energy at all).
  • This can be made with a Blender or purchased from a Mining Vendor or a Meal Vendor.
  • You need to learn Blending I to use a Blender.
  • The recipe for this will become available after you learn Blending II.

Buffs

  • Impervious Miner: Mining uses no energy at all
