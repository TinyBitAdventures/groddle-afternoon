This item is in the remake.

How to get it

  • Kitchen Tools Vendor, 550 currants, sell for 330

What it is for

  • It holds 16 stacks of spices, and nothing else.
  • New spices go into it first, before your pack.
  • Rename it (up to 32 letters); drop it and it keeps what is inside until you pick it up again.
