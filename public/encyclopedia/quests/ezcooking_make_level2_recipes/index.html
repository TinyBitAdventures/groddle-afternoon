The quest

Make friends and influence people with this late-night snack, featuring a Juicy Carpaccio, Common Crudites and a Cold Taco.

When it is offered

Snacks kid. They make the world go round.

Is there an echo? Snacks. Especially those of the late-night variety. Go make a Sleepytime Feast: a Juicy Carpaccio, some Common Crudites and a Cold Taco. Then you'll see what I'm talking about.

There you have it. A Juicy Carpaccio, Common Crudites and a Cold Taco. A textbook Sleepytime Feast. If I had hands, I would clap. I believe this should more than cover you for your troubles.
