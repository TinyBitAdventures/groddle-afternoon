In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Visit storyland and read a bedtime story, once told all around Ur, to help a little piglet sleep.

When it is offered

You know after a nice full meal, you feel all sleepy and slumberful?

Well, I know of this one piglet that no matter how much he ate, just couldn't feel sleepy.

It was bad, this unsleepiness. But there was one thing which finally helped: a favorite bedtime story told all 'round Ur since the dawn of the Third Era. Would you like to go to storyland and read it?

When you finish

Wasn't that sweet? Here's a cuddly Emotional Bear to remind you of this bedtime story.
