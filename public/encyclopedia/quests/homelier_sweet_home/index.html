In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Once you have the penpersonship skill, visit the home streets (or house) of every one of your friends and leave them a house-warming gift and a note to accompany it.

When it is offered

Isn't it nice to receive house-warming gifts? Now that you're a skilled penperson, visit the homes of each of your friends and leave them a gift with a friendly note attached. I guarantee it'll make you feel all warm inside.

When you finish

Congrats! You've spread warmth to all of your friends and made their homes a little homelier. I bet you feel some of that warmth too.
