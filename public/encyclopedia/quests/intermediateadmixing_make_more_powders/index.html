In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Make a jar of Fairly Hallowed Shrine Powder and the Powder of Mild Fecundity. Make these, and more recipes will be yours.

When it is offered

You’re learning to love the Beaker, kid. Now learn to let the beaker love you. Harder.

Make yourself a jar of Fairly Hallowed Shrine Powder and some Powder of Mild Fecundity. You have the recipes already: all you need are the ingredients.

All in time, little one. Now beak away, my sorcerer. Beak like you’ve never beaked before.

When you finish

You’ve beaked, I can tell. You’re radiant and kind of glowy. But don’t worry, that’s a known side effect, it wears off.

Still, try donating to a Shrine after sprinkling it with a Shrine Powder - the effect will be powerful, if sadly temporary. And be sure to use your Powder of Fecundity on a street full of Animals. They’ll fecund all over the place for you.

Sure I do. Now fecund and leave me be, kid.
