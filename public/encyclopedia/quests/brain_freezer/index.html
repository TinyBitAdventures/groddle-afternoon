In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

You know what's better than a Sno Cone? Five Sno Cones! Collect one of each color: Blue, Green, Orange, Red and Purple. Get them from your friends, from a Sno Cone Vendor in Wintry Place (accessible via Northwest Passage in Groddle Forest or Newcot Close in Groddle Heights) or from the Auctions.

When it is offered

Psst. Kid. It's been two million years since I burned through this atmosphere, but I'm still hot.

Get me a Sno Cone, will you? Better yet, make that five Sno Cones.

Remember, kid, five. That's one each of blue, green, orange, purple and red.

And just so you know, unless you have friends with Sno Cones, it might be the easiest to visit the Wintry Place via Northwest Passage or Newcot Close to do this.

And if you go to Wintry Place you're going to have to find a Sno Cone Vendor. Think you can manage all that?

When you finish

Yoink! I'm taking all those Sno Cones.

Don't worry, kid. You deserve some kudos.

Unfortunately, I gave away my last kudo yesterday. Take some currants and points instead.
