Word Up

Quest

Rebuilt

How to get this quest

  • Offered once you reach level 11.
  • Needs Four Letter Words done first.
  • Four Letter Words, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Spell as many words as possible in three minutes

Where to go

  • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

500 iMG, 500 currants, 250 mood

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Create as many words as possible in three minutes. All the fun of spelling combined with the thrill of racing the clock!

When it is offered

Hey there, kid.

Feel like playing a little game?

You're going to love this one. You get to spell.

Er, that's not quite what I meant. How about we just shrink you down and you start playing. You'll be re-embiggened when you're done, OK?

When you finish

You did good, kid.

For humouring Mr. Block in this latest escapade, I'd like you to have a little extra something.

Of course, they are! Here you go, kid.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
