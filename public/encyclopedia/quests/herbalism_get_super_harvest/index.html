Herbal Hat-Trickery

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Herbalism II.
  • Herbalism II, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Get 3 triple harvests of herb flowers

    • ×3 Silvertongue

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

500 iMG, 300 currants, 100 energy, 50 favor with Mab

Linked to this quest

Comes next

  • Herbalism III, you can learn it after

Things it involves

  • Silvertongue

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Get a 'super harvest' of Herb flowers, three times.

When it is offered

You know what's better than a single herbal harvest, my little underground gardener?

A double harvest. And the only thing better than a double harvest, of course, is…

My Giant, you're good. Yes! Triple! A herbal SUPER-HARVEST! A super harvest of herbs from single seed is a marvel to behold. Of course, while it's marvellous to behold it once, it's even more impressive if you behold it twice. And the only thing better than that…

Ok, ok. Get a triple harvest of herbs three times. Maybe THEN you'll understand my excitement.

When you finish

Super-triple-herbal harvest! Three times the herbs! Not once, not twice but three times! Now tell me that wasn't a marvel unlike anything you've ever seen before!

That's right, kid! Something! And here's something else, just for being you.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
