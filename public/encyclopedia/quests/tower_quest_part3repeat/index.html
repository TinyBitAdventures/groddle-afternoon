Gwendolyn's Task III

Quest

Rebuilt

How to get this quest

  • Offered by Ghost of Gwendolyn.
  • Offered by Zzybzfrx.
  • Needs Gwendolyn's Task II done first.
  • Ghost of Gwendolyn, talk to them
  • Zzybzfrx, talk to them
  • Gwendolyn's Task II, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

    Where to go

    • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
    • Hand it in to Ghost of Gwendolyn.
    • Ghost of Gwendolyn, hand it in

    Rewards

    • Random Kindness

    In Tiny Speck’s words

    The quest

    Complete your journey

    When it is offered

    Damn right. Never say Zzybzfrx doesn’t take you where the cool kids go. One more challenge lies before you. Complete it, and I will have no problem reaffirming your Measure of Self-Mastery. You can do it?

    Then let’s do it.

    When you finish

    This is not the end. You have completed the journey again, and must be on your way, but your self-knowledge is only begun. You may not see Zzybzfrx, that friendly Figment, but he will be there.

    I too am always here, somewhere. Remember me, and heed my warning – do not follow the ways of those who have gone before. You will make your own path.

    As for me, I will return one day, to tell you of my beautiful, lost-so-long, Butterfly-Bone Hair Clip. For now, take my warnings, and my blessing. And this: a token of our esteem.

    From Tiny Speck’s game code.

    In the remake

    You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
