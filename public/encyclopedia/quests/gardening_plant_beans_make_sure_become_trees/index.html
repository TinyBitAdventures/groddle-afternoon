Quest

In the remake

  • Arborology IV, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Plant three beans

    • ×3 Patch

  2. Fully pet three seedlings

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

650 iMG, 425 currants, 50 mood, 65 favor with Spriggan

Linked to this quest

  • Patch
  • Seedling

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Plant three Seasoned Beans in three Patches and lovingly nurture them through that trying Seedling phase. (Important: You can do this one bean at a time, not all at once.)

When it is offered

So, by now you've gotten the hang of this Arborology business, wouldn't you say?

Oh, sure, it's easy when you do it all random-like, running helter skelter all over Groddle Forest and such.

So I've got a challenge for you, my chartreuse-thumbed chum. I want you to plant three separate Seasoned Beans, and single-handedly raise them till they graduate from the Seedling stage.

You know it. But you don't have to grow them all at once. One at a time is fine. Okay?

When you finish

You've given those three Seedlings a promising start, young friend.

I find this all strangely moving.

I'm feeling a little verklempt up here. Take this little reward, then scram so I can collect myself.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
