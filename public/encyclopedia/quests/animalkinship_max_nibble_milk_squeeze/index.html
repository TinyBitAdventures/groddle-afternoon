In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Get the maximum yield from a Piggy, a Butterfly and a Chicken.

When it is offered

Hey, friend. Feeling fecund?

Good. Have you ever noticed how, when you're out there nibbling Piggies, milking Butterflies and squeezing Chickens, sometimes you get better results than other times?

To do this job, you need to go and get the maximum yield from a Piggy, a Butterfly and a Chicken.

Goldurned if I know, kid. They're critters of whimsy. Good luck!

When you finish

Howdy, kid. I see that you've left no piggy un-nibbled... no butterfly un-milked... no chicken un-squee-

Can't a rock finish a sentence around here?

What I was ABOUT to say is: here's your reward. And start practicing your finger exercises for your next job. It's going to be a doozy.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
