Bring In The Sheaves

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Arborology I.
  • Arborology I, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Fruit Tree Harvested

    • Fruit Tree

  2. Bubble Tree Harvested

    • Bubble Tree

  3. Spice Plant Harvested

    • Spice Plant

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

200 iMG, 125 currants, 25 mood, 20 favor with Spriggan

Linked to this quest

Comes next

  • Arborology II, you can learn it after

Things it involves

  • Fruit Tree
  • Bubble Tree
  • Spice Plant

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Harvest a ripe Fruit Tree, Bubble Tree, and Spice Plant. You won't need special tools to do this, but you may need to make space in your inventory.

When it is offered

Take Trees, for instance, kid.

Oh. Wait a sec... did I start in the middle of my thought again?

Let's start again. How about I keep this simple?

Get out there and harvest a ripe Fruit Tree, Bubble Tree and Spice Plant.

That's all you need, kiddo. But you'll also have to make sure you've got space to carry your harvest. Got that?

When you finish

That's a pretty fine bunch of harvests you've harvested. What are you going to do with all those Cherries and Bubbles and Allspices?

If I were in your foot-mittens, I'd consider getting a Fruit Changing Machine, a Bubble Tuner and a Spice Mill. Here's a little gift to help you out.
