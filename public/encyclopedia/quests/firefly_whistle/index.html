In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Explore the lab and learn what you can about how to charm Fireflies.

When it is offered

In the future of one of our pasts, the last days of the Alphite Age, some radical technologists had developed advanced techniques for the charming of fireflies.

This knowledge would benefit you tremendously. I can offer to send you back to this past future, but you must find your own way back ...

Well, it can't be THAT bad. And, you want to be enchanting those Fireflies, right?

When you finish

Well, well. Swallowed it right down, eh? That's one way to ensure you always have the whistle with you.

In any case, from now on, any time you open a Firefly Jar in the presence of Fireflies, your new whistling instinct will kick in. Enjoy!

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
