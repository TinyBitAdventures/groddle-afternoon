The quest

When it is offered

Some people think the butlers are creepy. Well, I KNOW they're creepy.

Well, ok, we can agree to differ on that. But anyway, I want you to make them creepier.

Go find 31 different butlers and give them a craving for brains. By which I mean, say the word "zombie" to each butler. ("Chat" to the butler to open an IM window.)

Not so fast, Buffy.

Whatever. It's all undead to me. Here's the catch: you have to do it all in one single game day.
