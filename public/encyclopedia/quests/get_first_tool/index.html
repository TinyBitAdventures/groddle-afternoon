Tooling Around

Quest

Retired by Tiny Speck in 2012

Not in Groddle

Players had this quest once, then Tiny Speck retired it.

The Gentle Island tutorial replaced the old introduction in 2012.

It isn’t planned for Groddle: the Gentle Island tutorial is in the game.

Reference

It survives in words only: a fan wiki of 2012 listed it among the early quests.

How it was given

  • Offered in the 2011 tutorial once you knew a skill: get the tool that goes with it. Street Spirits checked it when you bought one.
  • You need to have earned impossible_achievement.
