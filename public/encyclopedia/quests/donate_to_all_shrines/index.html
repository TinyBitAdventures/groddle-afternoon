In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Donate a little something to a shrine for each of the Eleven Giants. You can donate anything, but the more valuable the item(s) you donate, the more favor you earn.

When it is offered

I see you've discovered shrine donation …

That's good, because donation is an important thing: you'll need to do a lot of it to get ahead.

Find a shrine to each of the Eleven Giants and donate something — you'll learn a lot along the way.

When you finish

Very good indeed. Your favor with the Giants will come in handy as you learn more skills.

So, don't forget to keep donating. And, if I was you, I'd start inquiring into some of Shrine Powders

Yeah, Shrine Powders … ask around. For now, I have a little something for you.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
