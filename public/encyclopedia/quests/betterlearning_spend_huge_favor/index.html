This Favor Goes Up to 11. Or 1511

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Better Learning V.
  • Better Learning V, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Spend 1511 favor points at once.

    • Shrine to Alph

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

750 iMG, 500 currants, 100 mood

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Shrine to Alph

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Spend 1511 favor points in one go speeding up skill learning. (Subtle Hinty-Nudge: collecting multiple Emblems from one Giant will increase your favor capacity…)

When it is offered

You believe there's such thing as too much of a good thing, kid.

Darn tootin'. And you know what counts as a really good thing? Those glorious little Emblems you get from donating to Giants.

Oh yes. Your favor limit with a Giant increases with each subsequent Emblem. So multiple Emblems make for bigtime favor capacity, as my grandmother used to say. But she never elaborated. So try something for me, will ya?

Collect as many Emblems as it takes to increase your favor capacity to 1600. Then accrue 1511 favor points for whichever Giant you please.

Yup, 1511. Then spend them all at once on speeding up learning a skill. Which skill? Well, I'll leave that up to you. I'm easy like that.

When you finish

You know how they say diligence is its own reward? Well, that's only half of it. Because not only did you get ultraspeedy edumacation and the lasting admiration of your giant, but, ta da! A little clutch of thingamyjigs from me to you. You're welcome, kid.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
