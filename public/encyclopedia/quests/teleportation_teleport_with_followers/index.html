Three's a Cloud

Quest

In multiplayer

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Teleportation III.
  • Teleportation III, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Teleport with 3 or more followers

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

650 iMG, 350 currants, 100 mood, 65 favor with Lem

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Teleport with three Glitches (or more) in tow.

When it is offered

You know what's hot?

*Sigh*. Apart from you. Think about it: that whoosh you get when you suddenly feel the dispersed particles of three or more Glitches rush past your head. You know what that is, kid? Cloud commuting.

I mean what I say, peanut. Commuting from one place to the other as a group, in a dispersed cloud of teleported particles: Cloud Commuting!

That's the spirit. Gather three or more Glitches, get them to follow you and, when you're sure they're all following, whisk them away by teleportation. Where? Doesn't matter. Just as long as you zap there.

When you finish

BAM! Quick, easy, low in energy and high in efficiency, no?

And you all got here in one piece! One piece each, I mean! And you didn't even swap any pieces along the way.

Nooooooooo. Well, first time for everything, I guess. Well, to celebrate your survival… I mean, triumph… here's a little something.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

This quest needs other players: you can do it in multiplayer.

It counts things only other players can do for you.
