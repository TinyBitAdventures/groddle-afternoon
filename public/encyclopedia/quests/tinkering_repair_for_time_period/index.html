Tinker Well

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Tinkering I.
  • Tinkering I, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Repair for 15 seconds straight

    • Tinkertool

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

175 iMG, 100 currants, 50 energy, 15 favor with Alph

Linked to this quest

Comes next

  • Third Time Really Is the Charm, comes next
  • Tinkering III, you can learn it after

Things it involves

  • Tinkertool

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Use a trusty Tinkertool to sustain a 15-second repair on a broken tool. (Hint: You can break a tool yourself through excessive use, or you can get a broken tool from a friend.)

When it is offered

Let me drop a little hard-boiled philosophy on you, kid. Sometimes in life, things break. You can cry about it. Or you can cry about it and then fix it.

Of course you do. Now get a Tinkertool, then find a broken tool and Repair the daylights out of it.

When you finish

See? Good as new. Waste not, want not. A currant saved is a currant--

Grrr. I don't care for interruptions! But I'm still going to give you your reward. This time.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake
