Tower Defense

Quest · repeatable · with other players

Never finished

How to get this quest

  • The game offers it as you play.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

    Where to go

    • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

    Rewards

    1,500 iMG

    In Tiny Speck’s words

    The quest

    Call down a rook attack on your Tower and defend it with a little help from your friends.

    When it is offered

    Nice Tower you've got going there. It'd be a shame if something were to... happen to it.

    Oh, you know: war, pestilence, Rook Attacks... that sort of thing.

    O RLY? Well then, if you're ready, I think I can arrange it. You're going to need some assistance though. Let me know when you're ready.

    When you finish

    Well, you managed to handle that pathetic little Rook Attack. [slow clap].

    You're an incisive one, aren't you. Well, here's your rewards. Maybe next time it'll be harder.

    From Tiny Speck’s game code.

    In the remake

    Never finished in the original: Tiny Speck’s code has it, but the game never offered it.

    It is a game played against or with other players.
