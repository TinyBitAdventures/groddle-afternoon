In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Obtain and activate the Card Carrying Qualification. You can get it at any one of the Bureaucratic Halls: Gregarious Grange in Groddle Meadow, Baeli Bray in Muufo, or Chego Chase in Andra. Hint: You can avoid the 'red tape' by acquiring it from other players or at an Auction.

When it is offered

Pssst. As all respectable citizens know, you must have Your Papers in order to use public transportation.

That's right, but before you can 'complete' Your Papers you need to get and 'activate' the Card Carrying Qualification.

Perhaps. But, you're in luck. I know a guy who knows a guy that can help you with your problem. Interested?

When you finish

Congratulations! You have taken the first step toward Bureaucratic officialdom.

As with all first steps, there is a second, a third …

Once you learn Bureaucratic Arts I, then get and 'complete' Your Papers, you'll be able to use public transportation. Hint: You can also get Your Papers from other players or at Auction. Off you go!
