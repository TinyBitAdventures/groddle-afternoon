Batterfly Bounty Booster

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • You need to be over level 17.
  • You need to have earned 4-Star Cuisinartist.
  • 4-Star Cuisinartist, earn it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Pet Piggy

  2. Nibble Piggy

  3. Feed a Batterfly Meat

  4. Collect Guano

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

50 iMG, 50 mood, 50 energy, Recipe: Batterfly Bounty Booster Bar

Linked to this quest

Comes next

  • Batterfly Bounty Booster II, comes next
  • Awesome Pot

Things it involves

  • Guano
  • Batterfly
  • Piggy
  • Meat

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Help the Rock to discover how much guano you can get from a batterfly.

When it is offered

Ever wish you could get a bit more outta those batterflies? That guano is worth its weight in currants!

Right, let's see how much guano you can get from a batterfly.

Find yourself a piggy and give it a pet and nibble for some meat. Then feed the meat to a batterfly roaming the caves.

When you finish

Hmm. That wasn't as much guano as I'd hoped for, but it reminded me of an old recipe I saw in a book once.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
