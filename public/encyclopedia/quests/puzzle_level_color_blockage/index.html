In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Unblock your way to the exit, collecting rewards along the way.

When it is offered

You look like a fashionable young glitch. "On trend", "in-the-know", so to speak.

Indeed. But, have you heard of color blocking? It's all the rage.

Oh brother. Yes! But, I'm not actually here to talk to you about fashion. I'm here to tell you that you're about to learn about color blocking, or perhaps I should say "color unblocking". If you're as intrigued as you appear puzzled, then I'd say it's time to go!

When you finish

I see you are well-versed in the art of color unblocking. Well done. Although, in my opinion, a little color suits you. Oh, and here's a little something for you to play with!

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
