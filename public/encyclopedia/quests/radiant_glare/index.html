Radiant Glare

Quest

Rebuilt

How to get this quest

  • Offered once you have 11,306 iMG in all.
  • Needs Walk Toward the Light done first.
  • Needs the Imagination upgrades for walking speed and jumping.
  • Walk Toward the Light, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. You made it!

  2. Complete the Journey

Where to go

  • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

1,500 currants, 200 mood, 200 energy, 150 favor with Alph

Linked to this quest

Comes next

  • Mental Block, comes next
  • Radiant Glare, comes next

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Hone your skills as you travel toward the radiant glare above.

When it is offered

Young Glitch, you once walked toward the light, proving your eyes are sharp. But, without practice, your skills may become dull.

Hone your skills as you move up toward the radiant glare. And, remember, "Do or do not … there is no try."

When you finish

A wise Glitch once said, "True mastery of a skill was only the beginning step to understanding it."

You are one step closer to mastery. Understand? Either way, you deserve this reward.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
