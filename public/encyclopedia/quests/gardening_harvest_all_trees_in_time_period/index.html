One, Two, Tree, Four, Six, Eight

Quest · timed

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Arborology II.
  • Arborology II, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Harvest an Egg Plant

    • Egg Plant

  2. Harvest a Bean Tree

    • Bean Tree

  3. Harvest a Bubble Tree

    • Bubble Tree

  4. Harvest a Fruit Tree

    • Fruit Tree

  5. Harvest a Gas Plant

    • Gas Plant

  6. Harvest a Spice Plant

    • Spice Plant

  7. Harvest a Wood Tree

    • Wood Tree

  8. Harvest a Paper Tree

    • Paper Tree

  9. All of it within 4 h: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

350 iMG, 225 currants, 25 mood, 35 favor with Spriggan

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Harvest each of the eight different kinds of Trees, all in one game day.

When it is offered

Hey, kid. Do you know how many different kinds of Trees there are in the world?

And?

Eight. There are eight kinds of Trees.

Tell you what. Just to help you refresh your memory, I'm going to propose that you harvest each kind at least once within one game day. Pretty easy, right?

Well, apart from maybe Wood Trees, they're a rare breed. But then, if you had one in your back yard, or knew someone who did… Not so hard, right?

Funny, that's what the wood tree said. Right. Go, kid! And if you do it, I've got something special for you, right here in my invisible left pocket.

When you finish

Good work, bub. Now, quick! Tell me the names of all eight Trees that you harvested. In order!

Just messing with you. Here's a little present, just for being such a sport.
