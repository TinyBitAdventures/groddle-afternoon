In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Fire up a patented, accept-no-substitutes Famous Pugilist Grill and make a Simple BBQ and 3 Grilled Cheese.

When it is offered

You know, kid, I've never met a person who doesn't like a good Grilled Cheese.

So how's about you get yourself a Famous Pugilist Grill, fire it up, and grill up a Grilled Cheese. Better yet, make that three.

And while you're at it, cook up a Simple BBQ.

All good people like BBQ, kid. You in?

When you finish

Well, kid. If the last great Famous Pugilist himself were here right now, he'd say...

Um "mumble-mumble, humna-humna-humna".

Hecked if I know, kid. He lost his last tooth approximately the same time as his last braincell. But I bet he still liked Grilled Cheese.
