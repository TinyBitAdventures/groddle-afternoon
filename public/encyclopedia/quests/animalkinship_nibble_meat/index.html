Get a Rasher

Quest · timed

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Animal Kinship III.
  • Needs Porcine Pleaser done first.
  • Animal Kinship III, learn it
  • Porcine Pleaser, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Collect Meat

    • ×13 Meat

  2. All of it within 4 h: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

175 iMG, 120 currants, 50 energy, 15 favor with Humbaba

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Meat
  • Piggy

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Successfully obtain 13 Meat from 13 Piggies, all in one nibbly game day.

When it is offered

Piggies are smarter than dogs you know.

Er, never mind that now. Just find yourself 13 different Piggies, and nibble them ever so gently to get 13 Meat.

And don't forget to pet them first. Just because they're animals doesn't mean they don't appreciate the niceties.

When you finish

Wow. Thirteen piggies nibbled in one day. That is a lot of Meats.

You can say "Nice job, self!" Here's a little something. Go buy yourself some shopping things.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
