Aranna Scramble

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Comes after Master Milker.
  • Master Milker, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Milk Butterflies

    • ×11 Butterfly

  2. Collect Meat

    • ×7 Meat

  3. Water trees

    • ×17 Watering Can

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

350 iMG, 250 currants, 150 mood, 100 energy, 75 favor with Humbaba

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Butterfly
  • Meat
  • Watering Can

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Take a hike through Aranna to care for the animals and trees. Nibble from 7 piggies, milk 11 Butterflies, pet and water 17 trees, squeeze 23 chickens, get at least 1 Super Harvest from them, and incubate 3 seasoned eggs in a game day.

When it is offered

Whoa there, I say whoa there!

There is a mountainous and forested region, that could use your loving touch.

Aranna, my friend- Aranna needs you.

The trees and animals throughout Aranna need a friendly Glitch like you to give them your full attention. Are you game? Good. You have 1 game day to complete this task.

Go!

When you finish

You did it- you provided your loving touch to the residents of Aranna and for that, they thank you with this substantial reward. Huzzah!
