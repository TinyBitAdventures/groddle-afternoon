In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Gumbo, Pasta & a Pickle? Why not. Using an Awesome Pot, prepare a Meat Gumbo and a nice Tasty Pasta to go with it. Oh, and a Pickle on the side. (Note: You may need to use the pot for a bit to learn the recipe for Tasty Pasta.)

When it is offered

I'm getting hungry. You know what makes a nice meal?

Meat Gumbo and Tasty Pasta! That's my favorite.

Sure does! And with a Pickle on the side, it is a complete meal. Make me one? (Note: You may need to use the pot for a bit to learn the recipe for Tasty Pasta.)

When you finish

Oh boy! It was great of you to make me this dinner. But you know what?

No, it's good. I already ate! So, you can keep the dinner ... and let me give you a little something so you know I appreciate it.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
