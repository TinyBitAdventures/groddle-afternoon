In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Visit 3 "Seam Streets" located behind key-locked doors around the world. Hint: You will "Finds" the entrance to one of the seam streets on the west side of Andra. (To find the rest, you'll need to ask around, or go exploring!)

When it is offered

Ancient lore tells of locations made from the dreamy goo that act as "seams" between the fully-imagined regions of the world.

You will not find them on any map, and to be worthy of entering, you will need the correct key.

Go forth, and visit 3 locations that act as "seams" between various regions of the world.

When you finish

Congratulations, worthy glitch. You have visited 3 of the seam streets in the world.

The giants smile upon you!
