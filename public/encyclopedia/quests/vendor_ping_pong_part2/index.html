In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Deliver 1 pack of tomato seeds, 1 pack of onion seeds, a hoe, and a watering can to the Tool Vendor in Cebarkul, in Uralia.

Don't forget to buy these items from the Gardening Vendor before you head off.

When it is offered

I have a friend who sells tools over in Uralia, in Cebarkul. He's been thinking of growing a garden, but he needs some seeds.

He has a terrible green thumb, so he needs my help. Can you take him 1 pack of tomato seeds, 1 pack of onion seeds, a hoe, and a watering can?

You'll have to buy these items from me first. The tool vendor will pay you back. You can count on him. He may have a black thumb, but he's chock full of scruples.

When you finish

Thank you so much! I can't wait to get started on my garden.

Don't believe anything the Gardening Vendor tells you. He's just jealous that my pumpkin took first prize in the Groddle Forest Harvest Fair.

Here's your money, plus a little something extra for your trouble.
