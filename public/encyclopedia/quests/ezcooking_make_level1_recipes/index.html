The quest

Put a Knife & Board to work and make Flour, a Lazy Salad and a Cheese Plate.

When it is offered

Kid, it appears you have some facility with the Knife & Board now.

Great. There are few things easier to make than Flour, Lazy Salad or a Cheese Plate.
