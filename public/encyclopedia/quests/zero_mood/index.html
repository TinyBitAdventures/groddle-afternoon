Tickle Me Emo

Quest · timed

Never finished

How to get this quest

  • Offered when something happens in the game (mood zero).
  • You need to be over level 15.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Hug 33 Different Players

    • ×33 Emotional Bear

  2. All of it within 4 h: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

750 iMG, 1,000 mood, 400 energy

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Emotional Bear

The quest

Break free from your angsty funk and embrace 33 different players in one game day – you just might feel better!

Hey emo kid! Looks like too much black has got your mood to zero!

You’re dark! Dark in the heart! Go hug 33 different players to assuage that inner torment. But hurry, you only have ‘til breaking dawn!

When you finish

Didn’t I tell you you’d chirk right up? You know what they say about hugs per day!
