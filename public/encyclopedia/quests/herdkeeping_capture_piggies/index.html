Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Herdkeeping, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Hogtie Piggies

    • ×3 Hog-tied Piggy

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Capture three wily Piggies in the wild (NB: Your garden's not very wild, Sly…). Want a tip? Nothing tempts Piggies like Pig Bait.

When it is offered

Hey, kid. How do you feel about doing some good old-fashioned hogtying?

Aw. You're cute. No, don't worry about it. Piggies love being tied up.

So get out there, find three Piggies, roaming in the wild - not your own garden, mind, the wildy-wild - and bring'em back bound and gagged, OK?

Just for effect. You in?

When you finish

Soooeeey. Nice work, kid. I like what you did with the butterfly knot. You sure you haven't done this before?
