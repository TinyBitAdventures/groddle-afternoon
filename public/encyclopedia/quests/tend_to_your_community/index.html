Tend to Your Community

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered after you finish Shucks To Be You.
  • Shucks To Be You, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Visit the Herb Gardens

    • Silvertongue

  2. Plant 5 Yellow Crumb Flower Seeds

    • ×5 Yellow Crumb Flower Seed

  3. Plant 5 Silvertongue Seeds

    • ×5 Silvertongue Seed

  4. Harvest 5 Herbs

    • ×5 Yellow Crumb Flower

Where to go

  • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

100 iMG, 50 mood, 150 favor with Friendly

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Visit the Chakra Phool Herb Gardens, in Chakra Phool. Plant 5 Yellow Crumb Flower Seeds and 5 Silvertongue Seeds. Then, harvest 5 herbs of any type, and donate them to the shrine at Pahsmeq Diggs in Kajuu.

Well, shucks. It looks like you're getting pretty good at planting and harvesting herbs.
