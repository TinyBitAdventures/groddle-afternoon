In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Go crazy with the sauce. Use a Saucepan to make a Tangy Sauce, a Wicked Bolognese Sauce and a Creamy Catsup. Tasty on their own, they're also key ingredients in Meat Tetrazzini, Tasty Pasta and Expensive Grilled Cheese.

When it is offered

There are people, and I won't name names, but people who fail to take sauce seriously.

I'm afraid so. They assume it's just a sauce, and not a path to something else.

I don't care for your tone, but get a Saucepan and use it to make a Tangy Sauce, a Wicked Bolognese Sauce and a Creamy Catsup, and we'll see if maybe a little sauce will spill your way.

When you finish

Now you've done it. You've gone and made a Tangy Sauce, a Wicked Bolognese Sauce and a Creamy Catsup. Now you're ready to make Meat Tetrazzini, Tasty Pasta and Expensive Grilled Cheese. I'd tell you to put this towards chef school, but it looks like you're doing fine on your own.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
