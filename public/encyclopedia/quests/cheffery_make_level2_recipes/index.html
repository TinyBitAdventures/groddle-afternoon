The quest

Make some eggselent dishes. Use your Frying Pan to make 7 Mexicali Eggs, 11 Green Eggs and 15 Eggy Scrambles. And yes, eggselent is too a real word.

Mmmmm. Eggs. Gotta love em. Right, kid?

Well, let's put your Cheffery skills to work here and see what you can do. Grab your Frying Pan and go make 7 orders of Mexicali Eggs, 11 Green Eggs and 15 Eggy Scrambles.
