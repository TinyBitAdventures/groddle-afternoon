The quest

Use a Blender to make a Fruity Juice, a Lemon Juice, and an Orange Juice. Hold the pulp.

When it is offered

Hey, kid. I feel a touch of the scurvy coming on. Can you hook me up with some juice?

I don't know if you've noticed this yet, kid, but I'm not quite like other rocks.

So scurvy. Capiche?

So could you get yourself a Blender and whip me up some Fruity Juice.

Not so fast. And a Lemon Juice.

Hold on. And an Orange Juice.

And what? That's it.

Hold the pulp.

That juice looks mighty bracing, kid. But I forgot something.

I can't drink it.

Forget what I said. I'm a rock. I say a lot of things. But here. For being such a good sport, I'm going to give you an extra-big reward.

One thing, though.

I see pulp.
