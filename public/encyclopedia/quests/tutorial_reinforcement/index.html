A Little Tutorial Reinforcement

Quest

Retired by Tiny Speck in 2012

Not in Groddle

Players had this quest once, then Tiny Speck retired it.

The Gentle Island tutorial replaced the old introduction in 2012.

It isn’t planned for Groddle: the Gentle Island tutorial is in the game.

Reference

It survives in words only: our reference library holds a picture of its quest window (pet a Pig, water a Tree, mark the Map, for 150 xp, 20 energy and 30 mood) and a quest log showing it done. We describe them here rather than show them.

How it was given

  • It came straight after Start Learning Your First Skill in the 2011 tutorial.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

    • Piggy

  2. Water a tree

    • Watering Can

  3. Reach a map destination

150 iMG, 30 mood, 20 energy

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

As a wise giant once said, repetition breeds love: go and find a Pig to pet (then pet it), a Tree to water (then water it) and mark a destination on the Map, and go to it.
