In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Create 1 Piggy Egg, 1 Chicken Egg and 1 Butterfly Egg using an Egg Seasoner.

When it is offered

Hey, kid. You like eggs, right?

Whoa. Hey. No need to overcompensate.

Happens to the best of us, kid. Now, you know where eggs come from, I hope?

I deserved that. OK, so here's what I need you to do. Harvest three Plain Eggs. Then use an exciting mix of porcine, poultry and lepidopteral spices to transform three Plain Eggs into 1 Pig Egg, 1 Chicken Egg and 1 Butterfly Egg.

So you don't know everything, do you, kid? Plain Eggs can be seasoned using spices, gases, bubbles and other stuff.

I could, but I won't. Go figure it out, kid. It'll be fun!

When you finish

I knew you could do it, kid. Now did you know that seasoned eggs can be incubated under chickens?

Another time, kid. Another time.
