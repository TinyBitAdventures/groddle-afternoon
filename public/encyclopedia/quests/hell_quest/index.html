The quest

Enter the Hell Bar in Panka Surazu, Naraka, exchange your Diabolical Drink Ticket for Wine of the Dead and drink to your good hellth… er, "health."

When it is offered

I know it looks gloomy down here, but it ain't so bad; great atmosphere, no cover charge and even a free drink ticket.

Take this drink ticket to the Hell Bar at the far end of Naraka and exchange it for a bottle of Wine of the Dead. I have a feeling you’ll enjoy its revitalizing qualities.

When you finish

How do you feel now, kid? Like a great weight has been lifted off your shoulders?

Great! Now go explore Naraka. Have fun. Don't do anything I wouldn't do. Oh, and take these extras for your excellent work.
