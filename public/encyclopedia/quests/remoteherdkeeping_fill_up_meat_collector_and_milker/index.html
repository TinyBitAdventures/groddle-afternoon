In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

And after you got Milk, got Meat? Get a Butterfly Milker and a Meat Collector, install them in your yard, then set them to milking and harvesting until they're full up.

When it is offered

I've been watching you kid. You're busy and important. You're on the move, and you've got better places to be than hanging around the house waiting to harvest butterflies and piggies.

You need to get yourself a patented, practically dumdum-proof Butterfly Milker and Meat Collector.

Set them up in your yard, and I'll check back with you when they're full up, mkay?

When you finish

Looks like that Butterfly Milker is as full as it gets. And if that Meat Collector were any more full, it would be... really, really full.

Here's an idea you maybe haven't tried: give one of your Butterfly Milks a good shake and see what happens.

Nothing happens. It just makes other people uncomfortable.
