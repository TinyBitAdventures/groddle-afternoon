In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Use a Focusing Orb to fetter your unfettered thoughts and achieve the elusive state of Max Relax. (Receive 50 mood in one meditation)

When it is offered

So you're coming along in the Meditative Arts, kid. But have you achieved Max Relax?

If you're not sure, then you haven't. So grab your Focusing Orb, clear your head, let your mind and your nether bits feel free, and try hitting 50 mood in one try.

When you finish

You did it! Max Relax!

That's the best part. Congratulations, kid. And enjoy this little reward. You deserve it.
