Beam You Up

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • The game offers it as you play.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Teleport to <b>Tamila</b> from <b>Shimla Mirch</b>

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

250 iMG, 50 currants, 25 favor with Lem

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Click on Teleport in the imagination menu to help you create a landing point anywhere in Tamila, and then jump to it from anywhere in Shimla Mirch. It's practically pain-free and almost foolproof.

When it is offered

So you've chosen to embrace the fine art of Teleportation.

Exactly. You've got better things to do with those legs. Just to give you a little teleportational jump start, I'm going to offer you a little something special if you can figure out how to get yourself from, oh, let's say Shimla Mirch, over to Tamila.

I'll even give you a little hint. Go find a nice spot somewhere in Tamila. When you get there, click on me and create a new landing point. After that, you're on your own.

When you finish

Nice work. The first Teleportation jump is always the toughest. But look at you! You re-assembled almost perfectly.

Now that you've figured out the basics, keep practicing. You can change your landing point any time you want. And in the meantime, enjoy this little gift.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake.
