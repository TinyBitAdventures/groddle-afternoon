In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Get 23 favor points at the Shrines of five different Giants by donating to them. (Only the stuff donated from this moment onward counts.)

When it is offered

Hey, don't panic, kid, but…

Well, I've heard a rumour that you've been favoring some Giants over others.

Well, I'd advise you to repair your reputation, and fast. You want to know how?

Watch the sarcasm, chummo. Get yourself to the Shrines of five different Giants, and donate... oof, counting from this moment onward, at least 23 favor points-worth of stuff to all of them? Yeah, that should do it. Git, kid!

When you finish

Phew. I think everyone feels like you're spreading your favor a little more evenly.

And just for that, here I am, spreading MY love, all over you, very evenly. In the form of remuneration and stuff. This isn't coming out right at all.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
