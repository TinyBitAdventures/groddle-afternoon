Meditation Station

Quest · repeatable

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Comes after Relax to the Max.
  • Relax to the Max, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Meditate on the same street on 11 game days

    • ×11 Focusing Orb

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

1,000 iMG, 500 currants, 50 energy, 100 favor with Cosma

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Focusing Orb

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Visit the same street at least once every 6 game days and meditate for 60 seconds. Do so on 11 game days.

When it is offered

Ah, the virtues of meditation know no bounds.

But y’know, meditation without practice is kind of, like, spacing out every now and then. I mean it's nice, but...

You know that favorite spot of yours?

Well yeah, of course there’s a few, but pick one and meditate there for the next 11 game days and then we’ll talk real enlightenment.

When you finish

How ya feeling, chum?

Well, there you go. The best part is that you can keep finding new places to practice and the rewards are just the same.

Here’s some stuff for you. Not that you need it - you transcendent being, you.
