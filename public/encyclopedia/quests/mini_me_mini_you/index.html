Mini Me, Mini You!

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered once you reach level 10.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Give 7 cubimals to the NPCs they depict

    • ×7 Chick Cubimal

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

1,000 iMG, 10,000 currants

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Chick Cubimal

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Give seven different characters their likeness in cubimal form.

When it is offered

"There are no grades of vanity, there are only grades of ability in concealing it..." and nothing makes it harder to conceal vanity than the cheap thrill of possessing a cubimal which looks just like you.

Hold your horses, vainy-pants! No-one said anything about a cubimal that looks like you.

Many of the inhabitants of Ur have cubimals that look like them. Also, low self-esteem. It would greatly improve the mood of the world if their vanity were tickled a bit.

That you are! But don't get too touchy-feely. I just need you to find 7 different cubimals and deliver them to the Ur-inhabitant they resemble. Can you do that for me?

No. But maybe some other stuff. And the pride that comes from a job well done.

When you finish

What-ho! By my calculations the collective mood of Ur has been raised precisely 0.324%, thanks to your hard work.

It's significantly more than zero.

Perhaps, but... look, do you want these rewards or not?
