When you finish

You have seen all there is to see here. Do you now feel prepared to do battle with the Rook?

I understand your trepidation, but you must simply remember this…

The Rook, while incredibly powerful, is vulnerable to the attacks of Martial Imagination. Before you can harm the Rook, you must use a Focusing Orb to stun it.

Once the Rook has been stunned, the Pious Glitch can prime a shrine, turning the donations of others into powerful attacks.

I believe you are now ready. Go, rally the forces of imagination in defence of Ur! And may the Giants be with you.
