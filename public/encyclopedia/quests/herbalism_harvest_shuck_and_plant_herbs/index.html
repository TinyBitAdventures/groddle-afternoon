Shucks To Be You

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Herbalism I.
  • Herbalism I, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Shuck herbs until you have 6 seeds

    • ×6 Yellow Crumb Flower Seed

  2. Plant 6 seeds

    • ×6 Gandlevery Seed

  3. Harvest your herbs

    • ×6 Hairball Flower

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

300 iMG, 200 currants, 50 mood, 50 energy, 30 favor with Mab

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Shuck herbs until you have 6 seeds. Then plant the seeds, and harvest the herbs.

When it is offered

The circle of all life can be traced to one simple, physical act, little Herbalist.

Shucking. You like to shuck, kid?

Shucking! No new Herbs exist without someone having shucked the generation before them. A good vigorous shuck provides the seeds for Herbal life as we know it to continue.

Try it: take these Herbs, and shuck them to release 6 Seeds, then plant those seeds to grow new Herbs, and harvest them. You in?

When you finish

Beautiful isn't it?

The act of shucking is beautiful, of course, but the whole circle of life thing: each generation giving up Seeds for the next, and… oh. You're just waiting for your reward, aren't you?

Go on, then, you little shucker. Take these tiny tokens, and begone…

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
