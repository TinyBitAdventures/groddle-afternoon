In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Using a Spice Mill, turn ordinary Allspice into 2 each of Ginger, Curry, Mustard and Black Pepper.

When it is offered

Spices are the variety of life, chum. And you look like you're ready for a little variety.

Don't fight it. We're going to start small. Get yourself a Spice Mill and some Allspice. Then use the Spice Mill to create 2 each of Ginger, Curry, Mustard and Black Pepper.

And then I'll tell you what. Yes?

When you finish

That's some solid Spice Milling, friend. And now look at all the Spices you've created.

Use them as ingredients in foods and drinks. Buy a Spice Rack for storing them. Use your Spice Mill to make even more Spices!

The question isn't what can you do with Spices. The question is what can't you do with them!
