In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Use your 'Teleport via Map' ability to investigate the disappearance of famed explorer Ilmenskie Jones, last seen in Axis Denyde.

When it is offered

Kid, I can only assume you’ve heard of Ilmenskie Jones.

Seriously? Ilmenskie Jones and the Marauders of the Lost Bean? Ilmenskie Jones and the Sparkly Femur? Ringing any bells?

OK, wow. Well, he’s only the most famous adventurer in all of Ur. And he’s a senior lecturer in porkthropology at Porcine University. In short, he’s dreamy.

Well he’s heard of you. He left a note in his office when he set off to explore the infamous Sealed Cavern of Axis Denyde. "If you don’t hear from me within a week," it read, "please notify whats-their-name. You know, the pedantic one" He could only have meant you, sure as eggs is eggs.

See?!? Clearly you. So using your newly acquired powers of Teleportation IV, perhaps you could hop right on over to him and help him out? Problem is, it being a sealed cavern, the entrance is blocked off. So you'll need to locate Axis Denyde in Pollokoo on the map, and then teleport in that way. Would you mind checking in to see what’s taking him so long?

When you finish

What a guy, huh, kid?

Amazing? I know. Man, if I could teleport using the map, like him or, well, like you? Imagine the jinks I could get up to…

Sweet thought, kid. Let's do that: whenever the mood takes you - well, a few times a day for free, and more with tokens - just open the map, choose a street to teleport to, and click! We'll be off! Exploring! That's cheered me up. Let me do the same for you (as if meeting Ilmenskie Jones wasn’t already reward enough).
