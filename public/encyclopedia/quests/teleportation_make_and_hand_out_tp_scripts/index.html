The World Through Ur Eyes

Quest

In multiplayer

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Teleportation V.
  • Teleportation V, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Make three teleportation scripts

    • ×3 Teleportation Script

  2. Give out three teleportation scripts

    • ×3 Teleportation Script

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

1,200 iMG, 750 currants, 200 mood, 120 favor with Lem

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Teleportation Script
  • Paper
  • Quill

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Create three Teleportation Scripts for some of your favourite places in the world, and distribute them among strangers.

When it is offered

Close your eyes, little Glitchling.

Have you closed them? They look like they're open. Close your inner eyes. The eyes beyond your Glitchly eyes. Are they closed? How can you still hear me? Look, just forget the eye thing. Just imagine your favourite places in all of Ur. Are you doing that?

Now, wouldn't it be nice to share those places with others?

Yes. Yes it would. So do it! With your newfound skill, some Paper and a Quill, write three Teleportation Scripts to your favourite places in Ur, and hand them out to three different people. Share your love of the land, y'hear?

When you finish

I say "favourite places in Ur", and you choose… Woah. Those? Really?!?

Don't get me wrong they're, y'know, lovely. Just. Um. Here! Here's a little reward - use it wisely. Have you thought about travel?

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
