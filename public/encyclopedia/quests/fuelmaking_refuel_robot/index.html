We're Off to See the Lizard

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Fuelmaking.
  • Fuelmaking, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Speak to the Shady Bureaucrat

    • Bureaucrat

  2. Make a Fuel Cell

    • Fuelmaker

  3. Refuel a Subway Maintenance Bot

    • Fuel Cell

Where to go

  • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Contribute some strictly off-the-record Fuelmaking support to a shady bureaucrat: make a Fuel cell and use it to refuel a Maintenance Bot in any Subway Station.

When it is offered

psst, kid. hey. hey, kid. psst. hey. fuelmaking.

A friend of mine wants to ask you for a Fuelmaking favour. He told me to ask you on ‘the down low,’ only I don’t know what that is. I think that means I’m supposed to be crouching, but I don’t have any legs, so I’m trying a sort of verbal crouch. It’s harder than it sounds.

I don’t exaggerate, kid. Anyway, this friend—or more of an acquaintance, really, or an acquaintance of an acquaintance—has asked to meet in an undisclosed location, for maximum secrecy. “Come alone,” he said.

See, I don’t understand what that means either. Does it mean you’re in?

When you finish

Good work, kid!

And now there’s a bureaucrat out there who owes you a favour. Might come in handy someday, you know. Speaking of handy, the Bureaucracy sent you a little something for helping out.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
