Start Learning Your First Skill

Quest

Retired by Tiny Speck in 2012

Not in Groddle

Players had this quest once, then Tiny Speck retired it.

The Gentle Island tutorial replaced the old introduction in 2012.

It isn’t planned for Groddle: the Gentle Island tutorial is in the game.

Reference

It survives in words only: our reference library holds two 2011 videos of new players starting the game, and both show this quest being completed. We describe them here rather than show them.

How it was given

  • Your Magic Rock gave it in the 2011 tutorial, soon after you arrived.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Start learning your first skill

Rewards

100 iMG, 1,000 currants, 20 energy

The quest
