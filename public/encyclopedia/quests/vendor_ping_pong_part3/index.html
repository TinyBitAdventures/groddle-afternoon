In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Deliver 3 tubes of butterfly lotion to the Cooking Vendor who works in Level 2 East, in the Ilmenskie Caverns.

Don't forget to buy the lotion before you set off.

When it is offered

You've been such a great help so far. Are you up for another errand?

There's a fellow who sells hard-to-come-by cooking ingredients. He works down in Level 2 East, in the Ilmenskie Caverns. He has a thing for butterfly lotion. I don't know if he eats it or uses it on himself or what.

It's not my place to judge, so can you bring him 3 tubes of butterfly lotion?

I try. You can buy the lotion from me. The Cooking Vendor in Level 2 East will pay you back. He's good for it. Despite his bizarre penchants, he's quite trustworthy.

When you finish

My lotion! Excellent. My precious butterfly will be so happy. He's been complaining about my calluses.

What has the Tool Vendor been telling you? Oh, never mind. Besides, you wouldn't believe what that guy's into.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
