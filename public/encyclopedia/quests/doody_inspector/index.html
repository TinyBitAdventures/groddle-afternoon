Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Comes after Porcine Pleaser.
  • Porcine Pleaser, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Plops Examined

    • ×5 Piggy Plop

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

1,200 iMG, 700 currants, 100 mood

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Piggy Plop
  • Piggy

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Find and investigate 5 Piggy Plops. Tip: you can make a Piggy plop by feeding it any Crop products (like potatoes, onions, broccoli, cabbage, corn, etc.)

When it is offered

Hey, kid! I've got a job for you. But I'll tell you right now: you're going to get your hands dirty.

Let me tell you about the gig first. See, something's up with the Piggies.

Yeah. And there's only one way to get to the bottom of it.

You need to get out there and examine some Piggy Plop. You may have to feed the Piggies to encourage them to do their doody.

Don't worry. Not only does Piggy Plop smell like fancy perfume, it also has powerful life-enhancing properties.

No. But will you do it anyway?

When you finish

That wasn't so bad now, was it, kid? For being a good sport, here's a bunch of currants and stuff.

Don't mention it. Now go wash your hands. No offense, but you stink a little.
