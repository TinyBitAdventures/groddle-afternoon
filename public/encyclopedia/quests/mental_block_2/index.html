How to get this quest

  • Offered once you have 39,898 iMG in all.
  • Needs Mental Block done first.
  • Mental Block, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Solved Puzzle

  2. Complete the Journey

Where to go

  • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

2,200 currants, 200 mood, 200 energy, 150 favor with Lem

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Create a path to the exit by dropping blocks along the way.

When it is offered

You look bedrazzled.

You know, bedrazzled, confuzzled, disorientled. I know, I dazzle you with my infinite knowledge of words.

But of course, you don't think. You've got another mental block. Ready to tackle it?

When you finish

You sure cleared up that block of a mess. Now fluster down and enjoy your reward.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake. Its place was never archived, so it is rebuilt from recordings of the original.
