Peter out Peat

Quest · timed

In multiplayer

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Bog Specialization.
  • Bog Specialization, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Fully harvest a Peat Bog

    • Shovel

  2. All of it within 9 s: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

375 iMG, 325 currants, 50 energy, 40 favor with Grendaline

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Shovel

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Grab your Shovel and wrangle some friends to help you harvest a full Peat Bog until it's empty. In less than 9 seconds.

When it is offered

Greetings, my round-headed friend. I have a job for you. Use your Shovel to harvest a full Peat Bog until it's empty. In 9 seconds.

Did I mention you can invite some friends to help you? Better now?

When you finish

Congratulations. You and your confreres harvested this entire bog in less than 9 seconds.

Unfortunately, I've only got enough on me to reward just you.

Might I recommend a round of heartfelt high-fives?

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
