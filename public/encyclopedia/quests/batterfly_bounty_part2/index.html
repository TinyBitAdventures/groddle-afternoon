Learn a long forgotten recipe that could get you more guano from those reclusive batterflies.

When it is offered

This recipe looks promising. To be safe you probably shouldn't eat any yourself though. Those ingredients could really foul up your mood.

Okay then, grab your Awesome Pot and whip up a batch of Batterfly Bounty Booster Bars and try feeding one to a batterfly.
