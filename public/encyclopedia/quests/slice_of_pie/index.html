A slice of Pie

Quest

Never finished

How to get this quest

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

    Where to go

    • Hand it in to Bureaucrat.
    • Bureaucrat, hand it in

    Rewards

    171 favor with Friendly

    In Tiny Speck’s words

    The quest

    An unfortunate accident put a stain on the relationship between the Rangers and the Bureaucrats. Time to help!

    When it is offered

    *sigh I love my job, helping keep Ur organized, but some days I have regrets.

    Many cycles ago, I was very good friends with a Bureaucrat named Henrique.

    It's not a typical Bureaucrat name, but he isn't your typical paper pusher either. Anyway, I decided to throw a party so that he could meet some of the other Rangers and we could start building some bridges.

    I completely agree! Sure, we were imagined by the giants to do different things, but that's no reason we can't be friends!

    It was great, until it became a disaster. The butterfly band was great. We were dancing up a storm. Until the pie.

    The giants imagined the Bureaucrats to like order, to keep things in line, to always have enough staples, to keep things clean.

    After pulling off a great full-gainer, I hit the edge of the table. The pie arced through the air and landed square on Henrique's boss's brand new tie.

    Uh oh is right. They all stood up straight and marched right out of the party. Henrique has not spoken to me since.

    Not a peep. I still feel awful about the pie on the tie. Say... could you help me?

    Great. Take this card I wrote. It just needs the signatures of some other Rangers. If you could then deliver it to a Bureaucrat, it will make its way to Henrique.

    Oh fantastic, this means so much to me.

    When you finish

    What is this?

    Hmmm. It is in the appropriate 27B-6 format. Oh and it is countersigned by the correct authorities within the Ranger organization.

    I agree. Where would we be if we didn't follow the correct procedures? I'll go ahead and get this filed appropriately.

    No thank you. Clearly you talked some sense into those ruffian Rangers. Maybe they are not such a bunch of savages after all.

    *stares at you* What are you implying? Do you also have a fondness for pirouetting pie?

    Good. Thank you for this apology form. It will be delivered. Good day.

    From quests/slice_of_pie.js in Tiny Speck’s game code.
