In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Use a Cocktail Shaker to froth up 5 Creamy Martinis. Then drink them! Here's a tip: To make a Creamy Martini, you're going to have to milk some Butterflies.

When it is offered

Kid, have I ever mentioned that you look like the daring, adventure-seeking type?

And daring, adventure-seeking types like you need daring, adventurous drinks.

That's why I think you're ready for this mixological adventure.

Get yourself a Cocktail Shaker, then make and drink 5 Creamy Martinis.

The creaminess comes from the Butterfly Milk. Or, specifically, how the butterfly milk reacts to the Onions.

I can tell you're excited. Ready to get started?

When you finish

Those looked even tastier than usual.

The secret is shaking AND stirring them. And then shaking them again.

That would be the onions.
