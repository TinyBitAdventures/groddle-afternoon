The Daily Grind

Quest · timed

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Refining II.
  • Refining II, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Refine 200 Ore

    • ×200 Grinder

  2. All of it within 2 min: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

500 iMG, 325 currants, 250 energy, 50 favor with Zille
