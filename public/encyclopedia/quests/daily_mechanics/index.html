A Day's Work

Quest

Never finished

How to get this quest

  • You need to be over level 4.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

    Rewards

    400 iMG, 250 currants, 50 mood, 50 energy

    In Tiny Speck’s words

    The quest

    Do these things in a game day: Collect Imagination from the magic rock in your house; re-deal iMG upgrade cards; hit your daily quoin limit; reflect on an icon (tip: you might visit Home Streets of high level players to find an Icon).

    When it is offered

    Hey, kid. Ever feel when a day ends that there's something you forgot to do?

    No more! I'll teach you a little routine that has been practiced since the dawn of imagination.

    OK! Do these things in one game day, and the motivational reward will be yours. Go!

    Oh - collect imagination from the rock, re-deal upgrade cards, hit your daily quoin limit, and lay your hands on someone else's icon.

    Visit the home streets of high level glitches.

    When you finish

    Productive day, innit?

    No worries, there's always the next day.
