A Slatheration of Jellisacs

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Jellisac Hands.
  • Jellisac Hands, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Get a Jellisac super harvest

    • Jellisac Growth

  2. Slather Jellisacs on a Shrine to Grendaline

    • Shrine to Grendaline

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

350 iMG, 200 currants, 100 mood, 50 favor with Grendaline

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Jellisac Growth
  • Shrine to Grendaline
  • Clump of Jellisac

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Gather Jellisacs until you get a 'super harvest', then take them to a Shrine to Grendaline and slather the Shrine in Jellisacs.

When it is offered

Fine pair of Jellisac Hands you've got there, old chap.

You're welcome. And well-equipped to do me a kindness. Well, not me, per se: Grendaline.

You heard right, kid. Grendaline, all reverence to her watery glory, has a bit of a craving for Jellisacs. Don't ask why: I think she might gargle with them or something. All I know is, it would be peachy if you could gather Jellisacs until you get a 'super harvest', then take them to a Shrine to Grendaline - any Shrine to Grendaline, and slather the shrine in them.

Yup, slather. Not donate: slather. You'll see when you get there.

When you finish

Grendaline thanks you.

Well, she would if she didn't have a throat full of Jellisac.

Don't ask how the science works, kid, somehow they soak into the shrine and end up in the mind of Grendaline's mouth. Seriously, it's very complicated. You know what's not so complicated? Rewards. You gots some. Enjoy.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
