Another Simple Sample

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered after you finish A Blissful Trip.
  • A Blissful Trip, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Mine 100 chunks of sparkly while in Ajaya Bliss

    • Chunk of Sparkly

  2. Grind 100 chunks of sparkly while in Ajaya Bliss

    • Grinder

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

200 iMG, 150 currants, 75 mood, 75 energy, 20 favor with Zille
