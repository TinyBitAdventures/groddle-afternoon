Chasing Hubba Dubba Hugs

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • You need to be over level 15.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Get 3 Hugs in Tremit Rub

    • Emotional Bear

  2. Get 3 Hugs in Patomi Bunya

  3. Get 3 Hugs in Krios Palm

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

467 iMG, 100 currants, 43 mood, 17 energy, 100 favor with Lem

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Emotional Bear

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Travel to the following streets within Besara and get one hug each from three different players.
Tremit Rub
Patomi Bunya
Krios Palm
Be careful some strange Glitches can hug a little to tightly!

When it is offered

Did you know the world of Ur is built on Hugs?

Well it is true, a rock never fibs! It is doubly important to Hug strangers and receive their cosmic hugs! Are you ready to do your Hugglily part?

Perfect, Besara hug levels are low, get there on the double and gather allies to give you hugs.

When you finish

Now don't you feel all warm and fuzzy?

Now that the cockles of Ur's heart is now full you can abstain from hugging for a little while.

In return for your gathering of all the huggers we offer you these fine rewards.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
