In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Make a jar of Fertilidust Lite powder. For this, you will need 15 Humbabol and 10 Potoxin compounds. And a Beaker. Obviously.

When it is offered

Delving into the dark arts of admixing, eh?

Well, dark in the sense of ‘pleasant and above board, if a tiny bit mysterious’. Try making some Fertilidust Lite powder. Your Beaker knows the recipe already: all you need are the ingredients.

When you finish

And they shall call you 'All-Powerful Font of Fertilidust!' They shall call you 'Beako! Beako!'

No. But they should. Still, you should try sprinkling some of your shiny new Fertilidust Lite powder near a Tree, and watch it fizzle with health, grow up quicker, and start speaking Humbabanese.

No. But the other two things? Totally.
