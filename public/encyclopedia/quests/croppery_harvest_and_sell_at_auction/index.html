Tater 'n' Mater

Quest

In multiplayer

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Croppery II.
  • Croppery II, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Harvest 50 Potatoes

    • ×50 Potato Seed

  2. Harvest 50 Tomatoes

    • ×50 Tomato Seed

  3. Sell 50 Potatoes at Auction

    • ×50 Potato

  4. Sell 50 Tomatoes at Auction

    • ×50 Tomato

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

700 iMG, 450 currants, 150 energy, 70 favor with Mab

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Potato Seed
  • Tomato Seed
  • Potato
  • Tomato

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Grow 50 Potatoes and 50 Tomatoes, then sell them at an Auction. You will, of course, need your own Crop Garden or a Community Garden for all this growing. You can cultivate a Crop Garden in your yard or home street.
