A Holiday Thing

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Your Magic Rock offers it once a year, in the Glitchmas season.

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Type "yay" into local chat

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

10 mood

  • Glitchmas Present

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

All you have to do is type "yay" into local chat and you will get a gift.

When it is offered

It's a special time of year. A time that makes me feel a little funny. And when I feel funny, I like to give presents.

All you have to do is type "yay" into the local chat and I will give you a gift.

When you finish

Yay. Yay! YAY! YAY!!! You did it. I put a little present into your pack. Unwrap it and see what you think!

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

In the remake

You can do this quest in the remake during the Glitchmas season.
