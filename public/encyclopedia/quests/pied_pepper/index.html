In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Show tribute to our Zilloweeny ancestor by backing oodles of pie, dropping them off and then paying tribute.

When it is offered

Hey kid, you know that tale of the Pied Piper right?

Well I bet you've never heard of the guy who baked all those pies!

Kid, you wouldn't be here if that man didn't bake his little heart out every Zilloween! Think of all the mice that got to dance!

Let's pay a little tribute to him, what do you say?

Good! I want you to bake 10 pies, and deliver them to 10 places. Once you are done, sprinkle some pepper on that place that has water for rememberance.

It's alright kid, you're doing that pie maker proud. FOR THE DANCING MICE!

When you finish

It's beautiful kid, just beautiful. The way that pepper floats on the water brings a tear to my eye and a runny nose. Probably because I sniffed it. Makes you feel good don't it?! Zille is very pleased.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
