In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Use a Tincturing Kit and your Tincturing skill to create all seven Tinctures in an act of Top Tincturous Tincturery. And ponder the word 'tincture', while you're at it. Tincture. Tincture. Tincture.

When it is offered

Ah, a student of the Tincturing arts, are you?

Fancy! Well, mastering tincture theory is one thing, putting it into practice is quite another.

Sure it is, kid. You'll need a Tincturing Kit, for one thing. A Tincturing Kit, the ability to function with scorched nasal hairs, a super-glitchean gag reflex and…

Never mind. Let's start with the Tincturing Kit. Get one, have a go at creating the seven essential essences, and then we'll talk again.

When you finish

Nice work! How are those nasal hairs?

If you can't hear'em screaming, you've mastered that Tincturing Kit faster than I ever thought possible.

You are. And you should try those tinctures. But meanwhile, for your dedication to the art of the tinct, here's a little bump…
