Epicly Perilous Potian Trial of Perilousness

Quest · timed

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Potionmaking II.
  • Potionmaking II, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Make 7 different potions in one game day

    • ×7 Door Drink

  2. All of it within 4 h: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

1,600 iMG, 200 mood, 400 energy, 160 favor with Tii

Linked to this quest

Comes next

  • Potionmaking III, you can learn it after

Things it involves

  • Door Drink
  • Cauldron

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Using a Cauldron, make seven different potions in a single game day.

When it is offered

Hm, what have we here? Intermediate Potion-Maker, is it? In days of yore…

Nono, not yawn, kid, YORE. In days of yore, no potion-maker was taken seriously until they undertook the Epic Perilous Potian Trial of Perilousness.

Oh it is. Are you prepared for the Epicly Perilous Potian Trial of Perilousness? Have you girded your loins for epic peril?

Right. Go forth then… and create seven unique potions.

…In one day.

When you finish

You survived!

Tough? I know. Perilous? Yes, it's legendary for that. Epic? You wouldn't be the first to say it.

Wow. You've come a long way, Potion Maker. I only hope you're not above accepting a little benediction for your quest? I thought not. Here you go…
