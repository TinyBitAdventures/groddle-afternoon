In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Make 1 of each type of the following cocktail drinks with your cocktail shaker: Creamy Martini, Mabbish Coffee, Spicy Grog, Carrot Margarita, Cloudberry Daiquiri, Gurly Drink, Slow Gin Fizz, Cosma-politan, Flaming Humbaba, Pungent Sunrise, Face Smelter

When it is offered

How well can you handle the Cocktail Shaker?

Well, prove your mix and shake skills by making each of these drinks: Creamy Martini, Mabbish Coffee, Spicy Grog, Carrot Margarita, Cloudberry Daiquiri, Gurly Drink, Slow Gin Fizz, Cosma-politan, Flaming Humbaba, Pungent Sunrise, Face Smelter.

When you finish

That's some fine shaking. Friendly is pleased!
