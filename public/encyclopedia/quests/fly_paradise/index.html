In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Use 3 tickets to goto Starlit Night, Cloud Rings, and Abysmal Thrill where gravity has been obsfucated by the Dark Energy of the Rooks.

When it is offered

Gravity getting you down?

Ever wonder how the Dark Energy of the Rooks effect Ur?

That's the ticket! Rook attacks often leave a dark residue adrift in the twirling mass of the giants imagination repelling the self-attraction of Ur. But there's some good news.

The giants have imagined wonderful lands surrounding this residue where gravity can't get you down! Get upgrade tickets and travel to Starlit Night, Cloud Rings, and Abysmal Thrill. If you've been there before, then it's always better a second or third or fourth time!

When you finish

Great work, a ticket to fly can really bring you up when you're feeling down. Believe it or not you were walking on air!

Here's something to give your arms a little lift.
