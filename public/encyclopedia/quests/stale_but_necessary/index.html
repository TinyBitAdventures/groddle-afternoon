Being Watched

Quest

Rebuilt

How to get this quest

  • Offered when something happens in the game (item use).
  • Needs Legalize It done first.
  • Legalize It, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Ride It Out

    • Cup of Water

  2. Peek Behind the Veil

    • Cup of Water

Where to go

  • This quest takes you to a place of its own: take it, and the game sends you there.
  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

750 iMG, 100 mood, 100 energy

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

You drank water from a questionable source and you don't look well. You need to ride it out.
