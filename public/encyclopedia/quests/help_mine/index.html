I Me Mine

Quest

Adapted for solo

How to get this quest

  • Offered after you finish Dullite, Beryl and Sparkly.
  • Dullite, Beryl and Sparkly, finish it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Help players to mine

    • ×3 Pick

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

175 iMG, 125 currants, 50 energy, 15 favor with Friendly

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Pick

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Help three people to mine rocks.

When it is offered

Jars.

Do you ever have trouble opening stuck jars?

Well, you know how sometimes you just can’t get a jar open no matter how long you work at it, but if you pass it to a friend, they get it open right away?

Kid, mining is like a stuck jar.

Mining, as in with rocks. Sometimes mining is tough on your own, but if you have a friend helping you, it gets way easier… and more fun, like squash.

No, I mean with the rackets and—you know what? Just forget the analogies for a minute. Find someone who’s working on a rock and help them mine by mining at the same time. Do it three times. Can you manage that? I promise you’ll find selflessness deeply fulfilling on a spiritual level.

When you finish

Nice work, kid.

Do you feel more helpful? Do you feel like a more fulfilled and productive member of society?

Well, mining is a lot of work. Why don’t you take a quick break?

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

You can do this quest in the remake, reworked so you can do it on your own.
