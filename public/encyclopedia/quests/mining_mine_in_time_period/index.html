Giv'r 'til you Shiver

Quest · timed

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Offered when you learn Mining II.
  • Mining II, learn it

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Mine 15 times in just 5 minutes.

    • ×15 Pick

  2. All of it within 5 min: a countdown runs from the moment you take the quest, and the quest fails if it runs out.

Where to go

  • Hand it in to your Magic Rock, wherever you are.

Rewards

225 iMG, 150 currants, 100 energy, 20 favor with Zille

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

  • Pick
  • Fancy Pick

Pick up your Pick or Fancy Pick and mine 15 times in just 5 minutes.

That was some zippy Mining. I could barely see those little arms of yours move.

Aw. Use this little reward to buy them something pretty.
