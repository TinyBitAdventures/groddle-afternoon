Remember the Rememberable

Quest

In the remake

How to get this quest

  • Needs Notice the Unnoticeable done first.
  • You need to have earned Wanderer.
  • You need to be over level 40.
  • Notice the Unnoticeable, finish it first
  • Wanderer, earn it first

Steps

Each step in Tiny Speck’s words, with what it needs.

  1. Find the Phantom Glitch's new haunts.

    • ×13 Faded Heart

Where to go

  • Nothing to hand in: when the last step is done, your Magic Rock comes to you with the end of the quest, wherever you are.

Rewards

2,400 iMG, 200 mood, 200 energy

Linked to this quest

Things it involves

In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Dust off your Faded Heart and track down the Phantom Glitch's new haunts.
