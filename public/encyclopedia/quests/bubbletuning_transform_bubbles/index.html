In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Harvest 28 Plain Bubbles and change them into 7 Tiny Bubbles and 7 Blue Bubbles. You'll need a Bubble Tuner, natch. You can buy a bubble tuner from a Street Spirit who sells Produce or Hardware.

When it is offered

So you want to get into the Bubble Tuning game, huh, kid?

It's a from-the-ground-up operation. So let's start at the beginning.

Harvest 28 Plain Bubbles and get yourself a Bubble Tuner. Use it to create 7 Tiny Bubbles and 7 Blue Bubbles. It's that easy. Ready?

When you finish

Let me see those bubbles. Hmmm... nice curvature... good surface tension... decent viscosity. Not bad for a beginner.
