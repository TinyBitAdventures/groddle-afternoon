In Tiny Speck’s words

The quest

Meditate simultaneously with 10 friends using your Focusing Orb.

When it is offered

As Cosma once muttered to herself, "Achieving oneness with the universe sometimes requires eleven-ness."

Yes, the magic number at which meditation achieves its full potential!

It will please Cosma greatly if you can multiply your meditation by gathering 10 fellow Glitchen and meditating together. It's very important that you all meditate at the same time!

You'll each need your own Focusing Orb, and a nice quiet place to gather and free your minds to ponder the meaning of life, the universe, and everything.

When you finish

You feel a deep sense of peace and eleven-ness.

You have made Cosma a very happy giant — if a giant could be said to feel an emotion as simplistic and corporeal as 'happy'. In return for the great contentment you have brought her, she offers you all the following reward.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.
