Sno Cone Vendor

Store, kept by its vendor

In the remake

About this store

An automatic, systematic, hydromatic snowcone vending machine, for the exclusive purchase of snowcones. An acquired taste (in that you have to acquire them before you taste them), some of the more expensive snocones are only suitable for a refined palate - you have to be a certain level to eat them.

Buy Sno Cones. Or sell anything (via "sell" tab)
