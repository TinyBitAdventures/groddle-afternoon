Butterfly

Animal

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

A morphously blue butterfly. As any fool knows, butter can't fly, but milk apparently can. Butterflies may need a little kindness (or massage) before they give up the milk, but milk can always be turned into butter… and cheese… and who knows what else?
