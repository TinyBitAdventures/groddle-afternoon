In Tiny Speck’s game

Sewn from scraps of imagination and brought to life with a breath of happy servitude, your personally customized Butler or Butlerina enjoys buttling on your Home Street and can help you with many things. These include: mail, taking messages and packages, handing out gifts, and chatting with you.
