Shrine to Humbaba

Giants’ shrines

In the remake The Giant’s icon

In Tiny Speck’s game

This is a shrine to Humbaba, the giant who rules both two-legged and four-legged beasts. Actually, she rules all the beasts with even-numbered quantities of legs. The odd-numbered ones are on their own.
