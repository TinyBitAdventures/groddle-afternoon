Mailbox Zero is nirvana for some, this is one better than that: Mailbox (1) is one mailbox, made to house your very own mail. Epistles, deliveries, snapshots or gifts, whatever you've been sent from another can be found lurking in your mailbox. If you're too busy to access your mailbox, your Butler can do it for you. After all, what else are butlers for?
