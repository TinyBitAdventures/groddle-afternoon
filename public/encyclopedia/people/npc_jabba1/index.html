Uncle Friendly

Vendors and stores

In the remake Shown as its Cubimal

In Tiny Speck’s game

An old-school corner store with a small stock of groceries and fresh (-ish) produce. It's a handy little place to shop, if you don't mind that Uncle Friendly isn't friendly (especially if you ask him about his centuries-old tempestuous relationship with Helga), or that everything you buy to eat there looks like the best-before date has been amended with a crayon.
