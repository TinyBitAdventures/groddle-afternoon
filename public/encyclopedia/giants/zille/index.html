Zille

Giant of Mountains

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Zille is the giant with dominion over the mountains. All rocks, caverns, hillocks, pingos, buttes and drumlins thank Zille for their existence. Zille, busy imagining up new flavours of gravel, acknowledges their thanks.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

Skills Zille favors

Favor spent while you learn a skill buys study time: 10 seconds a point with the skill’s main Giant, 7 with a Giant who also favors it and 5 with any other.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Zille, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Zille, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Zille, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Zille

Zounds! By unlocking the First Emblem of Zille, you've earned the privilege of learning Mining III, the penultimate Mining Skill. Unrelated: don't you just love the word penultimate?
