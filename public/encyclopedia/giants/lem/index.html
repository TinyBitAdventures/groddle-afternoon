Lem

Giant of Exploration

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Lem, the wanderer giant. Responsible for travel, directions, and knowledge. What Lem doesn’t know is not worth knowing. Also, what Lem doesn’t unknow is not worth unknowing. (That’s Lem’s favourite joke) (Lem doesn’t know many jokes).

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

With your first Emblem

The depth of your obsequiousness is matched only by the depth of your pockets. For accumulating 1000 favor points, you are now entitled to receive one shiny Emblem of Lem.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Lem, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Lem, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Lem, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Lem

This mighty unlocking opens the doors to Teleportation III. Should you choose to learn this skill, you will be able to teleport with less energy, save one more teleportation point, and be just one step away from Teleportation IV where things really get interesting.

Achievements
