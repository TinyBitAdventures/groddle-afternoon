Pot

Giant of Prosperity

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Round of belly and capacious of stomach Pot is the Giant of Prosperity, with dominion over anything edible, cookable, munchable or nibbleworthy. Pot himself is not munchable. Do not attempt to munch any giants.

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The portrait is Tiny Speck’s own painting of Pot.

Skills Pot favors

Favor spent while you learn a skill buys study time: 10 seconds a point with the skill’s main Giant, 7 with a Giant who also favors it and 5 with any other.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Pot, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Pot, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Pot, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Pot

You've unharnessed the savory favor of Pot, who confers upon you the privilege of learning Master Chef II. Is this a nummier version of Master Chef I, you wonder? Dingdingding! You are correct.
