Spriggan

Giant of Trees

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Before Spriggan, no giant had ever imagined a tree. After Spriggan, no giant ever needed to, because he had already imagined them all. Steadfast, persistent, and somewhat rigid, Spriggan is the slumbering Giant of all Trees and Plants. Or “Trants”.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

Your humble offerings pile around my toes like so many tickly pebbles. I am duly tickled. In exchange for your 1000 favor points, I grant you this Emblem of Spriggan.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Spriggan, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Spriggan, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Spriggan, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Spriggan

By the power of Spriggan's Watering Can, with this unlocking the verdant inner sanctum of Arborology III now beckons you with its, um, verdancy.

Achievements

  • First Emblem of Spriggan

In the remake

In the remake: shrines to Spriggan take donations, give favor and Emblems, and favor spent with Spriggan speeds up learning a skill.
