Alph

Giant of Creation

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Alph is the giant responsible for creating “things”. And also “stuff”. Approaching everything with the question “What IF?”, Alph is never happier than when the answer results in a complex wadjamacallit or a satisfyingly big boom.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Alph, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Alph, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Alph, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Alph

Whoa... do you feel it? Tingling fingers? Itchy palms? Greasy elbows? No, it's not a medical emergency. It's just the power of Alph conferring upon you the ability to learn Tinkering IV.
