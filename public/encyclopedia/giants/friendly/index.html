Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Friendly, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Friendly, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Friendly, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Friendly

The hallowed secrets of Cocktail Crafting II are shared only with the select few who unlock the First Emblem of Friendly. Should you choose to acquire this skill, you will learn to make drinks of mind-boggling fanciness and fancy-boggling mindfulness.

Achievements

  • First Emblem of Friendly
  • Spent First Emblem of Friendly

In the remake

In the remake: shrines to Friendly take donations, give favor and Emblems, and favor spent with Friendly speeds up learning a skill.
