Grendaline

Giant of Water

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Grendaline, raised in a swamp, has an imagination as free-flowing as water. This makes sense, as water is what she spends most of her time imagining. If it sprinkles, drips, flows or gushes, Grendaline is your giant.

From Tiny Speck’s game code.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Grendaline, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Grendaline, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Grendaline, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Grendaline

Look at your thumb. Could it be greener? The answer is yes. With this unlocking comes access to Light Green Thumb III. It's like Light Green Thumb II, but better.

Achievements

  • First Emblem of Grendaline
  • Spent First Emblem of Grendaline

In the remake

In the remake: shrines to Grendaline take donations, give favor and Emblems, and favor spent with Grendaline speeds up learning a skill.
