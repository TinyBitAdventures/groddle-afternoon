Cosma

Giant of Sky & Meditation

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

As flighty and aimless as anything can be (if that thing is also giant-sized), Cosma is the Giant in charge of imagining up all things airborne. From the heaviest gas to cling to a cavern floor to the tiniest fart to escape from a butterfly, Cosma is the source of it all.

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The portrait is Tiny Speck’s own painting of Cosma.

Giant of
Sky & Meditation
Followers
Cosmapolitans
Personality
Levitous, Aimless

With your first Emblem

Humble Glitchling, your petitions have pleased me greatly. Or at least they have not displeased me greatly. Either way, as a reward for obtaining 1000 favor points, I confer upon you the noble Emblem of Cosma.

Skills Cosma favors

Favor spent while you learn a skill buys study time: 10 seconds a point with the skill’s main Giant, 7 with a Giant who also favors it and 5 with any other.

Favor, Emblems and Icons

Donating at a shrine earns favor with its Giant. At 1,000 favor the shrine gives you an Emblem of Cosma, and each Emblem after that takes 100 favor more than the one before. 11 Emblems of one Giant make an Icon.

  • Emblem of Cosma, for 1,000 favor
  • Icon of Cosma, 11 Emblems

Spending your first Emblem: Spent First Emblem of Cosma

Feeling serene? Now you can get serener! With the unlocking of this emblem, you can acquire Meditative Arts III, which, to be frank, makes Meditative Arts I and II look kind of sick.

In the remake: shrines to Cosma take donations, give favor and Emblems, and favor spent with Cosma speeds up learning a skill.
