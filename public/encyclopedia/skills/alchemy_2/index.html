Alchemy II

Alchemy skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 h, Alchemy

With Alchemy II, you can harness your love of shiny things and use Alchemical Tongs to transform everyday Plain Metal into fancy metals such as Copper, Tin, and Molybdenum.

When you learn it

Now that you've learned Alchemy II, it's time to get serious. And there's nothing more serious than a pair of Alchemical Tongs. Not only do they look super hardcore, they also let you use elements to convert Plain Metal to fancy metals such as Copper, Tin and Molybdenum. So... Alchemical Tongs. Get some!

Giants

Tii (primary), Alph, Lem

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Alchemy I, learn it first
  • Smelting, learn it first
  • Helter Smelter, finish it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Metalworking

Quests

  • Rub Eleven Out, offered when you learn it

Things you can make with it

  • Tin Ingot, you can make it
  • Copper Ingot, you can make it
  • Molybdenum Ingot, you can make it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
