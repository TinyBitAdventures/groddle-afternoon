Bog Specialization

Gathering skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 12 min, Level 2 or more, Gathering

Peat is sweet, and the deeper you dive, the more you get. You can certainly dig for peat with a shovel alone, but become a bog specialist, and your peat excavation is much more efficient and rewarding.

When you learn it

You leapt wholeheartedly into the world of bog specialization, and now each dig will be a little deeper. To the tune of one or two extra blocks of peat. SWEET!

Giants

Grendaline (primary), Mab, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Soil Appreciation III, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Fuelmaking

Quests

  • Peter out Peat, offered when you learn it
