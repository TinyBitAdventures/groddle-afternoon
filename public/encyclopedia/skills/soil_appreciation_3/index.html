Soil Appreciation III

Growing skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 12 min, Growing

Soil Appreciation III gives you a mood boost when tending, wears the Hoe only half as much with each use, possible bonus items each time you tend a Patch, and even more chance of getting Loam from a Patch or a Dirt Pile.

When you learn it

Good. With Soil Appreciation III, you can now look forward to the tending of Patches as opportunities for little mood boosts. Wicked, no? It should also be mentioned that your Hoe will last longer AND you've increased your chances of coming across bonuses and Loam more often. So go nuts! Soil yourself silly.

Giants

Mab (primary), Spriggan, Zille

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Soil Appreciation II, learn it first
  • Can You Dig It?, finish it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Bog Specialization
  • Herbalism I
  • Nudgery
  • Soil Appreciation IV

Quests

  • Many Shovels Make Something-Something Loam, offered when you learn it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
