Arborology IV

Gathering skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 h 36 min, Gathering

Arborology IV is notable for increasing your chance of a bonus gift during harvesting, and increasing the imagination you get every time you do harvest. Which all serves to increase your reason for learning it.

When you learn it

Almost at the top of the muddy slide of arborology greatness, harvesting trees now grants double the imagination, and considerably more chance of getting a bonus reward. It's good to be green fingered.

Giants

Spriggan (primary), Grendaline, Mab

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Arborology III, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Arborology V

Quests

  • Bean There, Done That, offered when you learn it
