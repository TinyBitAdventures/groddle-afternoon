Fox Brushing

Animals skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 12 min, Level 4 or more, Animals

A fiber-hunter with the skill of Fox Brushing can expect to rub foxes up the right way (rather than the wrong one), reaping a handful more fibers with every brush.

When you learn it

Your newfound knowledge of the complex workings of the Brush (which end is the handle, etc) will enable you to pull more precious fibers from the bushy bits of a fox with each grooming.

Giants

Humbaba (primary), Friendly, Lem

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Animal Kinship II, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Fiber Arts I

Creatures and people it changes

  • Fox
