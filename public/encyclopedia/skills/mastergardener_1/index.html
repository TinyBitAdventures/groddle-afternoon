Master Gardener

Growing skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 3 h 36 min, Level 15 or more, Growing

Truly blessed is the gardener who masters Garden Mastery to become a true Garden Master, for they gain access to the five mystical potions of horticultural power. None but the Master Gardener can pour the potions that allow you to water, plant, fertilize, harvest and clear a whole garden at once.

When you learn it

Hush, oh hallowed Master Gardener, and you may hear the wind through the fronds of ripened crops seem to whisper your name as you pass. Why? Because you, master of all gardens (master in its most non-gender-specific sense) can use the five potions of gardening power. And that is the most awesome thing crops can think of.

Giants

Mab (primary), Grendaline, Tii

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Croppery III, learn it first
  • Herbalism III, learn it first

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
