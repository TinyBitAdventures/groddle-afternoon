Transcendental Radiation I

Intellectual skill

In multiplayer

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 48 min, Intellectual

The noblest among us act for the benefit of us. Or, it is good to do nice things for others? Whatever: if you learn Transcendental Radiation you will have mystical knowledge of a special form of Meditation which directs the benefits outwards, to those around you, rather than keeping them all to yourself.

When you learn it

Now that you've achieved Transcendental Radiation, you are an egoless particle of the universal no-soul. Why not share the non-wealth with others by using your Focusing Orb to radiate transcendence to other players?

Giants

Cosma (primary), Friendly, Lem

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

How long it takes

How long one go takes at each rank, and the energy and tool wear it costs. The numbers are Tiny Speck’s game data, which the remake reads too.

What it needs

  • Meditative Arts II, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Transcendental Radiation II

Quests

  • Sharing the Oneness, offered when you learn it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake; what it does needs other players, so it works in multiplayer.
