EZ Cooking II

Cooking skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 6 min, Cooking

The second level of EZ Cooking grants several new recipes with the Knife & Board and makes one's chopping, slicing, mincing and dicing more efficient.

When you learn it

Welcome to EZ Cooking II! Faster than a kitchen ninja, now you can use your Knife & Board to slice and dice with the best of them. Try your new recipes. Love your new recipes. Live your new recipes.

Giants

Pot (primary), Grendaline, Humbaba

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • EZ Cooking I, learn it first
  • Able Chopper, earn it first

What it opens

Quests

  • Sleepytime Feast, offered when you learn it

Things you can make with it

  • Simple Slaw, you can make it
  • Juicy Carpaccio, you can make it
  • Hearty Groddle Sammich, you can make it
  • Fruit Salad, you can make it
  • Common Crudites, you can make it
  • Potato Patty, you can make it
  • Spinach Salad, you can make it
  • Exotic Fruit Salad, you can make it
  • Desssert Rub, you can make it
  • Naraka Flame Rub, you can make it
  • Trump Rub, you can make it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
