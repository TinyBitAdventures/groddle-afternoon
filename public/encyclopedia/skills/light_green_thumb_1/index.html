Light Green Thumb I

Growing skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 min, Growing

This is the first step towards a better relationship with plant life of all kinds. It ensures success when watering and petting trees and plants, halves the time required to water and gives some modest imagination rewards.

When you learn it

With Light Green Thumb, you've just taken a step into a bigger world. A world where you grok plants, and they grok you right back. Armed with your trusty Watering Can, you will now enjoy some modest rewards now that you know how to water and pet the veg properly, and at twice the speed to boot.

Giants

Grendaline (primary), Mab, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

Nothing: anyone can learn it.

What it opens

Skills

  • Croppery I
  • Arborology I
  • Light Green Thumb II

Quests

  • Give some H2O love, offered when you learn it
  • Lift Your Light Green Thumb, offered when you learn it

Creatures and people it changes

  • Magic Rock

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
