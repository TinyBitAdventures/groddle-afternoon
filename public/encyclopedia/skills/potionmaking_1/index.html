Potionmaking I

Alchemy skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 h 12 min, Alchemy

In conjunction with a Cauldron, Potionmaking I opens the door to a world of bubbling unctions, shimmering fumes and acrid aromas. With this introduction to beginners wizardry, a number of potions become boilable.

When you learn it

The cork of unknowing has been removed, and the power of potions poured directly into your brain. Now get yourself a Cauldron, and you can bottle fame, brew glory and even put a stopper in death… Well, not quite. You can, however, make potions and stuff.

Giants

Tii (primary), Mab, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Tincturing, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Potionmaking II

Quests

  • Potion Detector, offered when you learn it

Things you can make with it

  • Tree Poison Antidote, you can make it
  • Charades Potion, you can make it
  • Amorous Philtre, you can make it
  • Rainbow Juice, you can make it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
