Cheffery III

Cooking skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 6 min, Cooking

The ultimate in Frying Pan friendship - BFFs, if you will - the third level of Cheffery bestows some very advanced recipes indeed.

When you learn it

With Cheffery III, you are now officially cooking with gas. Grease up your Frying Pan and try your hand at recipes that would make a brain surgeon rocket scientist throw in the towel. (What do those guys know about cooking, anyway?)

Giants

Pot (primary), Humbaba, Zille

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Cheffery II, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Master Chef I

Quests

  • Down Home Cookin', offered when you learn it

  • Corn-off-the-Cob, you can make it
  • Banana No-Names, you can make it
  • Applejack, you can make it
  • Bubble and Squeak, you can make it
  • Pumpkin Pie, you can make it
  • Salmon Jaella, you can make it
  • Kind BreakfURst Burrito (TM), you can make it
