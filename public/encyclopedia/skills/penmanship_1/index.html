Penpersonship

Intellectual skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 18 min, Level 7 or more, Intellectual

The pen is mightier than the sword. This is good, because we don't have any swords. With Penpersonship, you can use a Quill and Paper to write down all your important thoughts and feelings.

When you learn it

Congratulations! Now that you've learned Penpersonship, you can write down all your thoughts, feelings, and bad poetry and share them with your friends, no matter how unwilling they might be to read them.

Giants

Lem (primary), Friendly, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

Nothing: anyone can learn it.

What it opens

Quests

  • Homelier Sweet Home, offered when you learn it
