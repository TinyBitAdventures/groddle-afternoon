Saucery I

Cooking skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 12 minCooking

The most astute gourmands all know the secret to the best-prepared dishes is always in the sauce. Learn Saucery, obtain a Saucepan and you find you already know how to make several things – and even more recipes ideas will come to you as you use it. Plus, Saucery is a requirement if you ever want to become a Master Chef.

When you learn it

Well played, young saucier. With Saucery I, you have leapt upon a log on the river of sauce-learning. Trusty Saucepan in hand, you will discover exciting new recipes that will make tastebuds everywhere toss their underpants at you. Figuratively speaking.
