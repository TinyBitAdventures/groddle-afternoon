Potionmaking II

Alchemy skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 4 h 48 min, Alchemy

Far above the level of basic potions, more advanced potions exist. And the more complex the potion, the more startling the effect. And what is the secret ingredient in making these advanced potions? Yes: Potion-making II. Which is more a 'skill' than an ingredient, but the point remains the same, metaphorically speaking.

When you learn it

It may be the smell of acrid potion smoke that clings to your clothes, or the thick potion residue that drips from your hair after a particularly heavy session over a hot Cauldron, but crowds sometimes now part as you walk through a room. You like to think it is because they are in awe of you. Yes, that's it. It's the awe. Not that other stuff.

Giants

Tii (primary), Mab, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.
