Jellisac Hands

Gathering skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 12 min, Gathering

Jellisac scooping requires a special kind of touch. Learn Jellisac Hands and you'll be scooping those slippery suckers faster, and with less effort. You may even get more jellisacs per scoop!

When you learn it

Gone are the days of unskillfully scooping jellisacs. Now that you've got Jellisac Hands you're able to scoop those sacs like a pro! Scoop with ease, and toil away no longer.

Giants

Grendaline (primary), Mab, Zille

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Soil Appreciation II, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Fuelmaking

Quests

  • A Slatheration of Jellisacs, offered when you learn it
