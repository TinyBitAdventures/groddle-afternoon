Alchemy I

Alchemy skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 9 min, Alchemy

Ah, the transformational power of transformation. Alchemy puts that Element Handling to good use and teaches you how to use a Test Tube to craft loose elements into marginally more valuable compounds. Further down this path, compounds can become powders and soon enough you are making potions and recycling even the most complex items down to their components.

When you learn it

Well, you done learned that Alchemy good, but the question is: what kind of alchemical shenanigans can you get up to now? For starters, get yourself a Test Tube from the Tool Vendor. With it, you can experiment with combining various elements and seeing what kinds of compounds you can come up with. Eventually you'll be able to compound the compounds, but let's not get ahead of ourselves.
