Meditative Arts III

Intellectual skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 h 36 min, Intellectual

Meditative Arts III doubles the amount of time you can stay in the zone yet again, gives more iMG reward each time you meditate, and doubles the amount of energy and mood you get for every second spent meditating.

When you learn it

Om my goodness, you've just completed Meditative Arts III, the highest level of Meditative Arts. In the zone? Friend, you'll be the in-the-zoniest. If that's a phrase. Whatever: it is now.

Giants

Cosma (primary), Friendly, Lem

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Meditative Arts II, learn it first
  • Spent First Emblem of Cosma, earn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Levitation
  • Piety

Quests

  • Relax to the Max, offered when you learn it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
