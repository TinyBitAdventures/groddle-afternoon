Mining III

Industrial skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 2 h 24 minIndustrial

Mining III reduces the time and energy it takes to mine, reduces wear on your preferred mining instrument, and rewards more imagination.

When you learn it

You take 16 tons and what do you get? Most would get a little bit older and deeper in debt, but not you. With Mining III, you're like some sort of automatic mining-majig, what with the way you now can cut through rock and put less wear on your pick.
