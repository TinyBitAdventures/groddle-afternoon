Blending I

Cooking skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 3 min, Cooking

The first step on a successful career in drink making is understanding the basic operation of a Blender. The first level of Blending allows some basic recipes and the possibility of learning more through use.

When you learn it

Rejoice, for the tantalizing craft of Blendering is now yours. A Blender is your key to a new dimension of flavor. Get one, practice your beginner recipes, and lo! Your mixological odyssey begins.

Giants

Friendly (primary), Grendaline, Pot

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.
