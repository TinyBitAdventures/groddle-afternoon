Light Green Thumb III

Growing skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 36 min, Growing

A plant or tree giving you its fruit without even harvesting?! That's possible (though rare) when you have Light Green Thumb III, but you can count on a doubled imagination reward for watering and a nice mood boost.

When you learn it

A plant or tree giving you its fruit without you even having to harvest it? Unpossible, you say? Okay, yes, it's mostly unpossible. But it does happen from time to time. More importantly, though, you now get twice the rewards for watering, plus a nice little kick in the old mood box. Which is a good thing.

Giants

Grendaline (primary), Mab, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Light Green Thumb II, learn it first
  • Spent First Emblem of Grendaline, earn it first

What it opens

Quests

  • Water Water Everywhere, offered when you learn it

Things you can make with it

  • Irrigator 9000, you can make it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
