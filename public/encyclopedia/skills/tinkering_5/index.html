Tinkering V

Industrial skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 7 h 12 min, Industrial

Learn Tinkering V, and consider yourself a mastertink. Repair speed is nigh instantaneous and Tinkertool operation barely taxes your energy, but that's just the beginning. With this AND Engineering, you can practically create any tool imaginable. As long as your imagination is restricted to tools that already exist, of course.

When you learn it

Remember how draining tinkering used to be? With Tinkering V, it's practically refreshing! You know what? You should go fix some other players' tools. Maybe make them some fancy tools, too? That would be a nice thing to do, wouldn't it? Yes. Yes it would.

Giants

Alph (primary), Friendly, Tii

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.
