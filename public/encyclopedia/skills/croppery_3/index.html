Croppery III

Growing skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 h 12 min, Growing

Croppery III makes you a veritable master of the garden, giving you more mood and imagination for tending your crops through their lifecycle, and yielding a higher chance of surprise bonuses (or bonus surprises). Above all of that, every so often you will have a 'super harvest', which yields double the amount of crops from a plot.

When you learn it

Croppery III bestows upon you thrice the awesomeness of basic Croppery. Watch your toes while harvesting. Those are the most likely times you'll find a super fun happy bonus gift, or a 'super harvest', which yields twice the amount of crops from a plot. Just the land giving a little something back.

Giants

Mab (primary), Grendaline, Humbaba

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Croppery II, learn it first
  • A Beltful of Onions, finish it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Master Gardener

Quests

  • Crops a-Planty, offered when you learn it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
