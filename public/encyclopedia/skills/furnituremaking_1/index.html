Furnituremaking I

Industrial skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 1 h 12 min, Level 7 or more, Industrial

The first step toward a more comfortable home. Teaching the correct end of a Construction Tool and just where a Snail should be hit (or turned, depending), Furnituremaking I is essential for the proud homeowner and requirer of basic furniture.

When you learn it

Now you know which end of a side table is up (the square, flat end) and how tightly to turn your snail, the world of basic furniture construction is suddenly laid before you. Go frolic in it.

Giants

Alph (primary), Friendly, Spriggan

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.
