Meditative Arts II

Intellectual skill

In the remake

In Tiny Speck’s game

Learn time 24 min, Intellectual

A direct step up from the first level, Meditative Arts II doubles the length of time you can stay in the zone, and at the same time bumps up the Imagination rewards from meditation.

When you learn it

With Meditative Arts II, you are your own best guru. If you don't have one already, add a Focusing Orb to your spiritual arsenal and get meditating. All that bliss isn't going to find itself, you know.

Giants

Cosma (primary), Friendly, Lem

From Tiny Speck’s game code. The learn time is how long it takes in the remake, before Better Learning.

What it needs

  • Meditative Arts I, learn it first

What it opens

Skills

  • Meditative Arts III
  • Transcendental Radiation I

Quests

  • Cold Comfort, offered when you learn it

In the remake

You can learn this skill in the remake.
