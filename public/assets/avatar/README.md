# Avatar parts

The part art here is Tiny Speck's, converted from their avatar SWFs, and is CC0.

The 19 articles marked `reimagined: true` in `catalog.json` were greyscale masters in Tiny Speck's files. Their colourways are borrowed from sibling Tiny Speck articles (CC0); the pairing of colourway to article is our choice, and that choice is also dedicated to the public domain under CC0. Use them freely.
