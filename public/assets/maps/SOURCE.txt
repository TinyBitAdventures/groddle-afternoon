Hub map geometry + art preservation
===================================

  Source: Tiny Speck GSJS CC0 — inc_data_maps_prod.js
  assets/maps/index.json           — 94 hubs catalog
  assets/maps/hubs/hub-<id>.json   — streets + GO exits (760×460)

Art preserved offline
  assets/maps/art/thumbs/sm_*.jpg  — 48 hub thumbs (150×91) from world_map.fla
  assets/maps/art/world/*.jpg      — continent world-map bitmaps from same FLA
  thumbLocal on ~57 hub JSON files when a matching thumb exists

Region backgrounds from the thumbs (G471, round 5, 2026-10-31)
  assets/maps/art/bg/<region>.webp (760×460) and <region>@2x.webp (1520×920): each public
  region's thumb from world_map.fla, scaled to the frame and softened into a painting
  (tools/map-backgrounds/build.mjs, provenance in art/bg/backgrounds.json). The thumbs line up
  with the 2012 encyclopedia maps at scale 1 (reference only). js/map-graph.js draws one behind
  the streets when no full painting (bgLocal) exists; test, tower and POL hubs keep the plain map.

Repainted region maps (G522, 2026-11-05)
  A region listed in tools/provenance/repainted-maps.json carries a new painting made for the
  remake from its own thumbnail, in place of the enlarged thumb, under the same two file names.
  tools/map-backgrounds/build.mjs keeps those files as they are; tests/map-backgrounds.test.js
  checks their sizes, hashes and provenance. The next region is one entry in that file.

  Groddle Meadow (hub 58): art/bg/groddlemeadow.webp (760x460) and groddlemeadow@2x.webp (1520x920)

Streets left off the map (round 5, 2026-10-31)
  invisible_to_outsiders on a hub street: the Start streets, apartment towers and basements,
  machine rooms and Muufo's forgotten floor, which the client drew only for a player standing
  on them (MapHub.as drawStreet). Written by tools/build-map-hidden.mjs from GSJS

  region maps (reference only). The region list offers GSJS public_hubs only (js/map-graph.js).

World map (G471, 2026-11-13)
  Tiny Speck's CC0 glitch-overlays/world_map.fla, the source of the client's world_map overlay
  (GSJS inc_data_overlays.js, 2012-11-27): tools/map-views/build.mjs writes
  assets/maps/art/world/world_map_locked.jpg   the sketched chart (the FLA's JPEG as is)
  assets/maps/art/world/world_map_unlocked.jpg the painted chart (already here; byte for byte the FLA's)
  assets/maps/art/world/frames.svg             the region frames and locked tiles (the FLA's vectors)
  assets/maps/art/world/cards/<hub>.svg        each region's hover card (frame, thumbnail, lettered name)
  js/map-views-data.js                         the 47 regions' click frames, centres and card placements
  The subway map's paper and line overlays (subway_map_bg_59215.jpg, subway_*_line_fg_59215.png) are
  lost; js/map-views.js draws the line from config_prod.js map_pos, and the paper is recreated (below).

Subway map paper (G471, 2026-11-13)
  assets/maps/art/subway/subway-map-paper.webp (760x460) and subway-map-paper@2x.webp (1520x920):
  the paper the Subway System map is drawn on, twelve cream sheets on pale sage where the 2012 window
  had them, in place of the lost subway_map_bg_59215.jpg (GSJS config_prod.js 668). js/map-views.js
  renderSubwayMapSvg draws it under the line; tests/map-views.test.js checks sizes, hashes, provenance.
    Licence      AI-generated, dedicated CC0 1.0 (tools/builder/licence-policy.json)
    Model        GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst, in T3 Chat canvas, 2026-11-13 (16:9, High 2K, 1 image),
                 made by Grok Bot for Austin Ginder, who directed it
    Prompt       in full in tools/provenance/subway-map-paper.json
    Inputs       1. where the twelve cream sheets go (our own drawing; positions measured from a
                    November 2012 picture of the window, positions only)
                 2. that drawing with the game's own line, stations and names over it (our own;
                    stations from config_prod.js map_pos)
                 3. the Subway Map sign (Tiny Speck, CC0, glitch-items misc/subway_map)
                 4. the world map's two charts (Tiny Speck, CC0, glitch-overlays/world_map.fla)
                 5. the station signs (Tiny Speck, CC0, glitch-locations subway1.swf)
    After        the sheets measured against the layout and moved onto it (one x map, a piecewise
                 y map, bilinear at 2x, Lanczos down), then cwebp -q 88
    Reference    the Tribute Blog's capture of the original window (post 38013353380): looked at,
    only         measured for positions and described in the prompt's words; never given to the
                 model and not in this repo
